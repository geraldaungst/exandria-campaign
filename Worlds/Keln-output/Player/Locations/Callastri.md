> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Continent

## Description

Callastri is a continent divided by the wild [[Kelvantor Mountains]] and difficult seas. [[Varrinthal Kingdom|Varrinthal]] occupies the southeastern quarter, while [[The Ringhold]] holds the north and west. Smaller settlements, including [[Watcher's Rest]], dot the wilderness between.

## History of Callastri

Legend holds that [[The Ringhold]] was founded by five survivors of a lost coastal city, who found sanctuary in the mountains and built anew. [[Varrinthal Kingdom]]'s founding is less storied, but almost as old.

## Geography and Regions

[[Callastri]] is bounded by the [[Kelvantor Mountains]], which separate [[Varrinthal Kingdom]]'s territory in the southeast from [[The Ringhold]]'s lands in the north and west. Between the two lies wide, wild country. Forests, hills, and open land where few settlements exist and travel is best done on known roads and rivers, like the [[Skarnveil]].

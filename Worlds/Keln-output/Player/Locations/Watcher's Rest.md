## Description

A quarry town of roughly 1,000 people on the [[Skarnveil]], about 270 miles upriver from [[Varrinthal City|Varrinthal]]. Built around two industries: quarrying the exceptional "[[kneestone]]" from nearby [[Watcher's Knee]], and operating the only reliable ferry crossing for miles. Not built on a [[Deep]] — magic here follows standard rules. Named for a local legend about a giant who sat down to wait and slowly turned to stone. Historically an isolated, independent settlement, they joined Varrinthal's realm by choice rather than conquest.

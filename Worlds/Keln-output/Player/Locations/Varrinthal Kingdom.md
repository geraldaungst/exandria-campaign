## Description

**Varrinthal** is a hereditary kingdom occupying the southeastern quarter of [[Callastri]], ruled from its capital city of [[Varrinthal City|Varrinthal]]. Tradition holds that its founder, **Varrin**, was appointed by the [[Titans]] themselves to claim the land and its Deep, a claim the ruling house has upheld ever since.

Succession follows a distinctive pattern: the heir formally becomes the **[[Right of Varrin]],** Varrinthal's ruling monarch, upon the birth of their own first child, at which point their predecessor steps back into the role of **the [[Elderright]]**, a position of counsel and mentorship rather than public rule.

Where [[The Ringhold]] governs through election and consensus, Varrinthal's authority flows through bloodline and divine claim: two neighboring powers built on very different foundations.

## DM History

Titans were once-living beings capable of channeling the planet's core magic directly. They are dead now, their petrified remains scattered in multiple places worldwide ([[Watcher's Knee]] among them) — a fact almost no one currently knows. [[Varrinthal Kingdom|Varrinthal]]'s founding myth claims its first ruler, [[Varrin]], was personally appointed by the Titans to claim the land, giving the monarchy's "divine right" an unintentional grain of truth. Where Titans came from and why they died is an open question.

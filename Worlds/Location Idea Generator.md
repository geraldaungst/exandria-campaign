## Location Idea Generator

### Quick Location Creation

**When you need a location fast, combine:**

1. A type (from below)
2. One memorable detail (sensory, unusual feature, or current state)
3. Done.

### Location Types by Setting

#### Urban/Settlement

- Inn/tavern • Temple/shrine • Market square • Noble estate • Guard post
- Craftsman workshop • Underground cistern • City walls • Graveyard • Abandoned warehouse
- Sewer entrance • Rooftop garden • Back alley • Courthouse • Bathhouse

#### Wilderness

- Ancient tree grove • Rocky outcrop • Hidden cave • River crossing • Abandoned campsite
- Overgrown ruins • Natural spring • Cliff edge • Dense thicket • Hollow log
- Stone circle • Wildflower meadow • Ravine • Waterfall • Animal den

#### Dungeon/Underground

- Flooded chamber • Collapsed tunnel • Shrine room • Prison cells • Treasure vault
- Torture chamber • Library/scriptorium • Throne room • Barracks • Kitchen/pantry
- Alchemist lab • Secret passage • Trapped hallway • Guard room • Stable/kennel

#### Unusual/Magical

- Planar rift • Floating island • Crystal cave • Petrified forest • Pocket dimension
- Time-frozen chamber • Mirror maze • Gravity-reversed room • Living walls • Dreamscape

### Memorable Details (Pick One)

#### Sensory

- **Smell**: Incense, decay, salt air, metallic, sulfur, flowers, damp earth, cooking food
- **Sound**: Dripping water, wind howling, distant chanting, mechanical grinding, birdsong, silence
- **Visual**: Glowing moss, shattered mirrors, blood stains, golden light, deep shadows, frost
- **Feel**: Oppressive heat, bone-chilling cold, humid and sticky, electrically charged

#### Unusual Features

- Contains a monument/statue of (roll on [[z_compendium/tables/monuments|monument table]])
- Ceiling is unusually high/low
- Everything is oversized/undersized
- Covered in specific vegetation
- Built from unusual material (bone, crystal, living wood)
- Has specific symbol carved everywhere
- Contains evidence of recent violence
- Shows signs of hasty abandonment

#### Current State

- Recently looted • Under construction • Partially collapsed • Overgrown with plants
- Flooded with water • Filled with fog/smoke • Extremely clean • Suspiciously pristine
- Covered in graffiti • Ransacked • Frozen solid • Burning

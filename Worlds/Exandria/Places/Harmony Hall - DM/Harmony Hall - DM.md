# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Harmony Hall]]

**Status:** Dinner theater with live performances, in the former brothel Coriander & Mallow Exchange Company. [[Annah]] runs it and [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] owns half. The party keeps three rooms here plus Grisul's, and two paladins guard the door.
**Significance:** Hesterian's old home. [[Hesterian Shyr's Infiltration]] and [[Qilynn Duskwhisper's Revenge]] both name it. Varnes offers a free security assessment ([[Rescue Ilya's Brother]]).

> [!info] Current residents and employees
> - [[Grisul Adder]]: former owner, deceased, former madam
> - [[Annah]]: ticket booth and performer; given the establishment after Grisul's death and offered Hesterian half ownership
> - [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]]: co-owner
> - Sargenta Barrelcrest: guard and bartender, hired by Drawg

# What's Happening Now

- Operates as a successful dinner theater with live performances.
- Offers training in culinary and performing arts.
- Supported by the Pearl Shrine (Temple of Avandra).
- The party met Annah at the ticket booth. She was surprised Hesterian was alive.
- They saw Grisul, who seemed happier and on the up-and-up. Something big happened after Hesterian's murder, but Annah would not talk about it.
- The party stayed for a dinner theater show. As far as Annah knows, nobody else recognized Hesterian.
- Hesterian went back to confront Grisul. Later Grisul died, and Annah was given the establishment ([[Party Notes]]).
- Drawg hired Sargenta Barrelcrest as guard and bartender, and two paladins stand guard outside ([[Session 28 - Back at Harmony Hall]]).

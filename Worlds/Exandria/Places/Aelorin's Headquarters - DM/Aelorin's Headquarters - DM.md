# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Aelorin's Headquarters]]

**Status:** The [[Clasp - DM|Clasp]]'s Port Damali base, beneath Evaldo's Elegant Attire. [[Aelorin Nightshade]] runs it. The party staked it out in [[Session 22 - Raid on Aelorin's Headquarters]] and followed [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM|Dreyara]] from it in [[Session 23 - Players Follow Dreyara and Return to Aelorin's]].
**Significance:** [[Qilynn Duskwhisper's Revenge]] waits on the party meeting Clasp operatives or returning to Port Damali. A sealed basement chamber holds a rift.

# Description

- Ground floor: [[Aelorin's HQ Ground Floor Text|main shop, workshop, fitting room and the hidden entrance to the basement]].
- Basement: [[Aelorin's HQ Basement Text|security corridor, guard post, storage hallway, agents' quarters, Aelorin's chamber, Calderax's chamber, emergency quarters, library, barracks, the sealed chamber, a hidden cache and the hidden exit to the surface]].
- The shop stands on a larger lot than most nearby, with cypress trees flanking the entrance.

# What's Happening Now

- Calderax met the party in Session 26 and agreed not to tell Aelorin ([[Calderax Dunhall]]).
- Aelorin wants the Clasp to expand into the Tumbledowns next.

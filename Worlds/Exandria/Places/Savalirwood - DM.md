# Quick Reference
> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Savalirwood]]

**Status:** Cursed forest across the Greying Wildlands, formerly the Veluthil Forest. No cure in about 250 years.
**Significance:** Destination of [[Cleanse the Savalirwood]] (upcoming) and the forest Seraphina left. It holds the Fey crossing toward [[Shard 8 - Feywild Shard|Shard 8]] and Popcorn's Feywild thread.

# Description
Sickly purple fog fills the forest. Plants grow twisted and aggressive, native creatures are warped into more dangerous forms, and cursed fey roam among them. The forest resists settlement and clearing. It surrounds the [[Ruins of Molaesmyr - DM|ruins of Molaesmyr]] and forms a natural barrier between Uthodurn and the rest of Wildemount.

# What's Happening Now
- No move is planned. The thread becomes active when the party heads toward the wood or Seraphina's patron raises it.
- The [[Malachite Cord - DM|Malachite Cord]] and Uthodurn's mages study the corruption.
- Harmonic rifts in the wood are growing larger and more frequent.

# Secrets
> [!secret]- DM Only
> - The corruption began in 585 PD and spread from the center of Molaesmyr after the [[Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing|Rupture]].
> - The rifts connect to the Feywild and sometimes other planes, and cursed creatures come through them.
> - Erma Schnieb, a halfling mage from Uthodurn, believes she has found a portal in the wood to the Lower Planes. She is only partly right: several rifts lead to several planes.
> - The Savalirwood Fey crossing reaches the Feywild region where Shard 8 lies. Popcorn's flute stirs near it and the whispers grow louder on arrival ([[Kelmen and Popcorn Connections]]).

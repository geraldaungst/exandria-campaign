# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Rexxentrum]]

**Status:** Capital of the Dwendalian Empire, ruled by King Dwendal. The party has not been here. They are heading toward it for [[Shard 5 - Rexxentrum Shard|Shard 5]], and [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] may come for her brother.
**Significance:** Shard 5 sits in a warded vault under the Cobalt Soul Archive. The [[Cerberus Assembly - DM|Cerberus Assembly]] keeps its research facility here, where [[Melthes]] was last seen. The Emissaries of the Sunfall run a cover shop here.

# Description

- Zemni Fields. More than twice the size of Emon. Most of the Cerberus Assembly lives here. Center of arcane learning in the Empire, with the Soltryce Academy.
- Districts named in the vault: Court of Colors ([[Scroll and Scribe]]) and Pearls' Rest (a secret townhouse, see Secrets). There is no map and no other district is set.
- The Amber Road runs south through [[Zadash - DM|Zadash]] to the Wuyun Gates.
- Prime Arbiter Sydnock Truscan, whose family holds Deastok, lives here (Player Wiki, Truscan Family).

# What's Happening Now

- [[Archivist Ovedo]] is studying the prophecy text on Shard 5 in secret. The Emissaries watch them from Scroll and Scribe.
- [[Azel Brightful]] has moved his shop here on the way to Xarzith Kitril, and is helping [[Kexira Suulveth]] catalogue Draconian materials for the Archive.
- [[Kite of the Wind]] is on his way to report to [[Valen Elderguard]] after the failed mission (about 20 days' travel).
- [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]]'s Rexxentrum job (the Archive heist) lost [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM|Dreyara]] before she arrived. Whether it goes ahead is open ([[Qalix's Network]]).
- [[Melthes]] may still be at the Assembly facility. It is open ([[Rescue Melthes]]).
- [[Eledyr Dephar]]'s whereabouts are open. His note says Rexxentrum; the player notes say Zadash.

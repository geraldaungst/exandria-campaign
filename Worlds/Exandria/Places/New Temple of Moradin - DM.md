# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[New Temple of Moradin]]

**Status:** Construction resumed after the party cleansed the crypt (Session 6) and the Ki'Nau agreed to the dwarves' work. In the northwest Crescents near the skyport. Barwynn and Thorlynn, dwarves at the temple, agreed to question the party's skull for 20 gp ([[Session 29-31 - Traveling to Cloudfang]]).
**Significance:** The Feywild rift in the crypt's collapsed floor is still open. The party holds the Journal of Solan Taku and the obsidian warhammer from the crypt.

# Description

**Exterior**
- Partially completed foundation of granite and basalt.
- Construction staging area with materials and equipment.
- Northwest section near the crypts remains unexcavated.
- Modest by dwarven standards but large by local measure.
- Planned design combines maritime and forge aesthetics: a central dome shaped like an anvil, buttresses resembling hammer handles, wave-like patterns in the stone facade, copper and brass accents that will weather green, stained glass windows depicting forge scenes, and a seaward-facing ceremonial forge.

**Interior**
- Foundation complete in most areas. Above-ground construction begun in several sections.
- Estimated completion: 1 year.

**Notable areas**
- Ancient crypts beneath the northwest foundation.
- Meditation alcove with three historical murals.
- Areas of shadow activity.
- Location of the Feywild rift in a collapsed section.

**Ki'Nau connection**
- Ancient burial site with a traditional meditation alcove.
- Historical murals depicting trial and execution scenes.
- Local elders led by [[Matiu]] oversee site protection.
- Traditional drowning ritual depicted in murals.

**Dwarven elements**
- Temple construction led by recent Tal'Dorei arrivals.
- Mixed workforce including dwarves, humans, firbolg, and elf.
- [[Durnvolk Durmir]] serves as construction advisor.
- Maram leads the pro-construction faction.

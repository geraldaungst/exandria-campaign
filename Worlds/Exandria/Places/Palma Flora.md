# Quick Reference

**Status:** Town at the southern tip of the Vezdali Peninsula on the Menagerie Coast, within reach of Port Damali. An earthquake has wrecked Flora Isle, the port half of the town. The [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM|Obsidian Echoforge]] keeps its headquarters on the outskirts, and the quake did not touch it. The party has not been here.
**Significance:** [[Lyren Willowwhisper]] leads the Echoforge from here and holds Shards 1 to 4. [[Aethor Kalisk's Workshop]] is in its research wing. The Emissaries of the Sunfall plan to watch it.

# Description

Town details come from published Wildemount material (see Sources). Check them against the books before the table relies on them. The first list is the town as published. The next section is what the earthquake left.

- About 1,780 people, mostly human, with halflings and dwarves. A popular vacation spot for merchants and Zhelezo from Port Damali.
- Flora Isle holds the port and a beach. A wooden bridge crosses a wide channel to the peninsula. The Riptide Inn and Tavern is where most visitors stay. Most residents live and work in the northern half and retire to the island.
- An elder handles town-wide matters and most residents run their own affairs. An untrained militia, mostly shark hunters, guards against pirates and sahuagin.
- Exports fish, fruit and alcohol, but most income is tourism. The Palma Flora Traders, founded by local farmers and brewers, bargain together with larger ports.
- Lush green foliage, an annual shark-hunting tournament, and a cocktail named after the town. The sahuagin of the Sharkfeather Abyss, a trench just west of the town, have long clashed with the hunters.

**After the earthquake**
- Flora Isle dropped into the Sharkfeather Abyss, and sahuagin attacked the survivors. The island's port, beach, docks and the Riptide Inn went with it, and so did the bridge that joined it to the peninsula.
- As published, the mainland half stood but was emptied by the attack. Survivors may settle in other Menagerie Coast towns and serve as contacts.
- The Echoforge facility, Aethor's workshop and the Willowwhisper residences were not affected.

**The Echoforge facility**
- A permanent collection and workshop on the outskirts. Veyda Willowwhisper expanded the collection facilities and founded the base ([[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]]).
- Lyren and [[Veyda Willowwhisper]] live there. [[Gaius Emberfell]], a field agent, is based here. [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor]] works from the research wing but is deployed to Port Damali ([[Aethor Kalisk's Workshop]]).

# What's Happening Now

- Aethor is in Port Damali. His deadline for the Stonefoot Compass passed on 8 Thunsheer 835 with no word ([[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]]).
- The town is in the quake's aftermath. The Echoforge works on as before.
- The Emissaries plan to watch the facility's security and Lyren's routines ([[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]]). [[Eledyr Dephar]]'s note calls it heavily defended, with predictable supply and staffing schedules.
- An unchecked seed from [[Session 17 - Aftermath at the Shrine of Melora]]: a courier from Palma Flora carries word of the Echoforge's progress.

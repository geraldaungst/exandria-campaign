# Quick Reference
**Status:** Corrupted region east of Xhorhas, with a coast. Home of [[Shoagragoth, The Acid Crown|Shoagragoth]].
**Significance:** Setting of the Shard 7 set piece: [[Rotthold]] on the approach, the [[Ebonglass Massif]] and [[Xagonstar]] at the end. [[Consecution's Hope]] sank just off its shore in 136 PD.

# Description
- Rosohna lies on the way to the Ebonglass Massif ([[Archivist Ovedo]], Open Issues).
- Weather, from the [[Climate Chart]]: spring toxic rains and strange weather phenomena; summer arcane storms and corrupted heat waves; autumn aberrant weather and acid rain; winter unnatural cold snaps and strange precipitation.
- Shoagragoth was born here about 85 PD.
- Places: [[Rotthold]], the [[Ebonglass Massif]] (with [[Xagonstar]]).

# What's Happening Now
- The party has not been here. The approach to Shard 7 is paused with the Stonefoot Compass lead ([[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]]).

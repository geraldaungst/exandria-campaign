# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Zadash]]

**Status:** Second-largest city in the Dwendalian Empire. The party has not been here. [[Hesterian Shyr's Infiltration]] brings them next.
**Significance:** The Myriad's seat in Zadash is held by "the Gentleman", who is [[Korfel Withrethin]], Hesterian's murderer. [[Eidechse - Amanda Jeane|Dechs]] has herbs to deliver here.

# Description

- Built upward, with leaning buildings and the Tri-Spire towers visible from far off. About 89,000 people. Crownsguard at the walls, Righteous Brand based at Marrow Keep, a Crown-appointed starosta and a lawmaster in the King's Hall. Districts and sites are on the player page.
- **The Evening Nip** is a tavern with the Myriad's seat beneath it, in tunnels that run under the city. No other tavern is set.
- The [[Cobalt Soul - DM|Cobalt Soul]]'s Valley Archive stands on the west edge of the Pentamarket (player page). It is a separate archive from the one in [[Cobalt Soul Archive in Rexxentrum|Rexxentrum]].

# What's Happening Now

- Korfel has held the seat since the crackdown of winter 834 to 835. His people are watching for the three Dreyara killers: a dragonborn with a wyvern, a striking blonde and a gnome with a thing for hats. He believes Hesterian is dead ([[Korfel Withrethin]]).
- The party's arrival: an invitation reaches them, then [[Cree Deeproots]]'s interview in a back room of the Evening Nip, then the flooded hall the next day ([[Korfel's Next Moves#Meeting him]]).
- Dechs needs her contact to deliver the herbs ([[Character Story Beats]]).
- The Gentleman holds Qalix's down payment for the Rexxentrum heist ([[Gentleman's Organization]]).
- [[Eledyr Dephar]], a Clasp agent, arrived about a week before the party. He poses as a scholar at the Valley Archive and asks Myriad runners about the crackdown ([[Korfel's Next Moves]]).
- Marta Halsen's refugee group came from here. [[Torvald Halsen]] is her brother-in-law in Deastok.

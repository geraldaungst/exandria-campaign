# Quick Reference
**Status:** The [[Cobalt Soul - DM|Cobalt Soul]]'s archive and library in Rexxentrum, a temple to Ioun. [[Archivist Ovedo]] works here. The party has not been here.
**Significance:** [[Shard 5 - Rexxentrum Shard|Shard 5]] lies in an unauthorized, warded vault in its foundations. Qalix wants it stolen.

# Description
- Library and temple. Open to anyone seeking knowledge. Some collections need special approval, visitors may be escorted by a monk, and nothing leaves ([[Cobalt Soul - DM]]).
- The Player Wiki page for Rexxentrum names it and gives nothing else. No layout, staff or collection is set in the vault.
- The Cobalt Soul also keeps the Valley Archive in [[Zadash - DM|Zadash]] and a third in [[Cobalt Soul Archive in Port Damali - DM|Port Damali]].

# What's Happening Now
- Ovedo is studying the prophecy text on Shard 5 and has chosen not to tell the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM|Obsidian Echoforge]].
- The Emissaries of the Sunfall watch Ovedo through [[Scroll and Scribe]].
- [[Kexira Suulveth]] sells to the Archive and does not know about Shard 5. [[Azel Brightful]] is helping her catalogue Draconian materials for it.
- Qalix sent [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM|Dreyara]] to steal the shard. She died before reaching Rexxentrum. The Gentleman holds the down payment ([[Qalix's Network]]).
- The Echoforge believes the Stonefoot Compass points to Shard 5. [[Lyren Willowwhisper]] may push to take it if the party befriends the order ([[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]]).

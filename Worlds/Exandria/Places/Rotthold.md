# Quick Reference
**Status:** Town in the Blightshore with a harbor and a Scavenger's Guild. Evidence of Shoagragoth and Malastryx survives here.
**Significance:** First stop on the approach to Shard 7. Shoagragoth plans to expand into it first.

# Description
- Harbor: the harbormaster's logs go back to about 535 PD.
- Scavenger's Guild: ledgers and a salvage journal.
- A shrine built by witnesses of the fight between Malastryx and Shoagragoth.
- A tavern song, "Copper's Last Riddle".
- The six surviving pieces of evidence, with the people who hold them: [[Rotthold Evidence of Shoagragoth and Malastryx]].

# What's Happening Now
- Nothing in the vault says how many of Shoagragoth's thralls are here, or whether any are.

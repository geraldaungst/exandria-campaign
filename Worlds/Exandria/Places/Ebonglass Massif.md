# Quick Reference
**Status:** Massif of volcanic glass in the Blightshore. [[Shoagragoth, The Acid Crown|Shoagragoth]], the Tyrant of the Ebonglass, rules it from [[Xagonstar]].
**Significance:** Holds [[Shard 7 - Ebonglass Shard|Shard 7]] (challenge 12-14, the major dragon encounter). One of the five sites on Lady Emer's rift map, which the party has seen.

# Description
- Volcanic glass formations and corrupted land.
- [[Xagonstar]], a buried dwarven stronghold, lies inside it.
- An Abyssal rift lies near Xagonstar. Emer's notes list a sample from "the Ebonglass rift": it looks solid, sometimes turns translucent, seems to move when not watched, weighs 0.5 to 3.2 lbs depending on how it is observed, and must not touch bare skin ([[Lady Emer's Research Notes]], item 10).
- Rosohna lies on the way ([[Archivist Ovedo]], Open Issues).

# What's Happening Now
- Shoagragoth sends corrupted thralls out to learn about other "sovereign powers" and similar black stone artifacts. He plans to expand through nearby settlements, starting with [[Rotthold]].
- The party has not been here. Aethor's compass lead is paused, and the Echoforge believes it points to Shard 5 ([[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]]).

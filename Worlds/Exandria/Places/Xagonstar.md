# Quick Reference
**Status:** Buried dwarven stronghold in the [[Ebonglass Massif]]. Shoagragoth's lair since about 500 PD.
**Significance:** Holds [[Shard 7 - Ebonglass Shard|Shard 7]]. Site of the Shard 7 encounter. Encounter ideas: [[Adaptable Modules]].

# Description
- Corrupted temple complex inside the stronghold. Shoagragoth rules a "kingdom" of corrupted thralls here with an elaborate court.
- Dwarven architecture, being "enhanced" with acid. Shoagragoth's crest scales are corroded into dwarven rune patterns and he wears a modified, corroded dwarven crown.

# What's Happening Now
- The acid is weakening the structure.
- Thralls go out to gather information about other "sovereign powers" and similar black stone artifacts.

# Secrets
> [!secret]- DM Only
> - The acid has exposed a sealed dwarven vault holding planar research artifacts.
> - One thrall is a trapped Kryn agent who found hints about the lost shard.
> - The shard is slowly affecting Shoagragoth's mind and feeding his sovereignty obsession.
> - The Abyssal rift nearby is unstable because he feeds it.

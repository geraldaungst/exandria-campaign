# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Port Zoon]]

**Status:** Industrial city of the Clovis Concord. The party has not been here. The [[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]] is paused: the party holds the [[Stonefoot Compass - DM]] and has not decided what to do with it.
**Significance:** [[Keldar Stonefoot - DM]], chief cartographer of the Cartographers Guild, owns the [[Consecution's Hope|map]] to a shard of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]]. [[Calderax Dunhall]] lived here before the Echoforge recruited him.

# Description

- Artisan and crafting guilds are abundant and powerful. Industrial areas are prominent. Smoke and smog hang over the city.
- The Port Zoon Cartographers Guild is led by Keldar Stonefoot, whose family made the compass.
- The Consecution's Hope stopped here to resupply on its way back to the Blightshore. A drunk crewman sold the map to a pirate, and Keldar bought it from him ([[Consecution's Hope]]).
- Calderax studied in Zadash, then moved here to research planar anomalies before the Echoforge recruited him ([[Calderax Dunhall]]).

# What's Happening Now

- Keldar will trade the map for the compass, which he says is a Stonefoot heirloom lost in the Othemoor ([[Keldar Stonefoot Letter to Calderax]]). Calderax is brokering the trade, but he does not hold the compass. The party does.
- [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor]] wanted the compass as a trade for Calderax's information. His deadline passed on 8 Thunsheer 835.

# Secrets

> [!secret]- DM Only
> - [ ] The map and the compass lead to [[Shard 7 - Ebonglass Shard|Shard 7]]. Aethor, Calderax and the Echoforge believe they lead to [[Shard 5 - Rexxentrum Shard|Shard 5]]. Keldar's map shows where Shard 7 once was, not where it is now.

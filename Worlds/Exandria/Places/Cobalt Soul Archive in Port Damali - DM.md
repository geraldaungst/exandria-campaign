**Status:** The [[Cobalt Soul - DM|Cobalt Soul]]'s library and archive in Port Damali, one of three (with Rexxentrum and the Valley Archive in Zadash). [[Archivist Vani]] works here. The party has used it.
**Significance:** Here the party learned the Echoforge's history and the prophecy. [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor]] visited several times while researching the Lorestone.

# Description

- A library and archive open to researchers. The Cobalt Soul keeps archives in major cities and works openly in the Empire.
- Vani, a halfling archivist, helped the party learn about the [[The Prophecy|prophecy]] and the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]]. She knows [[Archivist Ovedo]] in Rexxentrum.
- No layout, staff or collection is set in the vault.

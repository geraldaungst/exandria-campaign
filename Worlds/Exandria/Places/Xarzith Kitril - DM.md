# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Xarzith Kitril]]

**Status:** Dragonborn city in southern Xhorhas, governed by the six-member council of the [[Scars of Scale and Tooth]]. The party has not been here.
**Significance:** [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] holds the public office of Eye of the Scars here and runs his restoration of Draconia from a hidden structure in the Ruins of Draconia. [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] and [[Melthes]] come from here, and Melthes's parents may still live in the city. [[Restore Draconia]] comes up when the party heads this way.

# Description

- "Cold Jewel" in Draconic. Founded after Draconia fell to the Chroma Conclave in 811 PD, as a refuge for the survivors.
- Draconic architecture with practical defenses, built to withstand the climate. Carvings of dragonborn history decorate the buildings.
- Governed by the council of the [[Scars of Scale and Tooth]], all Ravenite dragonborn. The Eye of the Scars protects the council and leads the Wardens of the Ravine. Qalix holds that office. Members and their views are on the Scars note.
- History and dates: [[History of Xarzith Kitril and Draconia]] and [[Timeline of Draconia]].

# What's Happening Now

- [[Azel Brightful]] is moving from Rexxentrum toward the city.

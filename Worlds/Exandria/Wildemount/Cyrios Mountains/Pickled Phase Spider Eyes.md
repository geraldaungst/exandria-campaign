_Wondrous item, rare (consumable), requires no attunement_

These cloudy, brined spider eyes are stored in small stone jars filled with a sharply pungent, milky liquid. Once harvested from phase spiders, the eyes were pickled in an alchemical brine developed by firbolg monks who studied planar creatures. They used the eyes both as a research tool and as fuel for good-natured pranks. When consumed, the eye briefly imparts some of the phase spider’s strange abilities—along with some unsettling side effects.

---

**Consumable.** As an action, you can eat a pickled phase spider eye. Make a **DC 14 Constitution saving throw**.

- **On a success:** You feel a brief shiver of strange sensations but suffer no ill effect.
- **On a failure:** You become **poisoned for 1 minute** as nausea and vertigo set in.

A creature that is poisoned this way can **use its action on a subsequent turn to retch violently**, immediately ending the poisoned condition—but if it does so, the **magical effect of the eye also ends**.

Then roll a d4 to determine the effect:

|d4|Effect Name|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Twitch Step**|Within 1 minute, you can use a bonus action to teleport up to 5 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. A soft _pop!_ and faint vinegar scent accompany the blink.|
|2|**Light as a Web**|For 1 minute, you take **half damage from falling**, gain a **climb speed equal to your walking speed**, and count as **half your normal weight** for the purpose of traps, pressure plates, or fragile surfaces.|
|3|**Phantom Phase**|For the next 10 minutes, at the start of each of your turns, roll a d6. On a **1**, you **phase out** for one round. While phased out, you vanish from sight, are untargetable, and are unaware of your surroundings. At the end of the round, you reappear in the same or nearest unoccupied space. _(This is not actual Ethereal travel.)_|
|4|**Spider Sense**|For 10 minutes, you gain **advantage on Wisdom (Perception)** checks that rely on sight or detecting movement. Your pupils briefly become multifaceted like a spider’s.|

---

**Taste and Side Effects.** The eyes are rubbery and vinegary, with a faint metallic tang. A successful swallow is followed by tingling skin or sudden weightlessness. A failed swallow usually involves gagging, coughing, or impressive retching noises. The monks used to keep score.

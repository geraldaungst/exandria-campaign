## Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip

A one-room tavern and cheesery at the edge of the Cyrios Mountains. Not in a town—but close enough that regulars from nearby settlements make the trip, and far enough that travelers treat it as a known haven.

**Proprietor:** [[Grent Gnurlsotten]]

**First visited:** [[the-herdsman-of-gnurlsottens-nosh-and-nip]]

---

### The Building

Small, plain, sturdily built of mountain logs against the hillside. Good craftsmanship, no ornamentation.

A second sign hangs below the main one, in a slightly different and clearly later-added style: **"…and Cheesery."** Tacked on once the cheese took off; Grent never bothered redoing the whole sign properly.

**The cave.** The building is constructed directly over a natural cave mouth in the rock, fully enclosing it. From the road it just looks like a modest log tavern—no sign of a cave at all unless you're inside and know to look.

The cave's naturally cold, mineral-rich walls are where the cheese ages. **Grent does not let anyone back there.** Not customers, not regulars, not friends.

---

### Hollowfrost Wheel

The cheese. Legendary across Wildemount, and rumored—wrongly—to involve blood sacrifice, a lost dwarven recipe, or a pact with a cave spirit.

**The mundane truth:** it's goats, this specific cave, and thirty years of Grent not really knowing why it works so well.

**Price:** 80 gp a wheel, sold here. Buyers resell elsewhere for roughly double.

#### Echo Tongue

A genuine minor magical property, and part of why the cheese is so sought after.

> For 1 hour after eating a portion of Hollowfrost Wheel, your voice carries with unnatural clarity and volume—as if the stillness of the aging cave itself lives in your throat. At will during this hour, you can cause your voice to boom up to three times louder than normal for 1 minute, audible clearly at a great distance even over wind, crowds, or storm.
>
> This is the "manipulate your voice" option of the *thaumaturgy* cantrip and no other part of that spell. It has no combat application beyond being heard—it doesn't frighten, damage, or otherwise affect creatures.

### The Regulars

A small, consistent crowd—some local to nearby settlements, some travelers who've adopted this place as a haven, a few who specifically know the cheese's reputation. They know Grent's temper is mostly bluster, and they know exactly how to defuse it: never by asking him to concede, always by giving him a bigger and more public way out.

| Name | Nickname | Stat block | Role at the table |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yssa Marrenthal** | "Duchess" | Orc, Scout | Steps in when Grent corners himself |
| **Ferdinand Coelho** | "Lefty" | Human, Commoner | Comic relief, easy mark for information |
| **Old Ren Pallick** | "Winters" | Dwarf, Commoner *(or Scout)* | Keeper of what counts as "tradition" here |
| **Bram Ostwick** | "Loud Bram" | Halfling, Commoner | Runs the betting whenever there's a brawl |

#### Yssa "Duchess" Marrenthal—orc, Scout

Muted sage-grey skin, heavy brow, two small blunt lower tusks, one chipped. Weathered and sun-lined, forty-ish, in layered wool that smells permanently of livestock. Cuts her hair short herself and badly. Speaks in short declarative bursts and never softens anything.

**Nickname:** earned ironically after winning a card game with absurdly lucky hands. It's a joke about how thoroughly she isn't one, and she leans into it.

#### Ferdinand "Lefty" Coelho—human, Commoner

Lanky, mid-thirties, perpetually mid-sentence about something he's already lost the thread of. Pats his pockets constantly. Genuinely delighted by everything, including being the butt of the joke.

**Nickname:** misplaced his own left boot twice in one week and Grent never let it go. *(Not left-handed. Not missing a hand. Everyone guesses both.)*

#### Old Ren "Winters" Pallick—dwarf, Commoner or Scout

Gaunt and wind-burned, seventy or more, lean to the point of hollowed out—unusual for a dwarf, hands like knotted rope. Sits nearest the fire and tells the same four stories in rotation. The regulars have stopped checking whether any of them are true.

**Nickname:** from a boast made years ago that he survived six winters alone in the high passes. Sounds like a plain surname—easy to miss that it's a nickname at all.

> [!note] Stat block is a live choice
> **Commoner** if the boast was always empty. **Scout** if there's truth in it. Leave it unresolved and pick at the table depending on how the party treats him.

#### Bram "Loud Bram" Ostwick—halfling, Commoner

Four feet tall, stout and barrel-chested, roughly thirty, ruddy and gap-toothed, with a voice that carries through a closed door. Stands on a bench to be at eye level with anyone he's talking to. Takes bets on everything and settles them honestly, which is why people keep letting him.

**Nickname:** disappointingly literal. Also the funniest thing about him, given his size.

**In the brawl:** runs the book. If he insists on a warm-up round against Grent, he lasts one round and could not be happier about it.

#### House Traditions

- **The newcomer trial.** Grent's long-standing way of testing strangers and settling disputes—a series of escalating challenges, ending in a nonlethal brawl with Grent himself. Nobody actually fails; it's hazing in the warmest possible sense, and everyone is welcomed by the end.
- **The nickname needle.** Regulars tell newcomers to "ask Grent about his nickname." He doesn't have one. He is reliably, mildly annoyed about this.
- **Betting.** On the trial, on the brawl, on the goats. Bram runs the book badly.

### Hooks

- **The splurge items.** A few visibly expensive objects sit around the tavern, kept because Grent likes them rather than to show off. Unexplained. A reason to come back.
- **The cheese cave.** Nobody gets in. Grent doesn't fully understand why the cave works, either.
- **Banked trial ideas.** *Wheel-Balance Gauntlet* and *The Long Story*—unused challenges held for a return visit.

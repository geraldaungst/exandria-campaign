## Quick Reference

**Status:** A tavern that travels the multiverse, appearing in one realm after another. Proprietor: Elira Windacre.
**Significance:** Crossroads for travelers displaced from their worlds. Home of [[Shadowdance Sake]]. Opens the [[The Shining Shrine]] one-shot (the party meets Threeflower here). [[Unused Ideas]] lists it as a tavern that could visit the Eryndor campaign.

## Description

- An unassuming saloon that gives sanctuary to people far from home. Patrons come from many worlds.
- It appears in a realm for a short visit, then moves on. Time can run differently while it is there.
- Its signature drink is [[Shadowdance Sake]], served as a single shot, tasting of a moonless night.
- Ormund Faelan founded it and ran it for thirty years. Elira Windacre, a former adventurer and regular, took it over. Full text: [[The Single Swig Saloon - Founding and Handover]].

### Employees

- **Tibbs Quiverleaf:** halfling head bartender. Remembers every patron's drink and mixes to match their mood. Infectious laugh.
- **Mara Shadowend:** quiet, observant tiefling bouncer. Senses trouble before it starts and stops it with a look or a soft word.
- **Grennik:** the cook. Species unknown and debated. Communicates in grunts and gestures. Dishes blend flavors from many worlds and are loved by everyone.

### Regulars

- **Old Jorrum:** dwarf, a patron since the opening. Claims to have tried every drink and says none beats Ormund's original brew. Tells stories of the early days.
- **Zephyra Trephlax:** young mage who records where the tavern appears and tries to predict its movements with arcane algorithms.
- **Flick and Quick:** gnome twin inventors. They use the tavern as a workshop in each new realm and gather exotic materials. Their experiments sometimes go wrong.
- **Faelenya Liriel:** elf bard whose songs follow the Saloon's travels. Listeners feel they have traveled with it.
- **Rorik Montride:** human merchant who found the tavern on one of its stops and now trades goods between worlds through it.

### Three unusual events

1. **The Infinite Ale Night:** the Saloon appeared in a realm where time flowed differently. One night lasted what felt like months to the patrons. The ale never ran dry, and friendships made that night lasted a lifetime. Back home, one evening passed.
2. **The Gathering of Echoes:** bards from many worlds met by accident and played an impromptu festival. The music resonated with the crossing's magic, and echoes can sometimes be heard faintly in the Saloon at dusk.
3. **The Feywild Feast:** the Saloon visited the Feywild and hosted a feast for the local fey court. For a time the Saloon and everyone in it gained minor shape-shifting. Patrons tell of dancing the night away in forms not their own.

## What's Happening Now

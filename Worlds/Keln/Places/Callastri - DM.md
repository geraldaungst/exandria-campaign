**Significance:** Holds the two powers the Keln notes describe: [[The Ringhold]] (north and west) and [[Varrinthal Kingdom]] (southeast), divided by the Kelvantor Mountains.

# Description

## DM history

Callastri's known history begins with [[The Ringhold]]. Five survivors of the coastal city of [[Ruins of Marethis|Marethis]], destroyed by a great tidal wave, found a [[Deep]] high in the mountains and built anew: [[Tur-Marethis]], "New Marethis." One of the first Deeps discovered on the continent, it grew into one of Callastri's oldest and most influential powers, governed ever since by [[The Ring]], the council named for that founding five.

[[Varrinthal Kingdom]]'s founding came later and separately, discovered by a lone Gifted wanderer. Over generations it grew into the region's dominant city-state, its wealth and reach eventually extending to smaller settlements like [[Watcher's Rest]].

The vast wilderness between and around both powers remains loosely known. Travel is hard, dangerous, and mostly limited to established routes.

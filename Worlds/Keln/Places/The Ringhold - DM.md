# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[The Ringhold]]

**Status:** Confederation of settlements across the north and west of [[Callastri]], governed from [[Tur-Marethis]] by [[The Ring]], a permanent five-seat council. No campaign is set here yet.
**Significance:** Elected government with a known weak point (unaudited signature ballots). Its founders' myth is the Keln world's clearest case of the Quickened myth versus reality, and it contrasts with [[Varrinthal Kingdom]]'s divine-right monarchy.

# Description

## Government mechanics

The Ring is a permanent five-seat council (see [[The Ring]]). Seats are lifetime appointments; upon a Voice's death, the remaining Voices each nominate a [[Quickened]] candidate, and an election is held among the nominees.

### The Voices and the founders

- Each seat is named for one of the five founders. The official view, and the view of most commoners, is that the Voice in a seat speaks with that founder's viewpoint, much as the Pope is said to speak for God.
- Voices have taken this with different degrees of literalness. Some treat it as metaphor: their own beliefs drive decisions and they represent the founder by association. Others hold themselves to representing the founder directly. The idea was meant to give each successor standing.

## Elections

Charter grants suffrage to all Ringhold citizens, but practically, only settlements formally on the confederation's rolls participate—see membership below. Within a city or town, neighborhoods self-organize to choose a preferred candidate by whatever local method they like, then select one representative to carry a signed ballot (with all participating citizens' signatures) to [[Tur-Marethis]]. Votes are counted by signature, not by ballot—one person, one signature, regardless of distance traveled. Distant member-towns need only send one representative, not their whole population. Each town/neighborhood decides its own definition of "eligible voter."

## Membership and growth

The Ringhold grew primarily through voluntary petition, not conquest. A settlement learns of the Ringhold's resources/protection, decides the benefits outweigh independence, and applies to join—agreeing to Ringhold law and the electoral system in exchange. Conquest/annexation exists elsewhere in [[Callastri|Callastri's]] history but is the rare exception, not how the Ringhold itself expanded. This mirrors how [[Varrinthal Kingdom|Varrinthal's]] realm likely absorbed places like [[Watcher's Rest]]: not conquest, but opt-in alliance.

## The Founders

Yselda Torb, Bess Marawen, Hob Halloway, Corwin Somes, Poru Adderick. Ordinary survivors, not chosen ones. Over generations their names acquired mythic weight despite humble origins.

**Myth:** The Ringhold tells the five as extremely special, prime examples of the [[Quickened]]. This is the version that survives. What actually happened is in Secrets.

# What's Happening Now

- Nothing is set. The Ringhold has not appeared at the table.

# Secrets

> [!secret]- DM Only
> - [ ] The five founders were ordinary survivors. Circumstance pushed them into becoming adventurers, and they developed their abilities along the way. Being treated as special is what fed the idea that the Quickened are born different.
> - [ ] No formal audit exists for signature sheets. Fraud is possible and has likely happened, undetected, at least occasionally. Good seed for a local corruption plot.

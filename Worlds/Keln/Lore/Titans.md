## DM History

Titans were once-living beings capable of channeling the planet's core magic directly. [[Varrinthal Kingdom|Varrinthal]]'s founding myth claims its first ruler, [[Varrin]], was personally appointed by the Titans to claim the land, giving the monarchy's "divine right" an unintentional grain of truth. Where Titans came from and why they died is an open question.

> [!secret]- DM Only
> - [ ] The Titans are dead. Their petrified remains are scattered in multiple places worldwide ([[Watcher's Knee]] among them), a fact almost no one currently knows.
> - [ ] [[Watcher's Knee]] is the literal petrified knee of a Titan; the rest of the body runs underground through the region. Kneestone's warmth, chatoyancy and enchantment retention come from its being organic remains, not mineral.

- Origin and death are left unresolved on purpose: different cultures may hold different myths about the Titans, and none needs to be canon.
- Finding out about the Knee is optional background unless the party pursues it.

## Magical Essence

The animating life-force every living being carries in some degree. What separates the [[Quickened]] from "ordinary" [[Groundfolk]] isn't a hard line, it's a spectrum, and a person's place on it isn't fixed: anyone can develop it over time. Nobody in-world understands that; the common belief is that the Quickened are born different. Not common knowledge in-world.

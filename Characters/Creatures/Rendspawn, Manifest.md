## Core Information

### Manifest Rendspawn

*Large aberration, chaotic evil*

**AC** 18 (hardened aberrant flesh) | **HP** 195 (17d12 + 85) | **Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|22 (+6)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|

![[manifest-rendspawn.png|right|300]]
**Saves** Str +11, Con +10, Wis +7
**Skills** Athletics +11, Perception +7, Stealth +8
**Resistances** necrotic, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Immunities** charmed, frightened
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 17
**Languages** understands Deep Speech but can't speak
**CR** 13 (10,000 XP) | **Prof** +5

#### Traits

**Rift Sense.** Knows direction and distance to nearest planar rift within 5 miles.

**Incomplete Form.** Upon death, dissolves into dimensional residue. No corpse remains.

**Reality Hunger.** Regains 15 HP at start of turn if it has at least 1 HP. Stops if takes 30+ radiant damage from single source in one turn OR if more than 1 mile from active rift. Resumes next turn if conditions met.

**Dimensional Phasing (Recharge 5-6).** Reaction when hit: attack has disadvantage. If still hits, takes half damage.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** Can choose to succeed on failed save.

#### Actions

**Multiattack.** Four attacks: two claws and two tendrils, or Tendril Storm if available.

**Claw.** *Melee:* +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) slashing.

**Tendril.** *Melee:* +11, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 13 (1d12 + 6) bludgeoning, target grappled (DC 17). Can grapple up to four creatures.

**Tendril Storm (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature within 15 feet: DC 17 Dex save. Failure: 26 (4d12) bludgeoning + grappled (DC 17). Success: half damage, not grappled. Can grapple up to four creatures total.

#### Bonus Actions

**Predatory Grapple.** Tendril attack. On hit, target grappled (DC 17) and pulled 10 feet. If already grappling, pull one grappled creature 15 feet closer.

**Consume Flesh (Grappled Target Required).** *Melee:* +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 32 (4d12 + 6) piercing. Manifest Rendspawn regains HP equal to half damage dealt.

#### Legendary Actions (3/round, end of another creature's turn)

**Tendril Strike.** One tendril attack.

**Pull (Costs 2).** Pulls all grappled creatures 10 feet closer.

**Phase Step (Costs 2).** Teleports up to 30 feet to unoccupied space within 15 feet of active rift.

#### Encounter Notes

- **Tactics**: Coordinate with allies, use Tendril Storm to control battlefield, phase away when threatened
- **Vaud's forces**: Deployed as guardians and assassins during levels 12-17
- **Weakness**: Distance from rifts weakens Reality Hunger; radiant damage disrupts regeneration

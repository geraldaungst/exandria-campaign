# Core Information
## Rendspawn

_Large aberration, chaotic evil_

**AC** 17 (unnatural hide) | **HP** 115 (10d12 + 50) | **Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
![[rendspawn.png|right|300]]
**Saves** Str +9, Con +9  
**Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +5  
**Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Immunities** frightened  
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15  
**Languages** —  
**CR** 9 (5,000 XP) | **Prof** +4

### Traits

**Rift Sense.** Knows direction and distance to nearest planar rift within 5 miles.

**Incomplete Form.** Upon death, dissolves into warm, oily dimensional residue. No corpse remains.

**Adaptive Regeneration.** Regains 10 HP at start of turn if it has at least 1 HP. Stops if takes 20+ radiant damage from single source in one turn. Resumes next turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Three attacks: two claws and one bite, or four tendrils.

**Claw.** _Melee:_ +9, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing.

**Bite.** _Melee:_ +9, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing.

**Tendril.** _Melee:_ +9, reach 15 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (1d12 + 5) bludgeoning, target grappled (DC 16). Can grapple up to three creatures.

### Bonus Actions

**Predatory Grapple.** Tendril attack. On hit, target grappled (DC 16) and pulled 10 feet. If already grappling, pull one grappled creature 10 feet closer.

**Consume Flesh (Grappled Target Required).** _Melee:_ +9, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 23 (4d8 + 5) piercing. Rendspawn regains HP equal to half damage dealt.

### Encounter Notes

- **Tactics**: Grapple multiple targets, pull them close, consume while regenerating
- **First appearance**: 2-3 near active rift sites at level 7-9
- **Weakness**: Radiant damage disrupts regeneration

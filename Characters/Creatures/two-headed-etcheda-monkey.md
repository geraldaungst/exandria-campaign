*Small monstrosity, unaligned*

Armor Class: 12
Hit Points: 22 (4d6 + 8)
Speed: 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

![[etcheda-monkey.png|right|300]]

STR - 8 (-1)
DEX - 14 (+2)
CON - 14 (+2)
INT - 4 (-3)
WIS - 10 (+0)
CHA - 6 (-2)

Skills: Acrobatics +4, Perception +3
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft.

Actions:

**Multiattack**. The monkey makes one bite attack and one claw attack.

**Bite**. +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 1 piercing damage.

**Claws**. +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 slashing damage.

**Crystalline Flash** (Recharge 5-6). One head emits a flash in a 15 ft. cone. Creatures in area must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of monkey's next turn.

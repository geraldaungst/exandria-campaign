# Core Information
## Harbinger Rendspawn

_Large aberration, chaotic evil_

**AC** 19 (aberrant carapace) | **HP** 285 (24d12 + 120) | **Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |   INT   |   WIS   |   CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :-----: |
| 24 (+7) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

![[harbinger-rendspawn.jpeg|right|300]]
**Saves** Str +13, Con +11, Wis +9, Cha +7  
**Skills** Athletics +13, Insight +9, Perception +9, Stealth +10  
**Resistances** necrotic, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed  
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 19  
**Languages** Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft.  
**CR** 17 (18,000 XP) | **Prof** +6

### Traits

**Rift Sense.** Knows direction and distance to all planar rifts within 10 miles.

**Perfect Form.** Upon death, body stabilizes into crystallized dimensional matter (can be studied). Contains residual Far Realm energy.

**Unbound Regeneration.** Regains 20 HP at start of turn if it has at least 1 HP. Functions regardless of distance from rifts. Stops if takes 40+ radiant damage from single source in one turn. Resumes next turn.

**Reality Rejection.** Reaction when hit: attack has disadvantage. If still hits, takes half damage. No recharge—always active.

**Planar Anchor.** Cannot be banished, plane shifted, or forcibly teleported against its will. Movement abilities function across planar boundaries.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** Can choose to succeed on failed save.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Five attacks: two claws, one bite, and two tendrils.

**Claw.** _Melee:_ +13, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 20 (2d12 + 7) slashing plus 9 (2d8) necrotic.

**Bite.** _Melee:_ +13, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 23 (3d10 + 7) piercing plus 9 (2d8) psychic. Target: DC 18 Int save or stunned until end of Harbinger's next turn.

**Tendril.** _Melee:_ +13, reach 20 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (1d12 + 7 + 1d6) bludgeoning, target grappled (DC 19). Can grapple up to five creatures.

**Rift Pulse (Recharge 5-6).** 30-foot radius from Harbinger. Each creature: DC 18 Con save. Failure: 45 (10d8) force damage + pulled 15 feet toward Harbinger + grappled (DC 19). Success: half damage, not pulled or grappled.

### Bonus Actions

**Apex Predator.** Tendril attack. On hit, target grappled (DC 19) and pulled 15 feet. If already grappling, pull all grappled creatures 10 feet closer.

**Void Consumption (Grappled Target Required).** _Melee:_ +13, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 45 (6d12 + 7) piercing plus 18 (4d8) necrotic. Harbinger regains HP equal to total damage dealt.

**Dimensional Step.** Teleports up to 60 feet to unoccupied space it can see.

### Legendary Actions (3/round, end of another creature's turn)

**Tendril Strike.** One tendril attack.

**Observe (Costs 2).** Studies one creature within 60 feet. Until end of Harbinger's next turn, that creature has disadvantage on saves against Harbinger's abilities, and Harbinger has advantage on attacks against it.

**Breach Reality (Costs 3).** Creates 10-foot radius rift at point within 60 feet (lasts 1 minute). Creatures entering or starting turn in rift: DC 18 Wis save or take 22 (4d10) psychic damage + frightened (save ends). Harbinger can teleport to any active rift as bonus action.

### Encounter Notes

- **Tactics**: Highly intelligent. Observes before engaging. Uses Breach Reality for battlefield control and escape. Prefers strategic withdrawal to death.
- **Appears after**: Vaud's defeat (level 18+). Independent and purposeful.
- **Mission**: Herald Ceratos's arrival. Combat is secondary to gathering intelligence.
- **Weakness**: Massive radiant damage (40+) required to stop regeneration. Will flee rather than risk destruction before mission complete.

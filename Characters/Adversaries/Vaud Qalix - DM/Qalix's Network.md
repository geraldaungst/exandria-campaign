## Qalix's Network

How [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Vaud Qalix]]'s intelligence network passes information, and where it stands after [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM|Dreyara]]'s death. Add other parts of the network here as they are designed. DM only.

### How messages move

- Qalix trusts his spymaster to run the network and does not micromanage it.
- **Sending is rare.** Qalix reserves it for news that can't wait for a courier.
- **No sending stones** anywhere in the network. Too easy to lose, steal, or turn.
- **Urgent word from the field:** Dreyara used a scroll of Sending or went through the handler.
- **Routine traffic:** coded letters through cutouts and couriers. Each link knows only the next one.
- **[[Lady Emer - DM|Lady Emer]]** has never met Qalix. His letters came with Dreyara's visits. Her trained birds fly to the handler.
- **Courier east** to Qalix in Xhorhas: weeks, across a militarized border.

> [!note]- The handler (DM mechanism, not an NPC)
> - Based in [[Zadash - DM|Zadash]], inside [[Korfel Withrethin|the Gentleman]]'s network. Dreyara used the Gentleman's infrastructure as part of her operation; the handler is probably how Emer joined the network in the first place.
> - Receives coded traffic, re-codes it, and sends it on by courier. Can cast Sending.
> - Shares most of what passes through with the Gentleman, because it serves him to. Keeps back what is worth more to him unshared.
> - Personally loyal to Dreyara and shaken by her death. Sympathy for Draconia: undecided.
> - The party never needs to meet him. If asked, the Gentleman says only that his connections learned of her death.

### Who knows what (as the party reaches Zadash)

- [ ] **Qalix:** Dreyara is dead, Cloudfang was raided, [[Varnes Dwell - DM|Varnes Dwell]] is free, and the crystal and Shard 6 were taken by the people who did it. Names and Emer's full account follow by courier. His response is undecided.
- [ ] **Lady Emer:** everything she saw at Cloudfang, plus Ra'ak's account. Has vowed revenge. Slants her reports to protect her standing.
- [ ] **Ra'ak:** saw Dreyara die and the party take the shard. Free. Says he was just hired muscle.
- [ ] **The handler:** Emer's full report.
- **The Gentleman:** Dreyara is dead, killed by the three he is watching for. Still holds Qalix's down payment for the Rexxentrum job ([[Characters/Factions/Exandria/Gentleman's Organization]]).
  - [ ] Does not know: that the party carries the shard or the crystal, or that Hesterian is with them.
  - [ ] Wants: his identity kept buried; to come out ahead whether the job goes on, changes, or collapses; to judge whether the three are a threat or worth something.
- [ ] **The party:** Dreyara worked for a dragonborn employer ("the Eye," Xarzith Kitril), and her Zadash contact is the Gentleman. They do not know they are known.

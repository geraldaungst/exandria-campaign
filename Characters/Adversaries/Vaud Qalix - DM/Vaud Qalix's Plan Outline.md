### Vaud's Incomplete Understanding

While Vaud believes his plan is methodical and well-researched, his understanding contains several critical gaps that will ultimately doom his efforts:

**Oversimplified Lorestone Assembly**: Vaud assumes that simply possessing all the shards will allow him to reconstruct the complete Lorestone. He lacks understanding of the specific ritual requirements, the temporal constraints involved, or the fact that several shards may have been corrupted beyond use. His plan treats the assembly as a straightforward puzzle rather than the complex magical undertaking it truly represents.

**Flawed Rift Theory**: Central to Vaud's strategy is his belief that rifts follow predictable patterns he can decode and exploit. His research notes are filled with charts mapping "rift signatures" and "planar resonance frequencies." In reality, these readings represent random chaotic fluctuations.

**The Arcanist's Prism**: His theory also concluded that the Conduit needs an [[Arcanist's Prism]] for "final stabilization." It's a legend; no such artifact exists, and his agents have never found one.

**Underestimated Opposition**: While Vaud acknowledges that the Obsidian Echoforge, Ashari Riftguard, and Malachite Cord will oppose him, he believes their resistance stems from ignorance or fear rather than legitimate understanding. He assumes that demonstrating the Conduit's power will convert them to his cause, not realizing that they oppose him because they correctly understand the catastrophic risks involved.

**The Luxon Beacon**: He knows the Conduit needs a Luxon Beacon, but not what one looks like or how to get one; Beacon lore is closely held within the Kryn Dynasty. He never recognized the crystal Emer studied, and does not know he has lost one.

**Timeline Pressures**: Vaud feels urgency about completing his plan before war breaks out between the Kryn Dynasty and the Dwendalian Empire, believing this conflict will complicate his resource acquisition.

**Resource Management Blind Spots**: Despite his substantial treasury and network of agents, Vaud has overcommitted resources to objectives built on a flawed theory. His 12 arcane researchers are studying flawed theories, his 75 artifact seekers are chasing false leads, and his spies are gathering intelligence based on fundamentally incorrect assumptions about how planar magic functions.

His patron and rift theory: see Hidden Information in [[Vaud Qalix - DM]].

## Quick Reference

> [!info] Essential Details
> - **Location:** The Far Realm. Not present in Exandria
> - **Occupation:** Great Old One, the campaign's true Nemesis
> - **Goal:** Open a permanent passage from the Far Realm into Exandria. Chaos is its nature, not its fuel
> - **Attitude:** Superiority. Stake: nothing else matters, all in
> - **Attitude toward party:** Not established. It acts only through others
> - **Key knowledge:** It cannot touch the rifts and does not know where they came from
> - **Reveal:** Not until [[Vaud Qalix - DM]] is eliminated or defeated

## Description & Roleplay

The Never-Present Nemesis, working through the Apparent Nemesis ([[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]]) and other villains. The party meets its effects, its thralls and its early hints, never the thing itself, until the finale.

- **Appearance:** Not designed. [[Far Realm Effects]] covers what its presence does to a scene
- **Voice:** Not designed. Qalix credits his powers to "the voice beyond the veil"
- **Theme:** Order and Chaos

## Current Situation

*Ceratos wants a permanent passage from the Far Realm into Exandria, and is having difficulty because it cannot touch the rifts itself, its warlock thralls don't yet understand them well enough to connect them, and the PCs keep shrinking or sealing the rifts they find.*

- It doesn't care whether its thralls stabilize a rift or tear it wider, only that the result lets it through.
- Qalix is one of its thralls. Its early hints are the Rendspawn (Hidden Information).
- The rifts have been spreading since the original rupture. The spread is only now accelerating enough to be noticed ([[Understanding the Rifts]]).

## Hidden Information

> [!secret]- DM Only
> - [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Vaud Qalix]] is one of its warlock thralls. It steers him toward the rifts because it cannot touch them itself. He has never spoken with it and does not know what it is
> - It does not care whether Qalix's plan succeeds. A stable portal, a Planar Inversion or a rift expanding out of control all give it a way in; only a clean closure denies it ([[Harmonic Planar Conduit]])
> - It does not know the rifts' origin, the [[Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing]]. Only the DM knows ([[Understanding the Rifts]])
> - The Rendspawn were designed as the first hints of it: the lowest tier (CR 9) for levels 7-9, the Manifest tier (CR 13) as Qalix's controlled forces, the Harbinger tier (CR 17) as true heralds of the breach ([[Rendspawn Reference Note]])
> - The reveal comes after the Qalix confrontation and is meant to be short: a cliffhanger, one investigation session, one approach session, the finale

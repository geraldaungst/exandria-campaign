## Description & Roleplay

A woman in her early thirties with fair skin, her dark brown hair pulled back into a practical braid, revealing a faint scar under her left eye. Her piercing green eyes observe keenly, reflecting a mixture of intensity and hidden depths. She wears a fitted, high-collared tunic in dark green, paired with reinforced leather pants and knee-high boots. Strapped to her arm is her dagger, seemingly intended as an ominous and open threat.

- Voice: Precise and measured, with a hint of Rexxentrum accent she hasn't fully shed
- Mannerisms:
  - Maintains direct eye contact when speaking
  - Unconsciously touches the scar under her left eye when thinking
  - Keeps her hands visible but near her weapons
  - Moves with deliberate economy, no wasted gestures
- Notable Traits:
  - Projects an aura of controlled danger
  - Speaks with calculated honesty when lying would serve no purpose
  - Shows professional respect for competence
  - Never fully relaxes, always maintaining situational awareness

## Current Situation

Deceased. Killed by the Third Act outside the rear entrance of the [[Monastery of Dimensional Harmony - DM|Monastery of Dimensional Harmony]] on 25 Thunsheer 835. Her companion Ra'ak survived and was released. What her death set in motion: [[Qalix's Network]].

### What she did

- Ran Qalix's spy network and was his go-between with [[Lady Emer - DM|Lady Emer]], delivering research specimens and, once, [[Varnes Dwell - DM|Varnes Dwell]] as a prisoner to [[Cloudfang Keep - DM|Cloudfang Keep]]
- In Port Damali she stole [[Shard 6 - Marquet Shard|Shard 6]] from [[Calderax Dunhall]]'s room and had a forged letter planted to frame him and burn him as an asset ([[Aelorin's HQ Basement Text]])
- Recruited the Gentleman for the Rexxentrum Archive heist (below)
- Was cultivating Myriad contacts in Port Damali (below)
- Named [[Valen Elderguard]] as leader of the Emissaries of the Sunfall, and "the Eye", in conversation with Calderax. The party learned both through [[Radelia Caphax]]

> [!note]- Myriad Contacts in Port Damali
> Dreyara was cultivating alliances with the [[Myriad - DM|Myriad]] in [[Port Damali - DM]] on Qalix's behalf when she died. Key targets:
> - **[[Father Dwondaff Pierce]]**—head of the Pearl Shrine, contact for [[Korfel Withrethin|the Gentleman]]. Can provide safe havens, moral/social influence, and access to religious community resources.
> - **Lord Gabriel Rymmer**—manager of the Exalted Collection Auction House, Rymmer family. Can provide financial resources and black market access for rare artifacts.
>
> Approach: offered tangible proof of Qalix's resources (enchanted items, gold) and showed commitment by orchestrating operations against rival factions.

> [!note]- The Gentleman and the Rexxentrum Job
> Her Myriad partner for the Rexxentrum Archive heist was [[Korfel Withrethin|the Gentleman]], reached through [[Father Dwondaff Pierce]]. Details and current state: [[Characters/Factions/Exandria/Gentleman's Organization]].

> - [ ] She kept some old Volstrucker contacts who did not know her current allegiance. Her knowledge of Volstrucker operations and methods is a security risk to the Empire if it ever surfaces

> [!note]- Background
> Born near Rexxentrum. Recruited young into the Volstrucker (Cerberus Assembly's secret police); became one of their top agents specializing in espionage and assassination. Growing disillusionment set in as she discovered missions served political vendettas rather than Empire security—the final straw was learning one of her successful assassinations was orchestrated to cover Assembly corruption.
>
> Tasked with assassinating [[Vaud Qalix - DM]]. Spent months infiltrating his organization, but was captured during the attempt. Qalix offered her a position instead of execution. During her surveillance she'd observed how he treated followers—with respect, clear purpose, and genuine recognition. She saw the offer as an opportunity for the autonomy the Volstrucker never provided. Her loyalty is genuine but pragmatic: based on respect and opportunity, not true belief in his cause.

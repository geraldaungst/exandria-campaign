## Description & Roleplay

![[eledyr-dephar.jpeg|right|300]]

A 24-year-old half-elf, pale with a slight bluish undertone, silver-white hair in a long loose ponytail and piercing emerald eyes. He wears dark, practical clothes: a black leather tunic with silver embroidery, dark breeches and high boots, with hidden pockets and sheaths for tools and blades. He carries an intricately worked silver dagger, a gift from [[Aelorin Nightshade]] marking his place in the Clasp's inner circle.

- **Voice:** Smooth and calculating, with measured precision. Academic terms in his scholar's cover; street cant as a Clasp man.
- **Mannerisms:**
    - Touches the silver dagger without noticing when he feels threatened or challenged
    - Catalogues everyone he meets for possible usefulness
    - A different posture for each identity: the scholar's stoop, the operative's confident stance
- **Key traits:**
    - Cunning, ambitious and cold; very intelligent and observant, good at finding secrets and using them
    - Never forgets a slight, and holds grudges longer than is wise
    - Keeps his loyalties in compartments and can serve two masters sincerely
    - Underestimates opponents out of intellectual arrogance; more and more afraid his double loyalty will be exposed

## Current Situation

In Zadash, about a week ahead of the party. Aelorin posted him there at his own request to find out how badly last winter's crackdown weakened the Zadash Myriad, and whether the Clasp can push in or buy allies. He hasn't met the Gentleman and won't walk into the Evening Nip: this is reconnaissance. By day he researches old prophecies at the Valley Archive, which is his cover and also real work for Valen. By night he buys drinks for Myriad runners and asks who was taken in the crackdown and who came back. The questions are reaching [[Korfel Withrethin|Korfel]]'s people ([[Korfel's Next Moves]]).

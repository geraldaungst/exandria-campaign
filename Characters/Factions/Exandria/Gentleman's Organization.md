## Quick Reference

**Base:** beneath the Evening Nip tavern in [[Zadash - DM Notes|Zadash]], with tunnels and forgotten passages under the city
**Goal:** control of Zadash's underworld and as much of its government as can be bought
**Status:** recovering from the crackdown of winter 834–835 under a Gentleman who rules harder than the last
**Influence:** strong in the underworld; rebuilding in the tax offices, courts and lower Righteous Brand ranks

## Leadership

**Current Leader:** [[Korfel Withrethin]], as the Gentleman, since the crackdown. His people believe he is the same Gentleman as before

**Notable Members:**
- [[Cree Deeproots]]: blood magic; runs the [[Blood Sample Collection Protocol]]. Hired after the crackdown
- Sorah: goliath enforcer and bodyguard
- Kutha: 11-foot ogre, muscle
- The handler: receives and re-codes Qalix's network traffic in Zadash. A DM mechanism, not an NPC ([[Qalix's Network]])
- [[Father Dwondaff Pierce]]: contact in Port Damali, by courier only; knows only "the Gentleman"

## Culture & Methods

- Respectable surface; merchant fronts launder the money
- **Operations:** smuggling through the tunnels; protection rackets; information brokering through informants across the city; black-market trade in artifacts, stolen goods and contraband; bribery
- **Control:** blood samples from every associate and partner; blackmail on officials; violence when negotiation fails, arranged so it can't be traced
- **Since the crackdown:** less elegance, more force. Fewer people see the Gentleman, and those who do give blood first

## Relationships

**Allies:** the wider [[Myriad - DM Notes|Myriad]]. Zadash runs with wide independence and owes a share; other bosses deal with the Gentleman by letter and believe he survived the crackdown
**Rivals:** Myriad factions that sense weakness since the crackdown; Crownsguard and Righteous Brand officers who can't be bought
**Neutral:** [[Vaud Qalix - DM Notes|Qalix]]'s network, a paying client

## Current Activities

### The Rexxentrum Job

- After Korfel took the seat, [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM Notes|Dreyara]] recruited the Gentleman for [[Vaud Qalix - DM Notes|Qalix]]'s planned heist of the [[Cobalt Soul Archive in Rexxentrum|Rexxentrum Archive]] (Shard 5). His tunnels and smuggling routes were to move a crew into Rexxentrum and carry the shard out through Zadash. The crackdown had cost him people and money, so he took a risky job for a foreign employer
- The contact ran through [[Father Dwondaff Pierce]] in Port Damali
- Since Dreyara's death he holds Qalix's down payment and a half-planned job. His network told him of her death on 27–28 Thunsheer 835, with descriptions of three of her killers; his people are watching for them. Whether Qalix sends someone to finish the job, and who, is open. See [[Qalix's Network]]
- If asked at the table: "She was hiring for a job up north."

### Plans

- Rebuy the officials the crackdown cost him
- Push deeper into Zadash's government and out along the trade routes

## History

- **The first Gentleman:** Babenon Dosal, a water genasi who rarely let himself be seen; only his lieutenants knew his face. Under him the Myriad ran Zadash's underworld from beneath the Evening Nip for about two years and came to hold half the city's real power. The tales the party has heard come from this time
- **Korfel arrives** about 2–3 years ago, after the [[Port Damali Murders]], under a merchant alias, with money and a merchant's skills. The Myriad used him as a money man: his fronts laundered their income, and he kept the bribe ledger
- **The crackdown (winter 834–835):** a lieutenant plotted to assassinate a starosta. Korfel learned of it and sent the Crownsguard an anonymous tip, as he once did to [[Rylan Estevez]]. The sweep took most of the lieutenants. Babenon fled into the tunnels, where Korfel, who knew the safe routes and held the money, killed him. The body was never found
- **The new Gentleman:** Korfel came out with the treasury and the ledger, the one man who could keep the bribes flowing. He gave orders for the Gentleman, then as the Gentleman. The money man's alias vanished with the crackdown; people assume the Crownsguard took him or he fled. His new inner circle never saw Babenon; he appears to them as himself
- **What the street says:** the Gentleman vanished into the tunnels and came back changed and more brutal. The genasi detail, the glowing eyes and the voices in empty corners drifted in the retelling ([[The Gentleman]], Player Wiki)

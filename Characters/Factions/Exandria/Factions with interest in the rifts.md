[[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]]

- Trying to seal the rifts with the Lorestone.
[[Malachite Cord - DM]]
- Trying to stop the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] from assembling the Lorestone, which they think will cause further catastrophe. (They are partly correct--the Lorestone alone will indeed cause catastrophe.)
- Trying to find and cure the corruption in the Savalirwood and think it is spreading across Exandria via the rifts. (They are correct.)
- Knows Valen Elderguard leads the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]] and that they partnered with the Echoforge ([[Session 19 - Interrogating Radelia|Session 19]]). Does not know [[Vaud Qalix - DM]] by name or the Inkwell.
[[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]]
- Want to find a way to harness the power of the rifts for their own use. Originally directly opposed to the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]], in recent years they have begun trying to work together.
- A splinter cell within the group, led by [[Valen Elderguard]] and financed by [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Vaud Qalix]] is opposed to this partnership and is secretly working to undermine the work.

# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Cobalt Soul]]

**Base:** [[Rexxentrum - DM|Rexxentrum]]; the order coordinates its archives from there
**Goal:** acquire, preserve and share knowledge in the service of Ioun
**Status:** its Rexxentrum Archive holds [[Shard 5 - Rexxentrum Shard|Shard 5]] in an unauthorized, warded vault, for a secret research project run by [[Archivist Ovedo]]
**Influence:** archives in major cities: Rexxentrum, the Valley Archive in Zadash, and [[Cobalt Soul Archive in Port Damali - DM|Port Damali]]. It operates openly in the Empire

# Leadership
**Current Leader:** none named

**Notable Members:**
- [[Archivist Ovedo]]: Rexxentrum Archive. Holds Shard 5 and has chosen not to tell the Obsidian Echoforge

# Culture & Methods
- Order devoted to Ioun; each archive is a library and a temple
- Open to anyone seeking knowledge. Some collections need special approval; visitors may be escorted by a monk, and nothing leaves

# Relationships
**Allies:** none in fact. [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] says publicly that he works with the order and courts it at the national level, but he would steal from it if that served him. He expects the theft cannot be traced to him
**Rivals:** none set
**Neutral:** the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM|Obsidian Echoforge]], who believe the Stonefoot Compass points to Shard 5. It points to Shard 7. If the party builds a relationship with the order or Ovedo, [[Lyren Willowwhisper]] may push to take the shard by theft or political pressure ([[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]])

# Current Activities
- Ovedo is studying the prophecy text on Shard 5
- [[Varnes Dwell - DM|Varnes Dwell]] found the vault on a security assessment and was paid to keep quiet. He told the party on 19 Thunsheer 835 ([[Session 38 - What to Do with Lady Emer?|Session 38]])
- [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] sent Dreyara to steal the shard; she died before reaching Rexxentrum

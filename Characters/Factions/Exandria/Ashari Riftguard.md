**Base:** none named. The Riftguard works in Wildemount, because the new rifts will most likely occur in Xhorhas
**Goal:** long-term, prevent the rifts from forming and stop the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]] from constructing a permanent door. Medium-term, research the [[The Prophecy|Prophecy]]
**Status:** four Riftguard members are in Wildemount, one from each Ashari tribe. They are not allied with anyone yet
**Influence:** small. The four tribes formed an alliance to send the Riftguard

# Culture & Methods
The Ashari are extremely concerned about the potential for opening new rifts. They are adamantly opposed to the idea of a permanent door.

These four Ashari represent a microcosm of the world's elements and reflect the Ashari's holistic approach to maintaining the balance between the planes. They each bring unique perspectives and abilities to the group, making them a formidable force against the chaotic energies threatening to upset the natural order.

# Relationships
**Allies:** none yet. The [[Malachite Cord - DM|Malachite Cord]]'s note lists the Ashari as allies (see Open Issues)
**Rivals:** the Emissaries, whose permanent door they oppose
**Neutral:** the Ashari are not allied with "either of the other two groups (yet)" because they do not have enough information to know whether [[Aveqtaro Thaan]] is sincere. The original note does not name the two groups (see Open Issues)

# Current Activities
Though the Ashari are rarely found in Wildemount, since the rifts they guard are not there, they have learned that the new rifts will most likely occur in Xhorhas. Members of each of the Ashari tribes formed an alliance, and a small group known as the Riftguard was sent to Wildemount to research the [[The Prophecy|prophecy]] and find out how to prevent the rifts from forming.

# History
The Ashari were the people who arose to seal the rifts formed at the last eclipse.

# Quick Reference
**Base:** [[Aelorin's Headquarters - DM|Aelorin's Headquarters]], beneath a tailor shop in [[Port Damali - DM|Port Damali]]. The guild began in Tal'Dorei and keeps hideouts in its cities
**Goal:** not set. It works as a guild for hire: thieves, assassins, black-market dealers and contract brokers
**Status:** the Port Damali cell is under Aelorin. He threw Qilynn out after her failed final mission
**Influence:** hideouts across Tal'Dorei; in Port Damali the Myriad is the dominant syndicate

# Leadership
**Current Leader:** none named. [[Aelorin Nightshade]] is the senior figure in Port Damali

**Notable Members:**
- [[Aelorin Nightshade]]: runs the Port Damali cell; Qilynn's mentor and trainer
- [[Yrene Shadowthorn]]: one of his most trusted operatives
- [[Calderax Dunhall]]: Aelorin's recent mentee, recently promoted. He reported Shard 6 to the Obsidian Echoforge, not to Aelorin
- [[Qilynn Duskwhisper - Nicole|Qilynn Duskwhisper]] (PC): trained in the Clasp under Aelorin. She does not suspect him of the setup ([[Qilynn Duskwhisper's Revenge]])

# Culture & Methods
- Built on the remains of the old Winksman Thieves Guild
- Members saved many lives in the Chroma Conclave's attack on Emon
- Port Damali HQ: tailor-shop front, guard post, operatives' quarters and Aelorin's chamber ([[Aelorin's Headquarters - DM]])

# Relationships
**Allies:** none
**Rivals:** the [[Myriad - DM|Myriad]]: sporadic gang wars in Emon. In Port Damali the Myriad is dominant; Evaldo Ide, the tailor who fronts the HQ, is secretly a Myriad ally cooperating with Aelorin
**Neutral:**

- Aelorin's reaction to the failed heist is in [[Aelorin Nightshade]]

## Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Obsidian Echoforge]]

**Base:** a permanent collection and workshop on the outskirts of [[Palma Flora]] on the Menagerie Coast, where [[Lyren Willowwhisper]] keeps Shards 1 to 4
**Goal:** recover every shard of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM|Lorestone of Eryndor]], reassemble it, and use it to close the rifts. They don't know it needs a [[Luxon Beacon Integration with Lorestone|Luxon Beacon]] to work safely
**Status:** active. Holds Shards 1 to 4, knows Shard 6 was stolen, and expects the Stonefoot Compass lead to bring a fifth (it points to Shard 7). Publicly partnered with the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries of the Sunfall]]. Party standing: [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor]] hired the party to recover the [[Stonefoot Compass - DM|Stonefoot Compass]]. They found it and kept it without telling him, and his deadline passed on 8 Thunsheer 835. His response is still to come, and the compass and Keldar's map are the lead to Shard 7 ([[Aethor Kalisk's Secret Mission]], [[Search for the Stonefoot Compass]])
**Influence:** regional. Field agents, a permanent facility and archives four centuries deep

## Leadership

**Current Leader:** [[Lyren Willowwhisper]], at Palma Flora. Her aunt [[Veyda Willowwhisper]], the former leader, advises her. Caelum Silverstride's faction argued for merit over bloodline when Lyren was named; it is quiet but watching. The Willowwhisper line has led the order since its founding ([[Legacy of the Seekers]])

**Notable Members:**
- [x] [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor Kalisk]]: gnome artificer and the order's lead researcher on arcane refraction, working in [[Port Damali - DM|Port Damali]]. [[Session 8]]
- [ ] [[Gaius Emberfell]]: dwarf artificer at Palma Flora, building a device to disperse the arcane energy of the rifts
- [ ] [[Tessa Sunwarden]]: aasimar field agent tracking anomalies and materials for Gaius's device. Old academy rival of [[Sumad-Dekon Harrowgate]] of the Emissaries
- [x] Associate: [[Calderax Dunhall]], a Clasp operative who brings the order leads. He told them about Shard 6. [[Session 5 - Tracking Yrene|Session 5]]

## Culture & Methods

- [ ] Each fragment found is documented and studied, and its text is shared within the order
- [ ] Recovered fragments are guarded closely. Since the Massacre, security is distributed and fragment locations are secret
- [ ] Members train as both scholars and explorers
- [ ] Arcane methods for finding fragments, from Sylas Willowwhisper's detection work to Aethor's refraction device
- [x] They believe the complete prophecy will guide Exandria's future, and that the reassembled Lorestone can close the rifts. [[Session 7]]
- [ ] They believe the Lorestone itself is the prophecy's "beacon" and will close the rifts on its own. Used without a Luxon Beacon, it fails catastrophically
- [x] The order was first called the Seekers of Eryndor's Echo. Its later names are in [[Legacy of the Seekers]]. [[Session 7]]
- Symbol: obsidian disc fragments

## Relationships

**Allies:** the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries of the Sunfall]], in public, since the Emissaries' moderates opened talks. Both want the Lorestone assembled; they part ways on what to do with it. The Echoforge does not know about [[Valen Elderguard]]'s Inkwell
- [ ] The party told Aethor that Dreyara, who stole Shard 6, had named Valen and "the Eye" ([[Session 29]]). What the order makes of that is open (Open Issues)

**Rivals:** the [[Malachite Cord - DM|Malachite Cord]]. The two once stood on common ground and have divided over the rifts: the Echoforge seeks mastery, the Cord harmony. They still confer when the rifts require it. Lyren sees the Cord as well-meaning obstructionists whose caution will get people killed. Also whoever is stealing shards: Dreyara took Shard 6 for an employer the party linked to "the Eye" of Xarzith Kitril
**Neutral:** the [[Cobalt Soul - DM|Cobalt Soul]]. Aethor researches in its Port Damali library. The order does not know [[Archivist Ovedo]] holds Shard 5

## History

- Founded about 400 years ago by [[Lys Willowwhisper]] as the Seekers of Eryndor's Echo, after she found the Nightshade Shard. Leaders, names and achievements: [[Legacy of the Seekers]]
- [[The Massacre]] (785 PD): the Emissaries attacked a gathering where the order expected to assemble a large part of the Lorestone. Members died, the gathered shards were scattered, and the order took its current name and made protection part of its mission
- [[Veyda Willowwhisper]] built the Palma Flora facility. She was lost on an expedition in the Shattered Teeth; Lyren was named leader in her absence. Veyda returned months later, endorsed Lyren and became her advisor
- [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|Aethor]] was on the skyship *Unshaken*, the target of the Inkwell's skyjacking ([[Session 1 - The Skyship|Sessions 1 to 3]]). The party learned of the order at a Port Damali library ([[Session 7]]) and met Aethor at the Pudgy Pigeon ([[Session 8]])

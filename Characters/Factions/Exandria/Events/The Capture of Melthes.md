## The Capture of Melthes

**Timeline:** Approximately 833 PD (about a year after [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] was sold)

**Location of Recruitment:** [[Xarzith Kitril - DM]]

**Location of Processing:** [[Cerberus Assembly - DM]] research facility in [[Rexxentrum - DM]]

**Agents Involved:** Two Volstrucker agents (one was a former client of Hesterian's)

**Assembly Personnel:** [[Isolene Fenzana]] (researcher), [[Cree Deeproots]] (assessment)

### The Recruitment

The Assembly, realizing the potential of such a unique individual, used a blend of persuasion and payment to convince [[Melthes]]'s parents that he required special care only they could provide. They promised his parents that he would be trained to harness his abilities for the greater good, while secretly intending to use him as a key asset in their exploration and exploitation of Feywild connections.

**Investigation Process:**

- [ ] Volstrucker agents spent approximately one week investigating in Xarzith Kitril
- [ ] Acting on a tip from a local informant
- [ ] Successfully convinced parents to release Melthes to Assembly custody

### The Assessment

Upon arrival at the Assembly facility in [[Rexxentrum - DM]], [[Melthes]] (then 16 years old) underwent magical assessment by [[Cree Deeproots]].

**Assessment Results:**

- [ ] Confirmed unique Feywild/Feydark connection abilities
- [ ] Distinctive Feywild magical resonance, unlike typical magic users
- [ ] Recommended for advanced study under [[Isolene Fenzana]]'s research division

**Behavioral Observations:**

- [ ] Subject was terrified
- [ ] Repeatedly asked about his family and when he could go home
- [ ] Showed evidence of close family bonds

### Assembly's Intent

**Usefulness to Assembly:** Melthes's abilities make him a valuable asset in:

- [ ] Navigating and manipulating the Feywild and its energies
- [ ] Expanding Assembly's magical prowess and planar understanding
- [ ] Political maneuvering via Feydark connections
- [ ] Offering insights or leverage in dealings with fey entities

**Current Status:** Unknown - Assembly compartmentalizes information

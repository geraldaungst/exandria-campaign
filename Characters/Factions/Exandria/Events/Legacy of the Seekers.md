# Core Information

## Historical Span

Over 400 years of organizational history spanning multiple generations and name changes, representing the longest continuous effort to recover and preserve the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]] [[Lorestone Shard Status Tracker|fragments]].

## Key Accomplishments

**Exploration and Recovery:**

- Exploration of stormy seas in search of fragments
- Battles with sea creatures guarding or near fragment locations
- Decipherment of ancient clues and riddles
- Progressive recovery of prophecy fragments over centuries
- Development of detection methods for identifying fragments

**Organizational Growth:**

- Building a network of dedicated followers beyond the founding Willowwhisper family
- Establishment of permanent facilities and archives
- Development of scholarly and exploration training programs
- Creation of fragment preservation techniques

**Cultural Development:**

- Each discovered fragment treated as a symbol of hope and progress
- Documentation and interpretation traditions established
- Sharing of prophecy contents within the organization
- Strong emphasis on preservation alongside discovery

## Name Evolution

The organization went through five name changes, each reflecting shifts in mission or philosophy:

1. **Seekers of [[Eryndor]]'s Echo** - Original name under founder [[Lys Willowwhisper]]
2. **[[Eryndor]] Echo Seekers** - Streamlined version
3. **Echo Seekers** - Adopted to reflect universal importance beyond Eryndor's legacy
4. **Echo's Pursuit** - Emphasized gravity and formality of purpose
5. **Obsidian Echoforge** - Current name (post-[[The Massacre|Massacre]]), representing mission to reconstruct the prophecy
    - "Obsidian" for the disc material
    - "Echo" for Eryndor's wisdom
    - "Forge" for restoration and reconstruction

## Founding Figure

**[[Lys Willowwhisper]]** - Founder and originator of the quest to recover the shattered Lorestone fragments. Established the philosophical foundation and initial methodology that guided the organization for centuries.

## Legacy Impact

The centuries-long effort created:

- A robust organizational culture focused on preservation
- Extensive networks across multiple regions
- Deep knowledge of ancient languages and prophecies
- Specialized skills in fragment detection and authentication
- Strong family tradition (Willowwhisper family leadership)

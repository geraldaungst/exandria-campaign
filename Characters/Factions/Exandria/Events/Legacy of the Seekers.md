## Legacy of the Seekers

The history of the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM|Obsidian Echoforge]], from its founding by [[Lys Willowwhisper]] about 400 years ago to today: the longest continuous effort to recover the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM|Lorestone of Eryndor]]'s [[Lorestone Shard Status Tracker|fragments]]. The Willowwhisper family has led it from the start, through several generations and six names.

### Names

Each change reflects a shift in mission or philosophy:

1. **Seekers of Eryndor's Echo**: the original name under [[Lys Willowwhisper]]
2. **Eryndor Echo Seekers**: a streamlined version
3. **Echo Seekers**: adopted to reflect a mission that mattered beyond [[Eryndor]]'s legacy
4. **Echo's Pursuit**: emphasized the gravity and formality of the purpose
5. **Windwhisper Guardians**: Caelan's name after the Massacre, when protection joined the mission. It did not last
6. **Obsidian Echoforge**: the current name, taken under Naela. "Obsidian" for the disc, "Echo" for Eryndor's wisdom, "Forge" for restoration and reconstruction

### What four centuries built

- Exploration of stormy seas, battles with sea creatures guarding or near fragments, and the deciphering of ancient clues and riddles
- Recovery of fragments over the centuries, and methods for detecting and authenticating them
- A network of followers beyond the Willowwhisper family, permanent facilities and archives, and training in both scholarship and exploration
- Preservation techniques, and a culture that values preservation as much as discovery
- Traditions of documenting and interpreting each fragment and sharing its text within the order; each discovery is treated as a sign of hope and progress
- Deep knowledge of ancient languages and prophecies, and networks across several regions

## Core Information

### The Event

A devastating assault in 809 PD by the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]] on an [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] gathering. This attack occurred during a meeting where Echoforge members believed they would complete assembly of a major portion of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]].

### Outcomes

**Immediate Results:**

- Loss of several key Obsidian Echoforge members
- Previously gathered disc shards were scattered
- Multiple casualties on both sides
- Forced reorganization of the Obsidian Echoforge
- Organization renamed to its current identity

**Organizational Changes:**

- Shift in organizational focus to include active protection of fragments (not just collection)
- Implementation of distributed security measures
- Development of defensive protocols
- Increased secrecy around fragment locations

**Political Ramifications:**

- Creation of lasting enmity between the two organizations
- Significant internal strife within Emissaries of the Sunfall
    - Many members opposed the violence
    - Some members left the organization
    - Created lasting divide between moderates and extremists within the Emissaries

### Leadership Context

#### Emissaries Leadership

The attack was ordered by **Neris Solbane** (then-leader of the Emissaries of the Sunfall), who believed the Echoforge was close to assembling major artifacts. This decision was based on intelligence about artifact assembly progress.

#### Post-Massacre Evolution

- Initial period of open hostility between factions
- Under Talrek Sunshadow's leadership, Emissaries shifted to covert operations
- [[Valen Elderguard]]'s current leadership maintains public peace initiatives while secretly continuing opposition

### Historical Significance

This event represents a defining moment in both organizations' histories and fundamentally altered the relationship between the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] and [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]], transforming them from rivals into enemies.

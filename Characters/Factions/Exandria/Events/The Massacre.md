## The Massacre

In 785 PD the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries of the Sunfall]] attacked a gathering of the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM|Obsidian Echoforge]] at which the Echoforge expected to assemble a major portion of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM|Lorestone of Eryndor]]. The Emissaries' leader, Neris Solbane, ordered the attack on intelligence that the Echoforge was close to assembling it. The event turned two rival orders into enemies.

None of today's leaders took part: [[Lyren Willowwhisper]] was not yet born and [[Valen Elderguard]] was a child. They inherited the feud.

### Outcomes

**Immediate results:**

- Several key Echoforge members were killed, with casualties on both sides
- The shards gathered for the meeting were scattered

**For the Echoforge:**

- Caelan Willowwhisper reorganized the order and set up protection protocols. Protecting fragments, not only collecting them, became part of its mission
- Security was distributed, defensive protocols written, and fragment locations kept secret
- The order took its current name, Obsidian Echoforge ([[Legacy of the Seekers]])

**For the Emissaries:**

- Many members opposed the violence and some left. The order split into moderates and hardliners, and the split forced leadership changes
- Open hostility at first. Under Talrek Sunshadow the Emissaries turned to covert operations
- Late in Talrek's leadership the moderates who had stayed opened talks with the Echoforge, and the two orders became partners
- [[Valen Elderguard]] keeps the partnership in public. In secret, his Inkwell works to return the order to Neris's methods ([[The Inkwell]])

## Source

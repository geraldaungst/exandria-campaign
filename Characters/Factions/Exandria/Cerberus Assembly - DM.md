# Quick Reference
**Base:** [[Rexxentrum - DM]], with a research facility in the city
**Goal:** research the origin of the [[Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing|cataclysm]] that ended [[Ruins of Molaesmyr - DM|Molaesmyr]], and the exploration and exploitation of Feywild connections
**Status:** holds [[Melthes]] in custody since about 833 PD. His current condition is unknown
**Influence:** works within the [[Dwendalian Empire - DM|Dwendalian Empire]]

# Leadership
**Current Leader:** none named. Ludinus Da'leth is the Assembly figure the [[Ring of Three]] approached

**Notable Members:**
- Ludinus Da'leth
- [[Isolene Fenzana]]: researcher. Melthes was recommended for advanced study under her research division
- [[Cree Deeproots]]: assessed Melthes for the Assembly

# Culture & Methods
- Recruits with persuasion and payment: Melthes's parents were told he needed special care only the Assembly could give
- Uses Volstrucker agents to find and bring in recruits ([[The Capture of Melthes]])
- Compartmentalizes information

# Relationships
**Allies:** none set
**Rivals:** the [[Malachite Cord - DM|Malachite Cord]]: clash over magical research methods and artifact handling
**Neutral:** the [[Ring of Three]] approached Ludinus Da'leth for information about the cataclysm

# Current Activities
- Researching the origin of the Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing ([[Dwendalian Empire - DM]])
- Keeping Melthes. See [[Rescue Melthes]]

# History
- About 833 PD: took Melthes from [[Xarzith Kitril - DM]] ([[The Capture of Melthes]])

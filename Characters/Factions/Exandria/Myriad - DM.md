# Quick Reference

> [!quote]- What players know
> ![[Myriad]]

**Base:** no single seat. In Zadash, beneath the Evening Nip tavern ([[Characters/Factions/Exandria/Gentleman's Organization|Gentleman's Organization]]). Port Damali is a long-standing stronghold
**Goal:** money and control of city institutions through bribery, blackmail and debts of favor, not open violence
**Status:** the Zadash branch is recovering from the crackdown of winter 834–835 under Korfel; Port Damali's merchant guilds are mostly under Myriad control
**Influence:** the largest criminal syndicate in Wildemount, with a presence in its major cities and the Tal'Dorei coast (Stilben, Kymal)

# Leadership
**Current Leader:** none named above city level. Zadash: [[Korfel Withrethin]], as the Gentleman. Port Damali: not set

**Notable Members:**
- [[Korfel Withrethin]]: the Gentleman of Zadash
- [[Cree Deeproots]]: blood magic for the Zadash branch
- [[Father Dwondaff Pierce]]: the Gentleman's contact in Port Damali, by courier only
- Lord Gabriel Rymmer: manages the Exalted Collection Auction House in Port Damali; black-market access. Dreyara's target ([[Dreyara Drimvar - DM]])
- [[Ru'Shan Halrune]]: former Myriad rogue, now Qalix's head researcher

# Culture & Methods
- Began as a shipping company in Yrrosa that turned to smuggling
- Bribery, blackmail and debts of favor; violence when negotiation fails
- Zadash: smuggling through the old tunnels and sewers under the city
- Port Damali: control through the merchant guilds

# Relationships
**Allies:** [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] counts the Myriad as an ally (hub, Manifesto). Dreyara was still cultivating Port Damali contacts when she died. In Zadash the Gentleman is a paying partner for the Archive job ([[Qalix's Network]])
**Rivals:** the [[Clasp - DM|Clasp]]: sporadic gang wars in Emon. In Port Damali the Myriad dominates, yet Evaldo Ide, the tailor who fronts Aelorin's HQ, is secretly a Myriad ally cooperating with Aelorin ([[Aelorin's Headquarters - DM]])
**Neutral:** other bosses deal with the Gentleman by letter and believe he survived the crackdown

# Current Activities
- Zadash: recovery, the Rexxentrum job and rivals who sense weakness. Details in [[Characters/Factions/Exandria/Gentleman's Organization|Gentleman's Organization]] and [[Korfel's Next Moves]]
- [[Hesterian Shyr's Infiltration]]: Hesterian wants to infiltrate the Myriad and does not know it leads to Korfel

# History

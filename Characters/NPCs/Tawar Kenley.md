# Tawar Kenley

Last Edited: April 9, 2021 7:57 AM
Tags: NPC

![https://pbs.twimg.com/media/EAu4gFOXsAEy8ta.jpg](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/EAu4gFOXsAEy8ta.jpg)

80 year old half-orc, has been a member of the cult for decades. She is a loyal soldier, though harbors a very slight resentment that she hasn't been promoted higher. She appears elderly and fragile, but she is in fact quite powerful and agile.

*Medium (5'0") Half-Orc, Chaotic Evil (CR 7)*

**Armor Class 10**

**Hit Points 27**

**Attacks** Melee [[+5]];) / [[2d8+2]];), Ranged [[+3]];) / [[2d8+0]];), Grapple [[+2]];)

**Senses** Passive Perception 11

**Skills** Arcana [[+2]];), Sleight of Hand [[+3]];)

**Languages** Common, Orc

[[Tyranny of Dragons/Campaign Database/Untitled]]

This woman is lean and not particularly muscular. She wears light armors faded through time and improper care. She has brown hair. Judging by the bloodshot nature of her eyes and the massive bags underneath them one would imagine she has not slept for days.

**Personality:** She loves telling others what to do and how they should feel about things. As she has aged she has sought to do reparations for her actions as a youth, but she makes questionable choices about how to do so—her ethics are distorted by her adherence to the cult. She has a magnified sense of fairness and tries to make sure that others who never received punishment for their crimes are brought to justice. Even so, she seems unaware that her own actions are usually deserving of the punishments she metes out on others.

**History:** She was born a particularly ugly child, much to the horror of her parents. At a young age both her parents were killed in an accident. Her uncle (a powerful person with enemies on both sides of the law) took her in and had her trained in basic self defense.

**Motivation:** She has decided she is irredeemable.

**Ideals:** Entrepeneur., Opinionated, Justice. **Flaws:** Antagonistic., Ugly, Insomniac. **Bonds:** Poor, Religious, Adventurer., Criminal record. **Occupation:** Hunter for the cult caravans, supplying needed food.

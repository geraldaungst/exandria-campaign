## Talking with Korfel

Lines for the Evening Nip meetings, grouped by what just happened at the table. Pick one and read it. DM only. Terms and triggers: [[Korfel's Next Moves]].

### Meeting 1: Cree's interview

- [ ] **Opening:** "Sit. This won't take long, and it's the same for everyone."
- [ ] **Blood:** "One drop each. The Gentleman gives his own to his partners. It's how trust is kept down here."
- [ ] **Refused:** "Then we're finished. The stairs are behind you."
- [ ] **Questions:** "Who sent you?" / "The woman outside the monastery. Tell me how she died." / "What do you want in Zadash?"
- [ ] **If the party has been asking after Korfel Withrethin:** "Someone in your company has been asking after a man named Withrethin. Names are the Gentleman's trade. Who is he to you?" *(She notes who answers, who looks at whom, and who goes quiet.)*
- [ ] **Asked about Korfel Withrethin here:** "I don't know the name." *(She writes it down.)*
- [ ] **Asked to see the Gentleman now:** "He'll send for you. Not before he knows who you are."
- [ ] **Close:** "Go back to your rooms. You'll be fetched."

### Meeting 2: the flooded hall

**Entrance**

> The passage opens onto a long hall, cold and damp. A channel of black water runs its full width, fed by a thin fall of water from an opening high in the far wall. Beyond the channel, on a raised dais, a man sits at a table with his back to you. A woman in a green silk gown stands at his elbow. Crossbows rest on the rails of galleries on either side. The goliath and the ogre take their places on your side of the water.

- [ ] **Cree:** "You'll forgive the distance. We find it keeps conversations civil."
- [ ] *(The woman pours his wine. He kisses her hand, says something too low to hear, and she leaves by a door behind the dais. His back stays to the party.)*
- [ ] **Korfel, back still turned:** "You cost me a client."
- [ ] **If the party has been asking after his name:** "And I'm told one of you has been asking after a man named Withrethin. An unusual name. I'd like to know who wants him, and why."
- [ ] *(Turning, glass in hand:)* "Something to drink? It's from the Menagerie—"

**The turn**

> He is human, in his early forties, in a tailored coat with silver at the cuffs, his hair carefully kept.

- [ ] **Passive Perception 15:** the color leaves his face. A heartbeat later his expression is smooth again. This happens even if the party asked after his name: he expected someone from his past, not her
- [ ] **Whisper to Hesterian's player only:** "You know this face. It registers immediately. The man who murdered you. Korfel Withrethin." Then straight back to the table

**His priorities, in order:** his people must not hear his name tied to Port Damali; no fight in this hall; get her alone across the water; find out who else knows.

### What Hesterian or the party does next

**She keeps her composure**
- [ ] *(Finishing:)* "…Menagerie Coast. I find it travels better than most people." His eyes stay on her a beat too long
- [ ] "And your companion. I don't believe Cree gave me your name." *(Given an alias:)* "[Alias]. Of course."
- [ ] "There's a matter I'd rather discuss without an audience. Leave us. Sorah, the door."

**She says his name**
- [ ] "A name I don't know, from a town I've never seen. You must tell me about him sometime."
- [ ] "You have me confused with someone. It happens. I have that kind of face."
- [ ] Then he clears the floor.

**She accuses him in front of everyone** (murder, Port Damali)
- [ ] "Half this city claims I've killed them. You're the first to come back and complain." *(His people laugh.)*
- [ ] "Port Damali? I've never cared for sea air."
- [ ] "Leave us, all of you. The lady and I have a misunderstanding to settle."

**The party notices her reaction and asks**
- [ ] *(Answering before she can:)* "The water affects some people this way. Give her a moment."
- [ ] "Some faces stay with you. Isn't that right?"

**Someone else takes over the talking**
- [ ] "Finally. Someone who came to talk." His eyes keep going back to Hesterian
- [ ] "Your friend seems unwell. Perhaps you'll speak for her."

**A weapon comes out, or someone moves toward the water**
- [ ] *(The crossbows rise. Sorah steps forward. Korfel stays seated.)* "Think carefully about the next three seconds."
- [ ] "You came here by invitation. You'll leave the same way, or not at all."
- [ ] If they keep going: see "If they attack."

**She walks out**
- [ ] *(To his people:)* "Let her go. No one follows her."
- [ ] "Your friend has somewhere to be. We'll continue another time."
- [ ] **That night, an unsigned note at her lodging:** "There is a stable boy in Port Damali who need not die in a cell. Come back across the water and we will discuss him. Alone, or with whoever already knows."

**Clearing the floor** (common to most branches)
- [ ] "Leave us. Sorah, the door." Cree, Sorah and Kutha go to the far doors. The galleries keep their crossbows but are out of earshot over the falling water.

### Across the water (private)

- [ ] "They said you died in that room. I'd like to know how you're standing in mine."
- [ ] "Who have you told? Think carefully. It matters more to them than to you."
- [ ] "I paid for loyalty. I was sold a lie."
- [ ] **Playing the frightened man:** "I was a frightened man in a strange city. I am not that man now."
- [ ] Then his first reach: Rylan (below)

### Offers

**Rylan (his first reach)**
- [ ] "There's a boy in a Port Damali cell. Rylan, I believe. I can have him walking free by the end of the season."
- [ ] "A letter in his own hand will reach you within the month, with something of his you'll recognize. The door opens when our business up north is done." *(The courier's letter arrives with Xanaphia's leather bracelet, engraved "XL", folded inside.)*
- [ ] "Free, not innocent. Innocence would need a confession, and I'm not in a confessing mood."
- [ ] "You want him out. I want my name left where I buried it. Those two things fit together very neatly."

**Shard 5 (only if they showed interest in Rexxentrum)**
- [ ] "You're going to Rexxentrum. So was Dreyara. I have half her plan, all of her routes, and no one to walk them."
- [ ] "There are ways into the Archive the Cobalt Soul doesn't know about. I paid for every one of them."
- [ ] "If I'm dead, the job doesn't die with me. Someone finishes it, and what you want goes east."

**Silence**
- [ ] "People are asking about you. The ones who paid Dreyara would very much like your names."
- [ ] "Every word that leaves Zadash for the east passes through rooms I own. What they hear about you is my decision."
- [ ] "Kill me, and my people sell everything they have on you by morning. They're very thorough."

**Melthes (only after Cree reports the name)**
- [ ] "My specialist once worked for the Cerberus Assembly. She remembers a boy named Melthes. Frightened. Kept asking for his family."
- [ ] "What she remembers is mine to share. Or not."

**Information and rivals**
- [ ] "I found you in an afternoon. Imagine what I could find for you in a week."
- [ ] "There are men in this city who would like this chair. Help me keep it, and you'll find I remember my friends."

### Quiet threats

- [ ] **Blood:** "Cree has a drop of each of you. Ten miles is a large city. It's a very small hiding place."
- [ ] **Crownsguard:** "The officer who opened the gate for you has three children and a salary that doesn't cover them. I cover them."
- [ ] **People they care about:** "You have friends in this city. You'd be surprised how quickly I learn their names."
- [ ] **Qalix:** "I'd hate for your names to travel east without me."

### When they push back

- [ ] **"We'll expose you":** "Go ahead. Then explain to everyone in this hall why the man who feeds their families should hang on the word of a dead woman."
- [ ] **"Confess":** "I'll give you Rylan's freedom. I won't give you my neck. Don't confuse the two."
- [ ] **"We'll kill you":** "Many people have wanted to. The water is deep, and I know where it goes."
- [ ] **"You murdered me":** "And yet here you are, asking me for things."
- [ ] **They demand more:** "You have one coin to spend: your silence for mine. Spend it carefully."
- [ ] **They walk out:** "Take the night. The offer won't improve, but it won't vanish either."

### Closing

- [ ] "Then we understand each other. Cree will see you out."
- [ ] "Tomorrow, then. Use the front door. It's friendlier."

### If they attack

- [ ] *(Already rising)* "Disappointing." *(Into the water.)*

### The cell visit (Cree)

- [ ] "The Gentleman sends his regrets. The charges are real. The magistrate is reasonable, and reason costs money. The Gentleman has offered to pay."
- [ ] "He asks very little: your blood, the job up north, and your discretion."
- [ ] "Or you can wait for trial. I'm told the cells are dry this time of year."

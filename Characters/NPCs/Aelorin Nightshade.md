# Quick Reference
> [!info] Essential Details
> - **Location:** [[Aelorin's Headquarters - DM]], beneath a tailor shop in [[Port Damali - DM]]
> - **Goal:** Run the Port Damali cell of the [[Clasp - DM|Clasp]] and expand its influence (the Tumbledowns are next, per Calderax's letter)
> - **Attitude toward party:** Indifferent; he has had no dealings with them
> - **Key knowledge:** The Clasp's Port Damali operations. He believes the relic he sent Calderax for was only a rare relic

# Description & Roleplay
Tall high elf, [[Qilynn Duskwhisper - Nicole|Qilynn]]'s mentor and trainer. Qilynn's backstory calls him fiendish.

- **Voice:** Cold and level; delivers disappointment as fact.
- **Mannerisms:** Never lets Qilynn forget her mixed heritage; withholds training she hasn't earned in his eyes.
- **Key traits:** Rose from an orphan with no resources to a wealthy, powerful Clasp leader on his magical skill. Pragmatic about people: he uses talent where he finds it.

# Current Situation
- Threw Qilynn out after her unauthorised heist (see below).
- Recently got word of a rare and ancient relic and sent his new mentee [[Calderax Dunhall]] to pick it up. It was [[Shard 6 - Marquet Shard|Shard 6]]. Calderax reported it to the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]], who sent [[Aethor Kalisk - DM]] to get it, and the shard was then stolen from Calderax's room.
- Has attracted the attention of the Myriad and local rival gangs as his status and wealth grew.

## Reaction to the failed heist

- Unsurprised and cold, not angry or disappointed.
- Beat Qilynn, told her she was so pathetic that he was not surprised she failed so badly, and threw her out.
- Qilynn does not suspect him of the setup.

> [!secret]- Hidden Information
> - He does not know the relic was Shard 6, and does not know Calderax lost it.
> - Evaldo Ide, the tailor who fronts the HQ, is secretly a Myriad ally cooperating with him.

> [!note]- Background
> Aelorin was an orphan who grew up in the Driftwood Asylum. While there, he was often sent on "missions" by the cruel headmaster, Grankton. Eventually, he grew tired of the life there, and as a teenager he left on a mission and never returned. Grankton assumed he had died and never looked into it.
>
> Aelorin struggled to survive on his own until he connected with members of the Clasp who found his magical skills useful. As he established himself in the organization, he worked his way up, gaining status and wealth, and as a result, the attention of the Myriad and local rival gangs.

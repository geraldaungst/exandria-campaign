Dhazar Zarakas is the strongest of the gold dragons among Bahamut's seven Chosen. The Chosen are mistakenly assumed to be all gold, but only three are. Brew, the player of [[Drawg Stormbrew - Brew|Drawg]] and [[Elby Cinderdash - Brew|Elby]], supplied the details below and a character sheet: <https://www.dndbeyond.com/sheet-pdfs/brewdawggaming_116903175.pdf>

Use him if Bahamut should send a messenger who draws a reaction from Drawg or Elby.

- **Name:** pronounced "DAY-zar Za-RACK-us". His Draconic true name is given as "Dhrazzarkrakazaanon" in this note and as "Dhaorandaurzaras" in Drawg's backstory and Elby's note.
- **[[Drawg Stormbrew - Brew|Drawg]]:** a respectful but intense rivalry.
- **[[Elby Cinderdash - Brew|Elby]]:** generally dislikes Dhazar, and makes subtle insults that are lost on him.
- **Dhazar's view:** he can't comprehend an ally and "brother in arms" not appreciating his talent and skill. He considers Elby's triumph over him in the mandolin duel a fluke, and calls it Elby's "greatest victory, obviously".
- **Bahamut:** Elby's jibes are not lost on Bahamut, even when he finds them funny.

## Edhelri Lewel

Last Edited: May 1, 2021 9:48 PM

Tags: NPC

![https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/4228035/f05075f205e003a5f2c68ae13162567f.jpg](https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/4228035/f05075f205e003a5f2c68ae13162567f.jpg)

Lewel's wagon is loaded with exotic wood from the Jungle of Chult for the master carpenters and cabinetmakers of Waterdeep to turninto exquisite furniture. She is the exact opposite of Beyd Sechepol in temperament: impatient with people but exacting about her wagon and doting on her animals.

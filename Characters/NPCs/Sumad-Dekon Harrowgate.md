Sumad-Dekon (often called simply Sumad), a keen human wizard with a sharp mind for arcana and history, was recruited by [[Valen Elderguard]] for his controversial theories about the Age of Arcanum. His research into forbidden magics has led him to believe that the opening of the rifts is an opportunity, not a disaster waiting to happen. His hunger for knowledge and power has made him a valuable asset to the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]].

## Tessa Sunwarden

- Studied with [[Tessa Sunwarden]] at the Soltryce Academy in Rexxentrum. Both were top students who debated ancient texts; the partnership turned into unspoken romantic feelings, hidden behind academic rivalry.
- Her celestial heritage pulled her into field work with the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]]. His research into forbidden subjects pulled him away from her and to the Emissaries.
- Neither has confronted the feelings. Each is committed to an opposing side of the prophetic schism, and the tension shows whenever they meet.

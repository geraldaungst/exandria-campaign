# Aravax Foxtraveler

Last Edited: May 1, 2021 8:35 PM
Tags: NPC

![https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/3239401/326px_Elar.jpg](https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/3239401/326px_Elar.jpg)

Owner of a small roadhouse in the Outer City of Baldur’s Gate. Rumors in the city are that he is a cultist.

In fact he is not a cult member, but is a sympathizer and a beneficiary of their lavish spending, providing a safe house for the cult on their way through the city. He overcharges the cultists, but they are happy to pay.

He is extremely cautious spending his new wealth.

Aravax knows that the cult is gearing up for a large shipment on its way from the south and preparing a caravan to head north to Waterdeep. It will be difficult to get this information out of him—a large part of his reputation is based on his discretion—but he will not risk injury (let alone his life) or his wealth to keep the information secret, especially if there is low risk of anyone else finding out he gave it up.

That said, Aravax is as likely as not to give mixed or outright false information if he feels it's in his best interests to do so.

# Nyerhite Verther

Last Edited: May 2, 2021 8:01 AM
Tags: NPC

![https://i.pinimg.com/originals/51/e3/b3/51e3b3a38a96a0533e6171a1a9e5bafe.jpg](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/51/e3/b3/51e3b3a38a96a0533e6171a1a9e5bafe.jpg)

A load of Calishite silk will make Nyerhite Verther a rich man in Waterdeep, or so he belives. Sadly, he did not inspect his silk carefullt when he bought it, and it's infested with worms. If anyone spots them and points them out to Verther during the trip, he becomes unhinged in his anger and grief.

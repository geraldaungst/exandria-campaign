# Description & Roleplay
![[ovedo.webp|right|300]]

Archivist at the Rexxentrum Archive of the [[Cobalt Soul - DM]]. A nonbinary half-elf (they/them) with short pink hair with a rat tail, pale, almost luminescent skin and vivid green eyes. Lean and wiry from years of walking the Archive's stacks.

Owns an unusually fine hand-sized brass-and-glass focusing lens by a Xhorhasian artisan. The maker's mark is on the rim. Ovedo has no name for the maker and may mention the artisan if asked.

- **Voice:** Dry and unhurried.
- **Mannerisms:** Eyes narrow in concentration or flicker with mischief; works at night.
- **Key traits:**
  - Dry, sarcastic humor.
  - A neurological disorder gives them an eye for detail and an eidetic memory, and causes frequent insomnia.
  - Slow, methodical and persistent; used to getting what they want through relentless planning and preparation.
  - Polite and reserved in company, fiercely independent, and loyal to friends and allies.

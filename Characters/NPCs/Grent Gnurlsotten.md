# Grent Gnurlsotten

Proprietor of [[Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip]] and, by accident, the maker of the most sought-after cheese in Wildemount.

---

## At a Glance

**Voice:** Blunt, declarative, no flourishes. Says what he means and stops. Calls strangers "boy" or "little one," friends "friend." Gets genuinely, mildly annoyed about the nickname question every single time.

**Wants:** His goats safe. His cheese good. To be left alone about the nickname thing.

**Won't:** Let anyone into the cheese cave. Apologize gracefully. Admit he's famous.

---

## Species & Heritage

Goliath — notably short for his kind, because his mother was a dwarf and his father a goliath. He inherited his father's build, strength, and unmistakable goliath bearing, but his mother's stature.

At a glance most travelers assume he's a stout dwarf. Something nags at that assumption until it clicks that he's entirely hairless — no beard, no brow, goliaths lack body hair — and a closer look reveals the faint stone-mottled skin markings of his people.

**The reveal:** not a dwarf at all. Just a very small goliath.

## Appearance

Broad-shouldered, thick-armed, built like most goliaths *while seated*. The illusion breaks the moment he stands — barely taller than a stout human.

Skin pale grey-blue, mottled with faint darker patches like weathered stone. Moves with unmistakable goliath economy: deliberate, unhurried, powerful. That's exactly what throws people off. He looks *compressed*, like a much bigger person folded down.

Practical, well-worn clothes. An apron that's seen decades of cheese-work. Forearms that suggest he can still handle himself.

## The Nickname

Grent has no nickname, which is unusual for a goliath — their names are typically earned deed-names. This is a running local joke. Regulars needle newcomers with *"ask him about his nickname."*

> "Nickname? Why on Exandria would I need a nickname? Isn't my name good enough for ya? Too hard to remember? Don't want to bother?"

## Personality

Gruff but fair. Prickly about respect. Genuinely warm once trust is earned. Cares more about his goats and his cheese than his coin.

His anger is real but almost always bluster over something softer underneath — usually embarrassment. **He does not back down gracefully.** When cornered, he escalates rather than concedes, and the regulars know to give him a bigger, more public way out rather than asking him to admit fault.

## Wealth & Fame

**Wealth:** Quietly well-off. Plenty of gold saved, nowhere to spend it, no interest in flaunting it. A few visibly expensive items are scattered around the tavern, kept because he likes them, not to show off. A sharp eye would recognize their value; Grent won't volunteer it.

**Self-awareness of fame:** Partial. He knows people travel a long way for the cheese and isn't shy about charging a premium — but he doesn't really believe he's "world-famous," and visibly doesn't quite buy it when told.

## The Goats

Ten of them. His hobby, not his business, in his own mind — though the cheese depends entirely on them. See [[Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip]] for the full herd roster.

He is far more emotionally invested in the goats than in anything else he owns, and will react disproportionately to any threat to them.

## In a Fight

A goliath who's spent thirty years breaking up bar fights. Grapples, shoves, lifts people bodily, uses furniture. Never reaches for a weapon. Never does anything that looks like it's meant to seriously hurt.

Tavern brawls with newcomers are a tradition here, always nonlethal, always with the whole room watching and betting.

> [!important] Stat block — Gladiator, modified
> The **Gladiator** block is the right fit: it's built for fighting in front of a crowd, with showmanship baked in and no killing intent.
>
> **Strip the spear and shield entirely.** Everything is unarmed strikes, grapples, and shoves. Powerful Build gives him advantage on both grapple and shove contests.
>
> **Roughly double the hit points.** At CR 5 he won't last five rounds against a level 8-9 party piling in, and the fight needs room to breathe.
>
> **The crowd calling it is the end condition, not zero HP.** Don't run this to a knockout unless the table wants one.

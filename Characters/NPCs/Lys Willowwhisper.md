# Quick Reference
> [!info] Essential Details
> - **Status:** Deceased (founder, about 400 years ago)
> - **Achievement:** Founded the Seekers of Eryndor's Echo
> - **Legacy:** A 400+ year organizational tradition
> - **Discovery:** The first fragment of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]], [[Shard 1 - Nightshade Shard|Shard 1]], the "Shard of Willow's Legacy"

# Description
Firbolg scholar driven by dreams of a shimmering disc and echoing chants of prophecy. After years of research and piecing together fragmented tales and ancient maps, Lys led an expedition with a team of adventurers, found the entrance to the Nightshade Caverns, and discovered the first fragment of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]] there.

# Historical Significance
Lys Willowwhisper founded what would become the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]], beginning a 400+ year quest to recover all fragments of the Lorestone. The organization's dedication to this mission has passed through multiple generations of the Willowwhisper family.

For the complete history of Lys's legacy, see [[Legacy of the Seekers]].

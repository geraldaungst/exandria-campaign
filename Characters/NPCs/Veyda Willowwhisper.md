Former leader of the [[Obsidian Echoforge]], now serving as advisor to her niece [[Lyren Willowwhisper]]. A firbolg mage of considerable skill, her tenure is best remembered for integrating arcane methods into the group's search—an approach that led to the discovery of the penultimate fragment.

_Appearance:_ Medium height, lean build, deep blue eyes. Long dark hair streaked with silver. Robes bearing Echoforge symbols; carries an ornate staff passed down through generations.

## Relationship with Lyren

Veyda raised Lyren after Lyren's mother Selene—Veyda's older sister—was lost on an expedition. The bond is genuine but carries weight. Lyren sometimes chafes at her aunt's shadow; Veyda sometimes withdraws more than necessary to compensate. When they're in the same room, the dynamic is visible if you're looking for it.

## Lyren's Ascendancy

Veyda was lost during an expedition in the Shattered Teeth—a storm, an ambush, no survivors who could account for her. The group eventually presumed her dead and named Lyren as leader, though not without dissent: a senior seeker named Caelum Silverstride argued publicly that leadership should follow merit, not bloodline.

Months later, Veyda turned up alive, found by traders. She surprised everyone by endorsing Lyren's leadership rather than reclaiming it, and stepped into an advisory role. She has never explained how she survived. Caelum's faction went quiet but never fully dissolved.

## Hidden Secret

Veyda made a pact with something she encountered during those lost months—an entity she can't fully describe and doesn't fully understand. It saved her life in exchange for an unspecified favor. She doesn't know what it will ask for, or when. This is the real reason she stepped back from leadership: she's trying to keep whatever comes next from landing on Lyren.

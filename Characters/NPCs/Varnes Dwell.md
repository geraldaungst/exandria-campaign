# Description

Varnes Dwell is a human in his early forties with the disciplined bearing of a career military professional. Standing 6'0" with weathered but well-maintained appearance, he maintains professionalism even in difficult circumstances. His dark brown hair is kept in a tight military cut showing some gray at the temples, and his sharp gray eyes reflect a keen analytical mind constantly assessing his surroundings.

He favors practical, high-quality clothing—well-made travel gear that's functional without being ostentatious. Always carries a leather portfolio containing his professional tools: security diagrams, architectural sketches, and coded notes from various consulting jobs.

## Personality

**Professional:** Varnes takes his work seriously. His reputation as a security consultant depends on three things: thoroughness, discretion, and results. He's known for never cutting corners and never sharing client information—until circumstances make silence more dangerous than speech.

**Analytical:** Former military training combined with years of security consulting have made him exceptional at identifying patterns, weaknesses, and threats. He approaches problems methodically and prefers planning to improvisation.

**Loyal:** Deep bonds with his sister Ilya. The Dwell siblings share a family value: freedom and independence over faction allegiance. He's also fiercely loyal to those who prove themselves trustworthy—he pays his debts.

**Cautious but Decisive:** Years in security work have taught him to be careful, but his military background means he can act decisively when needed. Won't take unnecessary risks, but won't freeze when action is required.

## Roleplay Notes

- **Voice:** Crisp, professional, slightly formal. Military precision in speech patterns.
- **Mannerisms:**
    - Constantly observing surroundings, noting exits and threats
    - Takes detailed mental notes, occasionally jots things in his portfolio
    - Stands with military posture even when relaxed
    - Tends to give information in structured, organized ways
- **Speech Patterns:** Direct and efficient. Rarely uses unnecessary words. Asks clarifying questions to ensure he understands situations fully.

# Background

## Military Service

Served in the Dwendalian Empire military for 12 years as a tactical planning officer. Rose to the rank of Captain before honorable discharge. Specialized in defensive fortifications, threat assessment, and strategic planning. Left the military not due to any failing, but because he wanted freedom from rigid command structures.

## Independent Consulting Career

For the past 5 years, Varnes has run a successful security consulting business based in Port Damali. His services include:

- Facility security assessment
- Threat analysis and risk evaluation
- Protective detail planning
- Caravan security routing
- Vault and storage protection design

His clientele spans multiple factions and nations—he maintains strict neutrality and works for anyone who can afford his rates and isn't actively evil. Known throughout professional circles for his thorough work and absolute discretion.

## Family

Younger sister: Ilya the Sorceress, who runs Ilya's Realm Shop in Port Damali. The siblings are close despite their different career paths. Both share the family value of independence—they chose freedom and self-determination over faction allegiance. They support each other's businesses and stay in regular contact.

## The Rexxentrum Job

Six months ago, the Cobalt Soul hired Varnes to conduct a comprehensive security assessment of their Rexxentrum Archive. This was legitimate work—the Cobalt Soul occasionally brings in outside consultants for fresh perspectives on their security, especially for facilities holding valuable research materials.

During his systematic survey of the Archive, Varnes discovered an unauthorized vault. It wasn't on any official schematics, was heavily warded, and showed signs of regular but secretive use. Following proper protocol, he reported the discovery to his Cobalt Soul contact.

Archivist Ovedo immediately pulled him aside for a private conversation. Ovedo revealed that the vault contained a Lorestone shard—part of confidential but sanctioned research into the prophecy and rift phenomena. Ovedo explained that the Obsidian Echoforge would demand immediate surrender of the shard if they learned of its existence, but that premature assembly without full understanding of the prophecy could be catastrophic (referencing "the Assembly's mistakes at Molaesmyr").

Ovedo paid Varnes extremely well—double his original contract fee plus an ongoing retainer—for complete discretion. Additional NDAs were signed. Varnes was given detailed access to document the vault's security measures (ostensibly to recommend improvements) but sworn to secrecy about its contents.

Varnes kept that confidence. His professional reputation depends on client confidentiality, and Ovedo wasn't doing anything harmful—just conducting careful research on a dangerous artifact.

## Capture and Interrogation

Three weeks ago, while traveling between jobs, Varnes was captured by agents working for Dreyara Drimvar. The ambush was professional and well-planned—they knew his route and capabilities.

He was transported to Cloudfang Keep, where Lady Emer conducted days of brutal interrogation. She wanted to know everything about his recent jobs, particularly anything related to artifacts, archives, or the Lorestone shards. Varnes resisted, knowing that revealing client information would destroy his reputation and potentially endanger people.

Lady Emer used a combination of physical torture and memory-modification magic during the interrogation. Varnes's last clear memory is from early in his captivity. Everything after that is fragmentary—nightmare flashes of pain, questions, and magical intrusion. He doesn't know what he revealed, if anything, before Lady Emer gave up and petrified him for later interrogation by Vaud Qalix himself.

# Current Situation

## Post-Rescue Status

When restored from petrification, Varnes will be:

- **Physically:** Severely weakened - multiple levels of exhaustion from prolonged petrification and prior torture. Needs several days of rest before he can travel safely.
- **Mentally:** Deeply shaken by the memory gaps. Uncertain what information he revealed during interrogation.
- **Emotionally:** Profoundly grateful to his rescuers. Determined to prevent Qalix from getting the Rexxentrum shard, but pragmatic about his current limitations.

## Critical Information He Provides

Once he understands that the party rescued him and can be trusted, Varnes immediately shares everything despite his weakened state:

> "Six months ago, the Cobalt Soul hired me to assess their Archive security in Rexxentrum. Standard work—identify vulnerabilities, recommend improvements. During my survey, I discovered an unauthorized vault. Hidden. Heavily warded. Not on any official schematics.
> 
> When I reported it, Archivist Ovedo pulled me aside privately. Showed me what was inside—a Lorestone shard. Explained it was part of sanctioned but confidential research. Made me sign additional NDAs. Paid me extremely well to forget I'd ever seen it.
> 
> I kept that confidence. It's how I stay in business—client secrets stay secret. But after what happened to me... after days of torture trying to get information out of me... I realized something. If Qalix or his people learn about that shard, Ovedo and everyone in that Archive is in danger.
> 
> My professional ethics say I don't break confidentiality. But they also say I don't let people die when I can prevent it. You saved my life. And you're the only ones who can get to Rexxentrum fast enough to warn them—or better yet, help them secure it properly before Qalix's people figure out it exists.
> 
> I'm breaking my contract. Ovedo will be furious with me. But I'd rather deal with an angry archivist than live with the blood of innocents on my hands because I stayed silent."

He can provide:

- Exact vault location within the Rexxentrum Archive
- Access protocols and security measures
- Description of Archivist Ovedo
- Layout of the Archive facility
- His professional assessment of how to approach Ovedo
- Detailed documentation in his portfolio (hands it over)
- Timeline: he was captured 3 weeks ago, interrogated for days before petrification

## His Plan: Stay and Recover

After providing the critical intelligence, Varnes addresses the obvious question of what happens next:

> [Tries to stand, nearly collapses] "I can't come with you. None of us can." [Gestures to the other petrified victims] "Days... maybe weeks of being stone. We're all exhausted, weak, traumatized. We'd slow you down, and you don't have time for that."
> 
> [If the party suggests freeing the others] "Yes. Please. They didn't deserve this fate either. And honestly... I could use the company on the way back. Safer to travel in a group."
> 
> [If the party doesn't suggest it] "What about the others? They're victims too. If we free them, we can all make our way back west together once we've recovered. I need to reach my sister in Port Damali anyway - let her know I'm alive."
> 
> [Looks around the Keep] "This place is secure now. Lady Emer kept it well-stocked with supplies. We can rest here for a few days, recover properly, then make our way back to civilization together as a group."
> 
> "That's time you don't have. Every day you delay is a day Qalix's people might beat you to Rexxentrum. We'll be fine here - just go. Get there before they do."

**His Recovery Plan:**

- Rest at Cloudfang Keep for 3-4 days with other freed NPCs
- The Keep is defensible, supplied, and safe (major threats eliminated)
- Travel west as a group once everyone has recovered enough
- Head to Port Damali to reunite with his sister Ilya
- Send word to the party once he's recovered and settled

**Parting Words:**

> "I owe you my life. Once I'm back in Port Damali and recovered, I'll send word through my sister's shop. If you need anything - information, contacts, assistance - you call on me. That's a debt I intend to repay."
> 
> [Hands over his detailed notes on the Archive]
> 
> "Now go. Fast as you can. And... thank you. Truly."

## His Uncertainty Creates Urgency

Varnes genuinely doesn't know if Lady Emer extracted the information before petrifying him. His memory modification means he can't be certain. He tells the party:

> "I held out as long as I could, but... I don't remember everything. There were days—maybe weeks—of interrogation. She used magic, not just pain. My memories after the first few days are... fragmentary. I know I had full documentation about the Archive, about Ovedo's shard, all of it. Whether I talked before she gave up and turned me to stone? I genuinely don't know. You have to assume the worst—that Qalix knows, or will know soon."

This creates time pressure without forcing a specific timeline on the DM.

# Relationships

## Ilya the Sorceress (Sister)

Deep familial bond. They stay in regular contact and support each other's businesses. Ilya will be desperately worried about her brother and overjoyed when the party brings news of his rescue. Varnes's first priority after recovery will be getting word to her that he's alive.

## Archivist Ovedo (Complicated)

Professional relationship that Varnes is about to betray by breaking confidentiality. He respects Ovedo's scholarship and understood the reasoning for secrecy. Feels genuine guilt about revealing the information, but believes circumstances justify it. Expects Ovedo to be furious with him when they eventually meet.

## The Party (Grateful Ally)

Varnes considers himself deeply indebted to whoever rescues him. He takes personal debts seriously. He will:

- Provide all information he has that might help them
- Offer his professional services at significant discount
- Serve as a contact and information source going forward
- Call on them for help if he ever faces similar danger
- Remember and repay the favor in the future

## Lady Emer (Traumatized Hatred)

The torture and memory modification left psychological scars. Varnes is normally professional and controlled, but mention of Lady Emer provokes visible distress. If she's already dead when he's restored, he'll be relieved. If not, he'll warn the party extensively about her capabilities.

## Vaud Qalix (Enemy)

Varnes has never met Qalix personally, but knows he was ultimately the one who ordered his capture. He wants to stop Qalix's plans not out of abstract heroism, but because Qalix made it personal. Varnes also recognizes that Qalix represents a threat to Exandria that goes beyond their personal conflict.

## Handling Player Concerns

**If players worry about leaving them:**

- Varnes emphasizes the Keep is secure and well-supplied
- Multiple competent adults can watch each other's backs
- The urgency of reaching Rexxentrum outweighs escort duty
- "That's time you don't have. We'll be fine - just go!"

**If players want to take Varnes along:**

- He physically cannot keep up (exhaustion levels)
- Would slow party down by days

# Quick Reference
> [!info] Essential Details
> - Current Location: Rexxentrum, Dwendalian Empire
> - Key Motivation: Recover and preserve Draconian history; prove her people's worth; see Draconia restored
> - Attitude toward party: Unknown — has not met them yet
> - Status: Active

# Description
Young white Draconblood dragonborn, mid-twenties. Pale frost-white scales with faint iridescence in direct light. Tailed, as befits her caste. Lean and precise in her movements — she carries herself with the careful confidence of someone who has learned that people will underestimate her and has decided to use it.

Works as an independent artifact and information broker, selling recovered Draconian materials, historical documents, and scholarly intelligence to buyers across Wildemount. The Cobalt Soul archive in Rexxentrum is her most reliable and prestigious client. [[Archivist Ovedo]] finds her useful — not entirely trustworthy by institutional standards, but practical and knowledgeable in ways the monks rarely are.

She also, without fully acknowledging it to herself, passes information to [[Vaud Qalix]]'s network. She thinks of this less as working for Qalix and more as contributing to a cause she believes in. The distinction matters to her. It would not matter to the party.

## Roleplay
- Voice: Precise and unhurried. Chooses words carefully. Comfortable with silence.
- Mannerisms: Maintains eye contact a beat longer than is quite comfortable. Handles objects — especially Draconian artifacts — with reverent attention.
- Notable Traits: Genuinely warm with people she respects. Harder to read than she appears. Never volunteers more than she needs to.

# Current Situation
Based in Rexxentrum, cultivating her relationship with the Cobalt Soul archive while managing her broader brokerage work. Recently helped arrange [[Azel Brightful]]'s relocation from Emon, nominally to assist with cataloguing his Draconian collection for the archive. The arrangement benefits everyone — including, quietly, Qalix's network, which now has Azel's relics and firsthand accounts closer to Xhorhas.

She is genuinely fond of Azel. She tells herself this makes what she does less complicated. It makes it more so.

# Background
Born in the Draconian diaspora — grew up in a community of scattered Draconbloods who had lost everything and were slowly losing their cultural coherence as well. Never saw Draconia. Grew up with its absence as a defining fact of her life. Inherited grief she had no direct right to, and has spent her adult years trying to earn it.

Taught herself the artifact trade out of necessity and discovered a genuine talent for it. Her white scales — Vorugal's color — have followed her everywhere, drawing looks she learned early to absorb without reaction. She has never spoken publicly about whether it affects her. It does.

Encountered Qalix's manifesto in her mid-twenties through a contact in the trade. The restoration argument resonated immediately. She began passing information to his network not long after — nothing that felt dangerous at the time, just names and locations of Draconian materials and people who cared about them. It has never felt like a line being crossed. It has been a series of very small steps.

Met [[Azel Brightful]] in Emon while sourcing Conclave-era materials. Whether she found him by design or by chance, she recognized immediately what he was: a living primary source, a keeper of Draconian memory, and someone whose trust would be worth having. She pursued the friendship honestly. The honesty is real. The completeness is not.

# Hidden Information
> [!secret]- DM Only
> - Kexira does not think of herself as a spy or an agent. She thinks of herself as someone who believes in something and acts accordingly. This is not self-deception in the cynical sense — it is genuine moral compartmentalization, which is harder to argue with and harder to undo.
> - She does not know about Shard 5. Ovedo is extremely careful, and Kexira has not been inside the archive's restricted sections. She is close enough to be dangerous, not close enough to know it yet.
> - If she ever learns the full scope of what Qalix is doing — the patron, the manipulation, the cascading harm — her response will not be predictable. She is not simply a villain waiting to be revealed. She is someone who made a series of choices she believed in and will have to reckon with where they led.
> - She would not betray Azel deliberately. She may do so accidentally. She does not know this about herself yet.
> - If the party approaches her honestly and with evidence rather than accusation, there is a path where she becomes an asset. It is not an easy path.

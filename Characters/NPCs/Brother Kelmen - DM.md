# Description & Roleplay
Firbolg monk of the Monastery of Dimensional Harmony, dead for about 300 years. Before the ritual he flickered between solid and translucent and spoke archaic Giant; the party needed a translator (Hesterian's Universal Speech, Seraphina's Comprehend Languages).
- **Voice:** Quiet, formal, archaic Giant when first met. Short sentences. Says "I do not know" when he doesn't.
- **Mannerisms:** Looks at his hands and touches stone when something is real. Goes still when he recognizes something. Stands at edges and waits.
- **Key traits:** Protective (raised Ondur's shield defensively, never pursued). Apologizes for what he didn't know. Does not advocate when asked to choose.

# Current Situation
- The soulwood ritual was completed on 24 Thunsheer. Kelmen passed through the Whisper and entered Solvei's Vigilance.
- [[Qilynn Duskwhisper (Nicole)|Qilynn]] is attuned to the bow. Kelmen has not spoken to her or anyone yet.
- His first words, when he does speak: *"I understand now what we were missing."* He then describes what he experienced passing through the stabilized Whisper: the Lorestone can manipulate planar boundaries, but without something to hold time still while it works, the feedback is catastrophic. He does not elaborate unless asked.
- Open: the Shard 8 lead and the stabilizing-counterweight revelation are released through him when the party discusses the Lorestone.

> [!secret]- Hidden Information
> - He carries the monks' unfinished research: a resonant object deep in the Feywild, reachable through the Savalirwood Fey crossing. The monks' logs may hold directional notes. This is the Shard 8 lead.
> - Flute moment with Popcorn: the pan flute has been near a Fey crossing, and something there has listened to it for a long time. He doesn't know how he knows.
> - On learning of the Lorestone he recognizes that the monks tried by technique what needed an artifact. See [[Kelmen and Popcorn Connections]].

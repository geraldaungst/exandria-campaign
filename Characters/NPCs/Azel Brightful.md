# Quick Reference

> - Current Location: Rexxentrum, Dwendalian Empire (formerly Emon, Tal'Dorei)
> - Key Motivation: Tend the shop; honor the dead; live quietly — and now, see Draconia restored
> - Attitude toward party: Warm, unhurried, genuinely fond of Drawg
> - Status: Active

# Description
Elderly silver dragonborn. Former career soldier of the Draconian army, now owner of [[Veteran's Victory Vault]]. Warm and unhurried with everyone, but lights up around fellow soldiers and dragonborn. Loves telling war stories — always about the people around him, never about himself.

Known by two nicknames among those who know him well:
- **Vethkar** — used by veterans and fellow Draconians; his senior NCO rank, now a title of respect
- **Deacon** — used by long-time Emon locals; earned after the Conclave attack, when he began writing letters to fallen soldiers' families and performing informal memorial rites

Drawg knows him only as the kindly old dragonborn who sold him a pair of tactical pants and talked for hours.

## Roleplay
- Voice: Unhurried, warm, self-deprecating
- Mannerisms: Deflects praise; redirects stories away from himself toward others
- Notable Traits: Never the protagonist of his own stories. Doesn't lead with his military past but is open about it if asked.

# Current Situation
Recently relocated from Emon to Rexxentrum, en route to Xarzith Kitril. The shop is partially packed — this is a waystation, not a permanent home. He is cheerful about the move, though there is something quietly wistful about leaving Emon after so many decades.

His relocation was inspired by [[Kexira Suulveth]], a young white Draconblood dragonborn he met in Emon roughly a year ago. She arrived presenting herself as a scholarly collector of Draconian historical materials, with known ties to the Cobalt Soul archive in Rexxentrum. They bonded quickly — she was hungry for firsthand accounts of Draconia and the Conclave, and Azel found in her something that reminded him of a younger, more reckless version of someone he once knew. He has become something of a mentor to her.

Through Kexira, Azel was introduced to [[Vaud Qalix]]'s manifesto and the broader restoration movement. He has read the brief version carefully and responded to its core argument: that Draconia deserves a future, not just a memorial. He is under no illusions that restoration will be simple or clean — but he believes in the goal, and he believes Kexira's instincts are good. He is traveling to Rexxentrum partly to help her catalogue a collection of Draconian materials for the archive, and partly because Xhorhas feels, for the first time, like somewhere worth going.

He does not know that Kexira passes information to Qalix's network. He does not know that his own relocation was, in some small part, encouraged.

# Background
- Career NCO (Vethkar) in the Draconian army; never sought officer rank
- Stationed in Emon as part of Draconia's diplomatic-military presence
- Was present when [[Tiberius Stormwind]] invoked wartime authority in Emon to raise a Draconian army; refused to march on those orders, believing Tiberius lacked the authority and that the downstream consequences would be severe
- The refusal forced an extended interaction between them; Azel came to understand what was driving Tiberius and his view softened considerably
- When Draconia fell and Tiberius died defending it, Azel organized a quiet memorial for the Draconian soldiers in Emon who had no one to mourn them — Tiberius included
- Has a complicated, privately held view of Tiberius: saw his flaws clearly, but respects what he ultimately chose and what he died for
- After the Conclave attack, never returned to military service; opened the shop instead
- The shop is partly a livelihood, partly a way of keeping faith with the dead — a collection of things that belonged to a world that no longer exists

# Hidden Information
> [!secret]- DM Only
> - Azel's view of Tiberius is more nuanced than the historical record. He knew the arrogance and the recklessness firsthand. He also knew what drove it. He doesn't correct the statue's inscription — but he doesn't fully believe it either.
> - The "Deacon" nickname has a specific origin locals know. Azel has never explained it to an outsider.
> - Kexira's visit to Emon was not entirely accidental. Whether she was sent specifically for Azel or simply recognized his value when she found him is unclear even to Qalix's network. Either way, the relationship is genuine on both sides — which makes it more dangerous, not less.
> - Azel would be devastated to learn the full truth about Qalix. He would not abandon the goal of restoration. He would absolutely abandon Qalix.
> - If the party is honest with him and respectful, he will listen. He will not be easily convinced. But he will think about it.

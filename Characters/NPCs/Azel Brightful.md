> - Attitude toward party: Warm, unhurried, genuinely fond of Drawg
> - Status: Active

# Description
Elderly silver dragonborn. Former career soldier of the Draconian army, now owner of [[Veteran's Victory Vault]] in Emon. Warm and unhurried with everyone, but lights up around fellow soldiers and dragonborn. Loves telling war stories — always about the people around him, never about himself.

Known by two nicknames among those who know him well:
- **Vethkar** — used by veterans and fellow Draconians; his senior NCO rank (approximate equivalent of "Sergeant", now a title of respect
- **Deacon** — used by long-time Emon locals; earned after the Conclave attack, when he began writing letters to fallen soldiers' families and performing informal memorial rites

Drawg knows him only as the kindly old dragonborn who sold him a pair of tactical pants and talked for hours.

## Roleplay
- Voice: Unhurried, warm, self-deprecating
- Mannerisms: Deflects praise; redirects stories away from himself toward others
- Notable Traits: Never the protagonist of his own stories. Doesn't lead with his military past but is open about it if asked.

# Background
- Career NCO in the Draconian army; never sought officer rank
- Stationed in Emon as part of Draconia's diplomatic-military presence
- Was present when Tiberius Stormwind invoked wartime authority in Emon to raise a Draconian army; refused to march on those orders, believing Tiberius lacked the authority and that the downstream consequences would be severe
- The refusal forced an extended interaction between them; Azel came to understand what was driving Tiberius and his view softened considerably
- When Draconia fell and Tiberius died defending it, Azel organized a quiet memorial for the Draconian soldiers in Emon who had no one to mourn them — Tiberius included
- Has a complicated, privately held view of Tiberius: saw his flaws clearly, but respects what he ultimately chose and what he died for
- After the Conclave attack, never returned to military service; opened the shop instead
- The shop is partly a livelihood, partly a way of keeping faith with the dead — a collection of things that belonged to a world that no longer exists

# Hidden Information
> [!secret]- DM Only
> - Azel's view of Tiberius is more nuanced than the historical record. He knew the arrogance and the recklessness firsthand. He also knew what drove it. He doesn't correct the statue's inscription — but he doesn't fully believe it either.
> - The "Deacon" nickname has a specific origin locals know. Azel has never explained it to an outsider.

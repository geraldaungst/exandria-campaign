## What Aveqtaro Thaan Knows

Subnote of [[Aveqtaro Thaan]]: everything she can tell the party, by topic, with the stage at which she can tell it. DM only. Stages are defined in [[Aveqtaro Memory Recovery Tracker]], whose Now panel lists what she can tell them today and what unlocks next.

> [!warning] Draft
> Seeded 22 October 2026 from existing notes only. Stage placements are first guesses; move a fact by changing its code.

**Line format:** `- <requirement> · what she knows`. The requirement is `baseline` (she knows it as herself), a stage (`A2`, `B3`), or stages joined with `+`, all of which must be reached (`A3+B3`). Bullets without a requirement are DM notes and don't show in the panel.

## Baseline

- baseline · Precision instrument-making for magical phenomena: rift detectors, dunamantic focusing lenses, calibration devices. Clients include Dynasty military, civil infrastructure, and private researchers.
- baseline · Kryn faith and den life: the Luxon, consecution, the den's place in Kryn life. She has never undergone consecution.
- baseline · Luxon Beacons are sacred to the Kryn by nature, whoever holds one and however it was found.
- baseline · Empire culture from years of black-market texts and Dwendalian music.
- baseline · Her own experiences: déjà vu, spatial and geometric intuitions she can't explain, which resemble what consecuted elders describe. No one has been able to explain them, and she has stopped asking.

## Rifts and planar geography

- A1 · Measurement and mapping problems solved by methods no one taught her; she calls it "good instincts."
- A2 · Diagrams and terms she doesn't recognize; how a rift or an artifact works, understood without being told.
- A3 · The geometric and temporal alignments for safe rift closure.
- A3 · Ley lines and magical nexuses of the Age of Arcanum, including where the barriers between planes were weakest (Cadariel's life's work).

## Luxon Beacons and the Lorestone

- A2 · A Beacon and a Lorestone shard react to each other: within 100 feet both brighten.
- A3 · The dual-artifact requirement: the Lorestone channels planar energy and throws off temporal flux; a Beacon anchors local time. Neither is safe alone.
- A3 · The activation order: Beacon first, then the Lorestone inside its field (30–100 feet), with two attuned operators channeling at once.
- A3 · What improper Beacon positioning does: temporal shear, harm to the operators, Beacon overload.
- A3 · The stabilization ritual for assembly: preparing and positioning the Beacon (step 4), and why the final assembly must run with it active (step 5). See [[Lorestone - Assembly Ritual]].
- A3 · The prophecy's "beacon bright" is a Luxon Beacon, not the Lorestone.
- Sources: [[Luxon Beacon Integration with Lorestone]], [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM Notes]].

## Cadariel and Eryndor

- B2 · Feelings without facts: grief at ruins, warmth toward strangers, a sense of having lost someone important.
- B3 · Who Cadariel was: an elven cartographer and lorekeeper of the Age of Arcanum who set out to map the paths between worlds as well as the physical realm.
- B3 · Cadariel's friendship with [[Eryndor]]: long debates in the great libraries and under the night sky. Eryndor spoke of what might come to pass; Cadariel tried to work out how those futures could be reached.
- A3+B3 · The master map: what it charted and that Cadariel made it. Where it went is open.
- Source: [[Cadariel Ordo]].

## The Kryn Dynasty

- A3 · Why the party holding the Beacon serves Dynasty interests, and why returning it too soon is the more dangerous choice.
- A3+B3 · She can make that case to Dynasty leadership with authority, because she was there.
- Source: [[Luxon Beacon Political Consequences]].

## What she can't know

- Cadariel lived before the Calamity, so she has no memory of the Molaesmyr rupture (585 PD) or anything after it.
- Nothing about Qalix, Ceratos, the Obsidian Echoforge, or the shards' modern holders, beyond what the party or her own reading tells her.

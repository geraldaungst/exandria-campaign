# Description & Roleplay
Average height, wiry and scholarly, with wavy sand-colored hair and eyes that shift between green and blue. Wears flowing sea-blue and green robes hung with trinkets from many cultures, and an amulet that blends several styles. His accent is hard to place and shifts with his audience. Full description in [[Celdric Ambril - Persona and Sayings]].

- **Voice:** A mysterious storyteller. Not above a little exaggeration and misdirection in service of a good story and a little profit, but at his core he is mostly honest.
- **Mannerisms:** Patronizing when asked what his sayings mean: "My friend, when you have been around Exandria as many times as I have, the meaning is quite obvious is it not? Indeed. Indeed." When he catches himself rambling: "Ah, I'm going on and on. Well, you know what they say: Never trust a river that doesn't babble!"
- **Key traits:** Will go to great lengths to maintain his fictional persona, but not at risk of his health, life or collection. It is a great and wonderful game to him. The sayings, the stories he tells about them and the Celunor the Tidebound legend are in [[Celdric Ambril - Persona and Sayings]].

# Current Situation
- Hesterian named Celunor to the Truscans as a scholar interested in the Deastok site ([[Session 53]]).
- He is looking for a blade that matches Drawg's scabbard. Qilynn knows him as "Keldrick" and says he will research for a price.
- He was the vendor for [[Lady Triotta Thessaly]]'s two-headed etcheda monkey ([[Letter to Celdric Ambril]]).
- Knows Shard 6 was found, but not what it is or who owns it.

> [!secret]- Hidden Information
> - He is a human born to a pair of traveling merchants, with nothing mystic in his origin. An old book his parents acquired subtly sharpened his gift for mimicry.
> - After his parents died he took his inheritance to Port Damali and founded the Drowned Library. The sunken-archive theme is theater, and his "discoveries" are ordinary purchases from suppliers and auctions.
> - His expertise in history and the arcane is real.
> - Full text in [[Celdric Ambril - Persona and Sayings]].

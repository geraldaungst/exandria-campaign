## Orvustia Esseren

Last Edited: May 1, 2021 9:36 PM

Tags: NPC

![https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/4627442/0f7e6780d0d33ac2d399ab1992109819.jpg](https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/4627442/0f7e6780d0d33ac2d399ab1992109819.jpg)

Esseren grew up in the farmland outside Baldurs Gate, and this is her first trip more than two miles away from home. She is smart, tough, and talented with both spear and bow, but she knows nothing of the world beyond her aunt's farm or of people who deal dishonestly. Her aunt, a wise woman, believes this trip will be good for her.

Tessa, an Aasimar with a celestial heritage, serves as a field agent for the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]]. Her task is to track anomalies that could lead them to the materials needed for the construction of [[Gaius Emberfell|Gaius]]'s device.

## Sumad-Dekon Harrowgate

- Studied with [[Sumad-Dekon Harrowgate]] at the Soltryce Academy in Rexxentrum. Both were top students who debated ancient texts; the partnership turned into unspoken romantic feelings, hidden behind academic rivalry. Unknown to most.
- Her celestial heritage pulled her into field work with the Echoforge. His research into forbidden subjects led him to the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]].
- She still has complicated feelings about his choice. Neither has confronted them; each is committed to an opposing side of the prophetic schism.

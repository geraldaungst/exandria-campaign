# Aura Timber

Last Edited: November 7, 2021 6:30 PM
Tags: NPC

![[Aura_Timber.jpg]]

Aura Timber, Male Tabaxi Rogue 7

*Medium (6'8") Tabaxi, Neutral Evil (CR 7)*

**Armor Class** 10

**Hit Points** 50 (7d6)

**Speed** 30 ft.

STR 8; DEX 18; CON 16; INT 10; WIS 11; CHA 14

**Skills** Acrobatics +3, Religion +3

**Senses** Passive Perception 13

**Languages** Common

**Attacks** Melee +2 / 2d8-1, Ranged +3 / 2d8+0, Grapple +3

**Possessions:** 500 gp. 1 Minor magic item.

**Description:**  His left thumb is blackened and bruised, but otherwise he appears quite healthy. He is wearing the road beaten gear of a warrior. His fur is beige and very short. His blue unseeing eyes seem to look past you.

**Personality:**  He likes to fabricate stories about his past exploits and how crazy he is, but in actuality he is rather bland. Any perceived weakness will be pressed and exploited. He is seen as merciless and passionate.

**History:**  He was born with several neurological imbalances, which makes sleep difficult for him. It may also be what makes him so good at details. In his youth he was part of a gang. He got caught, but did not rat out his comrades and served his time in prison. He hasn't been sleeping well lately.

**Motivation:**  A rival would like him out of the picture; and he will attempt to ambush and attack anyone he thinks he can take on alone and try to knock them out with non-lethal damage. if successful, he will try to sell them to his slave-trader contact

**Ideals:** Fighting. **Flaws:** Disease, Mundane, Insomniac. **Bonds:** Criminal record, Immigrant. **Occupation:**  Weaver

**Voice:**  Uses lots of figures of speech

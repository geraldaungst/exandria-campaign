Sister of [[Gilxel Valthyra]].

Her father died in service to the Lance Defenders. Thava was distraught at the loss of her father, and turned to the Cult of the Dragon for solace. They took her in, and she left her home, leaving her mother and Gilxel behind. Mother did not last a year before she died of depression and grief.

Gilxel has dedicated her life to learning about the Cult in order to track down and rescue Thava.

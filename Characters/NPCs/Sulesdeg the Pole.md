# Sulesdeg the Pole

Last Edited: May 1, 2021 9:43 PM
Tags: NPC

![https://img.17qq.com/images/hmmdchodpov.jpeg](https://img.17qq.com/images/hmmdchodpov.jpeg)

Among his tribe in his homeland of the Shaar, Sulesdeg's name means "tall as a lodge pole". On the Sword Coast, he is just known as "the Pole". At 7' 5'' in height, he is probably is the tallest human the characters or anyone else in the caravan has ever seen. He doesn't talk much, but when he does, people generally listen.

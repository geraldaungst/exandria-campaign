## Quick Reference

> [!info] Essential Details
> - **Location:** [[White Peak Inn]], Garden Grounds, [[Deastok]]. Behind the bar
> - **Occupation:** Innkeeper. Henrik Halsen's brother; Marta Halsen is his sister-in-law
> - **Goal:** Run the inn quietly for the locals who already know it
> - **Attitude toward party:** Courteous and uninvested until Marta's name or the comb comes up, then warm
> - **Key knowledge:** Where Jorun Kettlewright lives (a stonemason's house in Garden Grounds, squared lintels); that Jorun hasn't been in; that Bramwell is a harmless fixture

## Description & Roleplay

A broad, weathered human in his fifties with a neatly kept grey beard, sleeves rolled past thick forearms. He wipes glasses he finished drying some time ago.

- **Voice:** Plain and unhurried. Few words at baseline.
- **Mannerisms:** Seats guests, takes the order, asks nothing about where they are from.
- **Key traits:** Warms only to family. Once warm he sits with the party, asks after Marta and Henrik by name, then raises Jorun himself, because Jorun is a friend and not just a regular.

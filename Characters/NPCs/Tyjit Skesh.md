## Tyjit Skesh

Last Edited: May 1, 2021 9:32 PM

Tags: NPC

![https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/2401931/Tyjit.jpg](https://db4sgowjqfwig.cloudfront.net/images/2401931/Tyjit.jpg)

Female Shield Dwarf

Tyjit is quick to anger and quicker to use her two blades, but is also honest to a fault. She does not tolerate bullying, and always explains the cause of her anger in the hopes that people's attitude would change.

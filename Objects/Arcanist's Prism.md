# Quick Reference
**Location:** None. The Prism does not exist; it is a legend that [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] has taken for fact.
**Significance:** Qalix believes it is the "final stabilization" component of his [[Harmonic Planar Conduit]]. The party has seen it named in [[Lady Emer's Research Notes]] (Day 120).

# Description & Properties
A rare crystal rumored to be capable of dispersing focused magical energy, reducing the risk of catastrophic feedback during planar manipulation. [[Vaud Qalix - DM|Qalix]] believes it is the "final stabilization" component of his [[Harmonic Planar Conduit]].

> [!secret]- DM only: the Prism is a myth
> In actuality, the Prism is a myth. No such artifact exists, and no one has ever produced one. Qalix's flawed theory latched onto the legend. The players have seen it named in [[Lady Emer's Research Notes]] (Day 120); if they pursue it, let them confirm quickly that it's only a legend.

# Lore
## Legend

The object known now as the Arcanist's Prism was originally crafted by a coalition of mages from the Age of Arcanum, a time when the mastery over magic was at its zenith. These mages were focused on the creation of a tool that could be used to safely study and harness the power of the ley lines, the invisible channels of raw magical energy that crisscross the world.

The prism was created from a unique type of crystal found only in a now-lost mine deep within the cliffs of the Orosk Mountains. This crystal was known for its purity and its innate property to diffuse magical energies without corrupting them. The mages used an intricate array of enchantments to enhance this natural property, creating a powerful and versatile instrument for arcane experimentation.

# Quick Reference
**Location:** Offered to the PCs by a rival in [[Prologue 3 - Popcorn and Hesterian]]. Current holder not recorded.
**Significance:** The rival believes it is a treasure map. It leads to the Blooming Grove, where [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] can learn a new bard spell.

# Description & Properties
An intricately drawn map depicting a hidden grove with an ancient tree, said to be the birthplace of a rare melody known only to a few bards.

> [!secret]- DM only: the Grove
> The map leads to the Blooming Grove and the tree grows over the grave of someone who will turn out to be important to the campaign (TBD). The melody will be a new bard spell that [[Hesterian Shyr - Dot|Hesterian]] will learn after visiting the Grove and completing a task there.

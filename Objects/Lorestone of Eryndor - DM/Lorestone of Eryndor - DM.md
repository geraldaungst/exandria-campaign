## Faction Understanding

### Faction Knowledge Evolution

- The [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] originally believed only 5 shards existed based on gaps in their reconstructed prophecy.
- Shard 6 surfacing in Marquet raised their count to six. Whether anyone in the world suspects more than six is undecided; the party should be the ones to work it out.
- They currently assume the Stonefoot Compass lead will take them to Shard 5, unaware it actually points to Shard 7.
- The party knows Shard 5 is in Rexxentrum and carries the compass, so they are positioned to be first to realize the compass shard is a seventh.
- When all 7 Exandrian shards are assembled and fail to fit together correctly, this will reveal the existence of a final 8th piece—which is no longer on Exandria.

### Current Interpretations (All Incomplete)

**[[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]]**: Believes the Lorestone itself is the "beacon" mentioned in the prophecy. Correct that it can close rifts, incorrect about it being sufficient alone. Unaware of stabilization requirements.

**[[Malachite Cord - DM]]**: Believes using the Lorestone will cause rifts to tear wider. Partially correct - unstabilized use WILL cause catastrophic failure. Unaware that proper stabilization makes it safe and effective.

**[[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]]**: Believes the Lorestone can create permanent controlled gateways. Fundamentally wrong about its purpose, but their desire to control rather than close rifts makes them especially dangerous.

**[[Vaud Qalix - DM]]**: Believes he can use it to create stable energy siphons for Draconia. Completely wrong about its capabilities and unaware of Ceratos's manipulation. Doesn't know a Luxon Beacon is what stabilizes it; no one living does.

## Notes

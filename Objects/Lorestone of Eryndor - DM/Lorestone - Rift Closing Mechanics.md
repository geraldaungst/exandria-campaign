## Core Information

### Rift-Closing Ritual Details

**REQUIRES**: Assembled Lorestone AND active Luxon Beacon for temporal stabilization

The assembled Lorestone may be used as an arcane focus for a ritual for closing a nearby planar rift. The Luxon Beacon must be positioned to create a stabilization field around the ritual area. The details of the ritual are similar for all rifts, but the size of the rift determines the length and complexity of the ritual. See the rift table for specifics.

#### Requirements

- **Assembled Lorestone** must be placed within specific range of the rift (see table)
- **Active Luxon Beacon** positioned for temporal field coverage
- **Two participants** must simultaneously apply:
    - One chaotic effect (channeled through the Lorestone)
    - One ordered effect (channeled through the Beacon)
- Effects can be magical or physical (DM's discretion on what qualifies)
- Both make relevant skill/ability checks
- **Knowledge of proper ritual** (held by [[Aveqtaro Thaan]] once her memories as [[Cadariel Ordo]] are recovered)

#### Failed Attempts

**Without an active Luxon Beacon**, the ritual always fails. The consequence scales with the rift's size, and the tiers match [[Temporal Stabilization Theory]]:

| Rift size       | Tier     | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Small, Medium   | Minor    | The rift grows one size. 4d10 force damage to all creatures within 30 ft. Time runs unevenly within 100 ft for 1d4 weeks.                                                                                           |
| Large, Huge     | Moderate | The rift grows one size. 8d10 force damage to all creatures within 100 ft. A temporal wound within 300 ft lasts for years, and riftcages no longer shrink the rift.                                                  |
| Gargantuan      | Major    | The rift becomes permanent. Corruption begins to spread, as at Molaesmyr. 12d10 force damage to all creatures within half a mile. The Lorestone survives and is thrown clear.                                       |
| Mega-Rift       | Critical | The Lorestone is destroyed. 20d10 force damage to all creatures within 1 mile (no save). Rifts tear open within 10 miles.                                                                                           |

**With an active Luxon Beacon**:

- **Below threshold**: No effect, but temporal strain accumulates
- **Less than half threshold**:
    - Rift tears further (+1 required success)
    - 2d10 damage to creatures within 15 feet
    - Damage type and saving throw based on connected plane

Assembling the Lorestone without a Beacon follows its own limit; see [[Lorestone - Assembly Ritual]].

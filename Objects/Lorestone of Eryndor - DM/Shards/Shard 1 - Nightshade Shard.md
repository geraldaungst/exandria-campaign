## Properties and rulings

- Security: high. Lyren wears it.
- Also known as the **Shard of Willow's Legacy**: fashioned into a pendant.
- Item block for all shards: [[Lorestone - Magic Item Blocks]].
- *Appearance:* A fragment of the original obsidian disc, fashioned into a pendant. Roughly the size of a palm, its deep black surface has a mirror-like sheen, catching light in peculiar, arcane patterns. Set in an ornate silver setting, suspended on a chain of intertwining silver and green, representing the melding of nature and magic.
- Heirloom, not a power: it passes from one Echoforge leader to the next. Its value is the meaning the leaders have given it. The bearer feels the memories of earlier leaders as impressions, with no mechanics.

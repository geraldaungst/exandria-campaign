## Notes

- Only the DM knows it exists. It lies on the Fey side of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing, reached safely through the Savalirwood Fey crossing, on the way to [[Ruins of Molaesmyr - DM|Molaesmyr]]. Ilixidor's notebooks (Caes Mosor level 6) record where he set it; Kelmen knows of a resonance, not what it is.
- The final shard to be discovered. When the seven Exandrian shards are assembled and don't fit together, the missing eighth is revealed.
- The Obsidian Echoforge, Vaud Qalix, and the Emissaries of the Sunfall will all pursue it once they learn it exists.
- Lead: [[Kelmen and Popcorn Connections]].

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

This large, thin coin seems to be carved from a single shard of bleached bone. It has a slightly uneven, organic shape. Thin, dark veins run across its surface like dried blood, giving the impression of ancient cracks that never quite healed. In the center of one side is an engraved symbol of a skeletal hand holding a scale. The reverse side bears a swirling pattern that seems to spiral inward, drawing the eye toward a tiny blackened point in the center.

You have advantage on death saving throws while carrying this magic coin. In addition, you can flip the coin as an action to send a magical ping to an undead creature with which you are familiar. The creature recognizes you as the sender if it knows you and becomes aware of your precise position.

## Luxon Beacon Political Consequences

> [!warning] DM Only

### The Situation

The party holds a [[luxon-beacon-egw|Luxon Beacon]] recovered from [[Lady Emer - DM Notes]] at

[[Cloudfang Keep - DM Notes]]. Emer found it herself at the Savalirwood rift before [[Vaud Qalix - DM Notes]] began funding her. Once he learned of it from her reports, he made it the priority of her research, with no indication he understood its true nature or sacred status.

### Provenance

This Beacon was previously unknown. Emer found it partially exposed in collapsed earth about twenty feet from the Savalirwood rift, after the rift's expansion disturbed the ground ([[Lady Emer's Research Notes]], Day 61; the players have this). How it came to be buried there is open. Because it was never catalogued by the [[Kryn Dynasty - DM Notes]], they

are not currently searching for it. The Dynasty's claim, when they eventually

learn of it, will be theological rather than possessive: *that object is sacred

to us by nature, regardless of how it was found or who holds it.*

This is a subtler and more interesting negotiating position than simple theft—

the party cannot be accused of taking something, but neither can they claim the

Beacon is simply theirs to keep.

### Three Active Problems

**Qalix escalates.** He made an artifact of significant value the priority of

Emer's research, and she lost it. He doesn't yet know the Beacon is essential to safely using the

Lorestone—no one living does—but he knows his operation

was compromised and that the party is responsible. They have moved from

"persons Dreyara monitors" to "persons Qalix wants dealt with personally."

**The Dynasty will eventually come.** When word reaches the Kryn Dynasty that

a Luxon Beacon is in the hands of Wildemount adventurers, they will make

contact. This need not begin as hostile—it may start as a formal inquiry or

diplomatic approach—but it creates an ongoing obligation the party cannot

ignore indefinitely.

**The party cannot safely return it.** The Beacon is required to assemble and

use the Lorestone without catastrophic failure. Returning it to the Dynasty

without a negotiated arrangement means losing the ability to close the rifts.

Every conversation about giving it back carries this unspoken cost.

### The Resolution Path

[[Aveqtaro Thaan]] is positioned to become the bridge between the party's need

and the Dynasty's claim. As her memories as [[Cadariel Ordo]] recover, she will

understand better than anyone why the party's possession of the Beacon serves

Dynasty interests—that returning it prematurely is the more dangerous choice.

She can make that argument internally to Dynasty leadership in a way the party

never could.

This makes finding and protecting Aveqtaro a practical priority for the party,

not just a narrative one.

# Overview

A small but unstable rift has opened at the Port Damali docks near where the merchant ship (The _Wavechaser_) is berthed. The ship recently arrived from Marquet carrying what was listed on the manifest as "Volcanic Glass Sculpture by Artist Hasani Tumil" - allegedly commissioned for a wealthy collector in Port Damali. The rift initially appeared as a curiosity but has since grown dangerous, with several guards now transformed into monstrous entities and a creature from the Far Realm attacking the docks. Panic and chaos have erupted at the pier.

# The Truth

- The "sculpture" was actually Shard 6 of the Lorestone that was secretly transported from Marquet
- A representative named Calderax picked up the artifact shortly after the ship docked
- Unbeknownst to Calderax, the shard was subsequently stolen from his room by Dreyara Drimvar, who is working for Vaud Qalix
- The rift appeared an hour or two after Calderax left with the shard, making the ship's crew believe the two events are connected (they are not)
- The Zhelezo patrol assigned to guard the rift and keep civilians away has been corrupted and is transforming into Gibbering Mouthers
- A [[gorillafish.jpg|Gorillafish]] emerged from the rift and is now attacking people on the pier

# Current Situation at the Docks

- The rift (now approximately 6 feet in diameter) hovers above pier 7, occasionally pulsing with energy
- Two corrupted Zhelezo guards (mid-transformation into Gibbering Mouthers) are attacking civilians
- A Gorillafish that emerged from the rift is causing havoc nearby
- Panic has erupted among dockworkers, merchants, and onlookers
- The _Wavechaser_ crew is in disarray, some fleeing, others trying to protect their ship
- Local authorities are overwhelmed and struggling to contain the situation

# Key Factions and NPCs

## Obsidian Echoforge

- Calderax was secretly gathering the shard for them, not for a "wealthy collector" as claimed
- Calderax is unaware that the shard has been stolen from his room
- Aethor Kalisk will arrive sometime after the party to assess the situation

## Dreyara Drimvar

- Has successfully stolen the shard from Calderax's room
- Currently not at the scene but has agents watching the party
- Working for Vaud Qalix, who wants the shard for unknown purposes

## Cobalt Soul

- A representative is present, documenting events and prepared to offer assistance
- Will be interested in any connection between the rift and the Savalirwood corruption

## Port Authority/Zhelezo

- Captain Bachan Briarfell has left a message for the party at their tavern requesting assistance
- Unaware that the "art piece" was actually a smuggled artifact
- More concerned with containing the current chaos than investigating the cargo

# Ship's Crew of the _Wavechaser_

## Captain Orlen Marshwind

- Hiding in his cabin, refusing to take responsibility
- Insists he delivered the art piece to Calderax as contracted
- When pressed: "Look, I run a respectable ship. The manifest said 'art piece,' and I delivered it to Calderax just like I was supposed to. Whatever happened after that isn't my problem."

## First Mate Tarik Voss

- Trying to maintain order among remaining crew
- Believes the artifact caused the rift despite the timing discrepancy
- If questioned: "That cursed thing! I knew it was trouble the moment it came aboard. Calderax took it, and then not two hours later, this tear in reality appears? That's no coincidence!"

## Quartermaster Jenna Fairwind

- Pragmatic dwarf organizing evacuation of remaining cargo
- One of the few level-headed crew members
- If approached: "Calderax took the package and signed for it—proper documentation and everything. The rift appeared later. Don't let these superstitious fools convince you otherwise. Though I admit, the timing is... unfortunate."

## Benno Kelks, Deckhand

- Young, superstitious human sailor who opened the crate during the voyage
- Hiding at The Barnacled Mermaid Tavern, drinking heavily
- Can provide a description of the shard if found and questioned

# The Corrupted Guards (Former Zhelezo Patrol)

## Guard Loren Mercer

- Once a tall, broad-shouldered human woman
- Mid-transformation state: lower body melting into a mass of gibbering mouths
- Patches of armor still visible, fused to liquefying flesh
- Eyes remain human and filled with horror, suggesting some awareness is trapped inside
- Occasionally screams coherent words between the gibbering: "Help... me... burn... it..."
- Uses Gibbering Mouther stat block but can be recognized as formerly human upon close inspection

## Guard Pellin Tornwell

- Former half-elf male in his 40s
- More advanced in transformation: almost entirely mouths and eyes
- Remnants of Zhelezo insignia visible in the melting mass
- Transformation spreading visibly during the encounter
- Occasionally stops attacking to clutch at head in apparent agony
- Uses Gibbering Mouther stat block with higher movement speed (30 ft.)

# Key Locations

## The Dock Area (Pier 7)

- Center of chaos with the rift hovering 10 feet above the wooden planks
- Damaged crates and abandoned goods scattered everywhere
- Several bodies of injured or dead civilians
- The _Wavechaser_ docked nearby, crew frantically trying to cast off
- Pools of strange fluid where the corruption is spreading

## The Barnacled Mermaid Tavern

- Refuge for fleeing dockworkers and sailors
- Rumors spreading rapidly among frightened patrons
- Benno hiding in a back room, drinking heavily
- Potential witnesses who saw Calderax take the "sculpture"

# Investigation Clues & Discoveries

- The corruption affecting the guards is visibly similar to that in the Savalirwood (key revelation for the party)
- Timing discrepancy: Multiple witnesses can confirm Calderax left with the package well before the rift appeared
- Shipping manifest lists the sender as "Marquet Fine Arts Collective" - a front organization
- One of Dreyara's agents (disguised as a dockworker) will be watching the party's investigation
- Small fragments of obsidian-like material near the rift that resonate when brought close to similar material

# Potential Hooks & Complications

- The rift is slowly growing larger and may release more creatures if not addressed
- Calderax will return looking for the shard when he discovers it missing
- Aethor Kalisk's arrival may escalate tensions, especially if the Cobalt Soul representative is present
- The corruption seems to be spreading beyond the affected guards to nearby organic materials
- A cryptic message found on one of the corrupted guards: "Beware the seven pieces, for when united..."

# DM Notes

## For the Gorillafish Encounter

- The creature is disoriented and terrified, making it especially dangerous
- It will prioritize attacking those who approach the rift (as if protecting it)
- If reduced below 25% health, it may attempt to flee back through the rift

### Description for Players

As you approach the chaotic scene at the pier, you spot a horrifying creature unlike anything you've seen before. Standing nearly eight feet tall on massive gorilla-like arms, its body is a grotesque fusion of simian and aquatic features. 

The creature that you see on the dock moves with a lurching, unnatural gait. It resembles a gorilla in its basic form—a hulking figure that walks on knuckles and powerful hind legs—but every aspect of its appearance feels wrong, as if designed by something that had only heard gorillas described in whispered stories.

Its translucent skin glistens with a constant sheen of mucus, revealing the horrific arrangement of organs beneath. Muscles shift and realign with each movement in ways that defy normal anatomy. Its proportions are distended—arms too long, fingers that sometimes seem to multiply when they flex, a back that hunches at an impossible angle.

The creature's face bears a twisted resemblance to a silverback's, but the jaw hangs slightly slack, revealing glimpses of concentric tooth rings within. Most unsettling are its eyes—too large and bulging with an internal light that pulses in hypnotic patterns. As it moves, parts of its body seem to momentarily lose cohesion, rippling like water before reforming.

## Party Important Discoveries

1. One party member should recognize the corruption is similar to that happening in the Savalirwood
2. The "art piece" was actually one of the Lorestone shards they are looking for
3. Calderax picked up the shard shortly after the ship docked

## Maintaining Ambiguity

- Allow the party to believe there's a connection between the shard and the rift
- Multiple NPCs will reinforce this false connection through their own beliefs
- The true coincidental nature of events can remain unknown to enhance the mystery

# Session Rewards Reference

## Reward Categories

### Information & Knowledge
- **Secrets revealed**: Learn villain's plan, discover hidden weakness, uncover conspiracy
- **Lore discovered**: Ancient history, prophecy details, how magic item works
- **Intelligence gained**: Location of target, schedule of events, security details
- **NPC backgrounds**: Learn someone's true identity, motivations, or past
- **Map or directions**: To hidden location, shortcut, secret entrance
- **Decoded message**: Translation of ancient text, cypher key, magical formula

### Social & Relationships
- **NPC alliance**: Someone now owes them, will help in future, provides safe haven
- **Favor earned**: Can call in later for assistance, information, or resources
- **Reputation increase**: Known as heroes, feared, respected in community
- **Faction standing**: Promoted in rank, given more responsibility, trusted with secrets
- **Enemy made ally**: Former opponent now neutral or friendly
- **Introduction/access**: Meet important NPC, granted audience with leader

### Access & Opportunities
- **Location access**: Granted entry to restricted area, given key/password
- **New quest offered**: Hook to next adventure, optional side quest
- **Training opportunity**: Learn new skill, fighting technique, spell
- **Job/position offered**: Hired as guards, investigators, advisors
- **Invitation extended**: To exclusive event, meeting, organization
- **Safe haven established**: Place to rest, hide, store items

### Resources & Assets
- **Useful item/tool**: Non-magical but valuable (spyglass, disguise kit, cart)
- **Service rendered**: Free healing, repairs, transport, lodging
- **Contact established**: Someone who can get things, knows things, goes places
- **Property gained**: Small building, ship, land deed
- **Business opportunity**: Investment, trade route, merchant connection
- **Mount or companion**: Horse, familiar, trained animal

### Progression & Power
- **Magic item** (traditional): Weapon, armor, wondrous item
- **Consumable magic**: Potions, scrolls, single-use items
- **Item upgrade**: Existing equipment improved or enhanced
- **Spell/ability learned**: New technique from mentor
- **Experience/insight**: Tactical advantage, knowledge of enemy weakness
- **Prophecy/vision**: Glimpse of future, divine guidance

### Political & Influence
- **Legal authority**: Badge, writ, royal decree granting power
- **Pardon/clemency**: Crimes forgiven, warrants dropped
- **Title/recognition**: Knighthood, official position, honorific
- **Political favor**: Owed by official, can influence decision
- **Witness testimony**: Someone will vouch for them, provide alibi
- **Blackmail material**: Leverage over NPC or faction

### Time & Circumstance
- **Time saved**: Shortcut discovered, faster travel method
- **Complication removed**: Problem solved, obstacle cleared
- **Enemy delayed**: Villain's plans disrupted, bought time
- **Warning received**: Advance notice of danger, ambush avoided
- **Resource preserved**: Didn't expend expected supplies, spell slots, etc.
- **Rest opportunity**: Safe place to recover when none expected

## Combining Rewards
Mix types for satisfying results:
- Gold + information about where to spend it
- Magic item + NPC who can identify/teach its use
- Reputation + new quest because of that reputation
- Access + contact who can guide them there

## Adjusting Value
**Minor reward**: One clear benefit, immediately useful
**Moderate reward**: Multiple benefits or significant long-term value
**Major reward**: Game-changing access, power, or resources

## Quick Selection Method
1. What did players just accomplish?
2. What would logically result from that?
3. What moves the story forward?
4. What would players find satisfying?

Pick 2-3 rewards of different types per significant accomplishment.

**Party Level:** 7
**Party Size:** 6 (Dechs, Drawg, Hesterian, Popcorn, Qilynn, Seraphina)

## Round 1 Damage Estimate

Estimated party alpha strike (all attacks hit, no crits): ~110–130

This is the number the [[Encounter Design Guide#The Delete Test|Delete Test]] checks against.

## Action Economy

Party actions per round: 6 (plus Dechs's iguana companion)
Notable bonus actions: Hunter's Mark (Dechs), Polearm Master attack (Drawg), Cunning Action (Qilynn)
Key reactions: Polearm Master OA (Drawg), Battle Master maneuvers (Drawg), Repelling Blast repositioning (Seraphina), Evasion (Qilynn)

## Concentration Spells in Regular Use

- **Bless** (Popcorn via Prayer Beads) — +1d4 attacks/saves for 3 targets
- **Call Lightning** (Popcorn) — ongoing 3d10 per round, the party's best sustained damage
- **Hunter's Mark** (Dechs) — +1d6 per hit, moves on kill

Pressuring concentration with multi-hit attacks or repeated small damage sources is the single most effective way to reduce party output.

**Gnome Cunning:** Popcorn has advantage on INT, WIS, and CHA saves against magic. Targeting him with magical effects is unreliable.

## Structural Vulnerabilities

- **One front-liner.** Drawg (AC 18, HP 60) is the only PC who can absorb sustained melee. If he goes down or is removed from combat (banishment, restrain, fear), the party has no front line.
- **Dechs is fragile.** HP 30 at level 7 is roughly half the party average. One focused round drops him, and losing Hunter's Mark + companion actions is a significant output loss.
- **Control-dependent.** The party relies on Hesterian's debuffs, Popcorn's Call Lightning, and Seraphina's Repelling Blast to manage threats. Silence, counterspell, or anti-magic effects hurt disproportionately.
- **Limited healing.** No dedicated healer. Seraphina has some Celestial healing, Popcorn has some druid healing, but neither is primary. Sustained damage grinds them down.

## What Creates Tension

*Update based on table experience.*

- Threats to Drawg specifically (he's load-bearing)
- Multiple threats requiring split attention
- Enemies that close on Dechs or Hesterian
- Concentration pressure on Bless/Call Lightning
- Environments that limit Repelling Blast's effectiveness (no edges, no hazards to push into)

## What They Trivialize

*Update based on table experience.*

- Single-target enemies with no secondary threats
- Enemies that stand still in open terrain with clear sightlines
- Anything that relies primarily on WIS saves against the full party
- Low-AC, high-HP enemies (focus fire shreds them)

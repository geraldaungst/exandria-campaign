# CLOUDFANG KEEP & MONASTERY - DM GUIDE

## CURRENT SITUATION

**Party Status:**

- 5 level 6 PCs currently in Library with Lady Emer
- Multiple players intend to attack her
- Combat likely next session

**Lady Emer (CR 11 Medusa - Modified):**

- Surrender threshold: ~40 HP (30-40% health)
- NOT loyal to Qalix - paid consultant
- Will try to warn Dreyara if given opportunity
- Pragmatic: values survival over employment

---

## PHASE 1: LADY EMER COMBAT & SURRENDER

### Combat Tactics

- Falls back to Stone Garden (area E5) when losing
- Warns Dreyara via bird if left alone for a round or more
- Uses petrified statues for Stone Sacrifice trait
- Lair action: Rift Pulse (custom planar-themed)
- Fights strategically but NOT to death

### Surrender (at ~40 HP)

> "Stop! I yield! You want information? The prisoner? My research? Take it all. Just spare my life."

**She Will Reveal (Truthfully):**

- Tier 1 (Freely): Qalix hired her 14 months ago, Dreyara is his spymaster, Varnes captured for Qalix's interrogation, basic Lorestone research
- Tier 2 (Pressure): Dreyara expected "soon" (doesn't know exact timing), mentioned "Rexxentrum contacts," travels with 2-3 people usually
- Tier 3 (Zone of Truth): Former Volstrucker, Qalix's general location, other researchers, Dreyara's capabilities, has trained messenger creatures

**She Will NOT Reveal (Doesn't Know):**

- Qalix's true goal (Draconia restoration)
- Other shard locations
- That "temporal resonance crystal" is Luxon Beacon
- That her pearl is Lawbearer's Tear
- Specific Rexxentrum heist timeline

---

## PHASE 2: PARTY CHOICES FOR LADY EMER

### A. Kill Her

- No warning possible to Dreyara
- Dreyara arrives to silent Keep → withdraws cautiously
- No additional intel from interrogation

### B. Release Her

- Flees immediately
- Sends warning to Dreyara once safe
- Dreyara arrives fully prepared or avoids Keep

### C. Hold Her Prisoner

- **She WILL try to warn Dreyara**
- See "Warning Methods" below

### D. Try to Hire Her (Unlikely)

- Requires 50,000+ gp AND protection from Qalix
- Persuasion DC 20 (with resources) or DC 25 (without)
- Most parties cannot afford this

## LADY EMER'S WARNING METHODS

**Most Likely: Trained Creature**

- Messenger birds (ravens/crows) trained to fly to specific locations
- Colored ribbon system (red = danger, green = safe)
- Surviving guardian sent to intercept Dreyara
- **Party Perception DC 14**: Notice bird release
- **Animal Handling DC 16**: Intercept/prevent

**Backup: Prearranged Signal**

- Lantern pattern (2 lights = danger)
- Colored cloth in window
- Object arrangement visible from approach
- **Party Perception DC 15**: Notice odd behavior
- **Investigation DC 18**: Understand it's a signal

**Last Resort: Code Words**

- If Dreyara arrives before warning sent
- Specific phrases in "normal" conversation
- "The weather has been _particularly cold_ lately"
- **Party Insight DC 22**: Realize she's using codes

### Preventing Warning

**Effective Countermeasures:**

- Kill all creatures during assault (no messengers)
- Keep her away from windows/exterior
- Watch for creature-calling (whistles, commands)
- Gag her (prevents verbal commands)
- Zone of Truth (forces honesty about methods)

## PHASE 3: VARNES DWELL INFORMATION

**After Being Freed (Immediately):**

> "Listen carefully. I don't have much time before I need to rest.
> 
> Six months ago, the Cobalt Soul hired me to assess security at their Rexxentrum Archive. During that work, I discovered a Lorestone shard they were keeping secret - Shard Five. The Archivist, Ovedo, paid me extremely well for my discretion.
> 
> That's why Dreyara captured me. Qalix learned I had worked at the Archive. They've been trying to extract the information under torture and memory magic. I held out, but I don't know what I revealed.
> 
> If they know about Shard Five, Dreyara will move on Rexxentrum. Soon. That shard is in danger.
> 
> I know there are other things you might want here - that dagger, that confrontation, information from her. But you can't protect everything. You have to choose.
> 
> I'll be fine here for a few days. The others too. This Keep is secure now. But you? You need to move. Fast."

**What This Creates:**

- ✅ Urgent reason to leave immediately (Rexxentrum timeline)
- ✅ Acknowledges Drawg's dagger quest (not dismissing it)
- ✅ Tactical framing (protect shard > personal revenge)
- ✅ Removes guilt about leaving NPCs (they'll be fine)

## PHASE 4: DREYARA'S ARRIVAL & DECISION

**Timeline:**

- Party arrives Keep: Day 16
- Party defeats Lady Emer: Day 16-17
- Dreyara arrives Keep: Day 18

### Dreyara's Approach (Standard Protocol)

**Step 1: Advance Scout (200-300 yards ahead)**

- Spots dead owlbears at entrance
- Returns to report: "Keep is compromised"

**Step 2: Team Assessment**

- Dead guardians = serious threat
- Silent keep = Lady Emer dead/captured/fled
- **This happens regardless of warning**

**Step 3: Decision Point**

**WITHOUT Lady Emer's Warning:**

- Unknown hostiles, uncertain situation
- Possible Lady Emer survived
- Worth investigating cautiously
- **Decision: Send scout to investigate carefully**
- Still very cautious, ready to retreat

**WITH Lady Emer's Warning:**

- Confirmed: powerful adventurers freed Varnes
- Lady Emer captured/dead
- They're likely waiting for ambush
- **Decision: Withdraw immediately, flee east**

### Dreyara's Professional Choice

**She Flees East (Not Track)**

**Why:**

- Has Shard 6 (protecting it is priority #1)
- Qalix has resources to respond with force
- If Varnes freed → he knows about Shard 5
- Must warn Qalix immediately
- Personal tracking = risking the shard

**Her Route:**

- Fastest path east toward civilization
- Will report to Qalix via Sending or in person
- Taking mountain pass (same direction as party, different path)

## PHASE 5: AVALANCHE & MONASTERY CONVERGENCE

### Timeline

**Day 18: Dreyara Flees East**

- Withdraws from Keep immediately
- Takes fastest route with Shard 6
- Traveling with 2 companions

**Day 18-19: AVALANCHE (First Strike)**

- Hits Dreyara's group in mountain pass
- **Both companions killed/buried**
- Dreyara survives: ~70 HP (lost 30-40), 1 exhaustion
- Path forward blocked, path back blocked
- Spots Monastery - only shelter for miles
- Takes refuge inside

**Day 19-20: Party Travels East**

- Leaves Keep (immediately or after 1-2 day wait)
- Heads toward Rexxentrum
- Encounters avalanche site

**Day 19-20: Fresh Snowfall**

- New snow overnight covers debris
- Party sees: fresh snow, impassable road
- Bodies partially exposed in snow

### Avalanche Site Discovery

> The mountain pass narrows here. The way forward is buried under tons of snow and ice. Recent avalanche - maybe a day or two ago.

**Perception DC 12:** Spot bodies in snow (2-3 visible)  
**Perception DC 15:** Professional travelers, good gear, Empire-style  
**Investigation DC 14:** Killed 24-36 hours ago, tracks lead toward Monastery before fresh snow covered them

**Key Information:**

- 2-3 bodies visible (Dreyara's companions)
- Someone survived and went toward Monastery
- Road ahead: completely blocked
- Road back: days of backtracking
- **Monastery visible: only shelter in area**

## PHASE 6: MONASTERY ENCOUNTER

### Dreyara's Status When Party Arrives

**Physical:**

- ~70 HP (wounded from avalanche)
- 1 level exhaustion
- Fresh phase spider wounds (been fighting)
- Torn/bloodied clothing, frostbite
- 2-3 healing potions remaining

**Mental:**

- Alone, wounded, outnumbered
- Needs to survive to complete mission (deliver Shard 6)
- Pragmatic: temporary alliance better than death
- Still carrying Shard 6 (hidden) and Blade of Maroth Fenn (visible)

**Knowledge:**

- Phase spiders infest Monastery (learned hard way)
- They phase through walls unpredictably
- Many of them throughout structure
- Been looking for exit, hasn't found safe route

### Discovery Scene Options

**Option A: Party Enters, Finds Her Resting**

> Heavy doors groan open. Ancient stone halls stretch into darkness. As you move deeper, you hear labored breathing.
> 
> Around a corner: a woman sits against the wall, dark hair matted with blood and snow. Cloak torn, one arm cradled. And strapped to that arm - Drawg recognizes it - your blade.
> 
> Her eyes snap open. Green eyes, sharp despite exhaustion. She sees you. Hand moves toward dagger.
> 
> Then freezes.
> 
> From deeper in the Monastery: _click-click-click-click_
> 
> Her hand drops away. "Tell me you have a way out of here."

**Option B: Both Encounter During Phase Spider Attack**

> Combat sounds ahead. Steel on chitin. A woman's voice, sharp commands.
> 
> You round corner: woman fighting phase spider, backing away, wounded and exhausted. Spider is winning. She hasn't seen you yet.
> 
> Then _click-click-click_ from behind YOU. Another spider, phasing through wall.
> 
> Woman spots your group. Recognition flashes - and Drawg sees: your blade, strapped to her arm.
> 
> Two spiders. Two groups. One room.

**Option C: Evidence First, Then Discovery**

> You find signs of recent occupation:
> 
> - Bloodied bandages, still damp
> - Empty ration wrappers
> - Bedroll, still warm
> - Phase spider ichor and fresh sword cuts
> 
> Investigation checks reveal: injured person, alone, been fighting spiders, systematically exploring for exit
> 
> You follow the trail...

## PARTY CHOICES AT MONASTERY

### Immediate Options

**1. Attack Dreyara on Sight**

- She's wounded, outnumbered, exhausted
- But phase spiders hear combat
- Multi-front battle: Party vs Dreyara vs Spiders

**2. Negotiate Temporary Truce**

- Both need to survive/escape
- Common enemy (phase spiders)
- Both going east (different reasons)
- Temporary alliance until escape

**3. Ignore/Avoid Each Other**

- Explore separately
- Risky - spiders are numerous
- Possible but difficult

**4. Attempt Capture While Wounded**

- She's vulnerable now
- Could interrogate about Qalix
- Could demand blade/shard
- Spider threat complicates this

**5. Demand Blade/Information**

- Drawg's personal quest
- Leverage: she needs help surviving
- She's pragmatic, might negotiate

### Dreyara's Calculation

> "These are enemies of Qalix. Killed Lady Emer, freed Varnes. Under normal circumstances, fight or flee.
> 
> But: wounded, alone, outnumbered, trapped in spider nest. They're fresh and numerous.
> 
> Attack = I die.  
> Flee = Where? I die to spiders.  
> Cooperate = Maybe we all survive. Then deal with enmity later.
> 
> Qalix needs Shard 6. Can't complete mission if dead. Temporary alliance."

**She Will:**

- Propose temporary truce (survival first)
- Share limited information about spiders
- Fight alongside party if necessary
- Watch for opportunity to escape once safe
- Try to reach Rexxentrum first

**She Will NOT:**

- Reveal Shard 6 location (keeps it hidden)
- Voluntarily give up blade
- Trust party fully
- Give away Qalix's operation details

- Avalanche hits her Day 18-19

- Dreyara wounded and alone

## CRITICAL REMINDERS

**Dead Guardians = No Surprise**

- Dreyara's scout spots them from 200+ yards
- She knows Keep is compromised regardless of warning
- Ambush at Keep is extremely unlikely to work

**Dreyara Has Shard 6**

- This is her #1 priority to protect
- Will flee east to secure it rather than track party
- Professional choice: preserve critical asset

**Natural Constraints Feel Fair**

- Avalanche = nature/weather, not DM forcing
- Monastery = logical shelter choice, not mandatory
- Phase spiders = environmental threat, not plot device

**Preserve Player Agency**

- They choose: how to handle Lady Emer
- They choose: how to approach Dreyara at Monastery
- They choose: whether to cooperate with enemy
- They choose: what happens after escape

**The Interesting Question**

- Not "Do we go to Monastery?" (obvious yes)
- But "What do we do about Dreyara?" (multiple valid answers)

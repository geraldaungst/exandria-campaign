## Cart 1 - The Halsen Family & Friends

**Marta Halsen** (human, 42, leader)

- Former merchant, organized the group's departure
- Practical, decisive, protective of her family
- Has the Deastock connection (brother-in-law Torvald at White Peak Inn)

**Henrik Halsen** (human, 45, Marta's husband)

- Carpenter, maintains the carts
- Quiet but capable, defers to Marta on decisions

**Sofi Halsen** (human, 16, daughter)

- Helps with cooking and caring for younger children
- Nervous about the journey but trying to be brave

**Grig Ironfoot** (dwarf, 58)

- Former miner, friend of the Halsens
- Lost his mine to Imperial requisition, bitter about it
- Strong, experienced with mountain travel

**Lena Kos** (human, 67, widow)

- Herbalist, stayed with the Halsens after her husband died
- Frail but knowledgeable about medicine
- Would appreciate the party's healing help

## Cart 2 - The Vorn Family & Associated

**Dmitri Vorn** (human, 35)

- Miller whose business was seized for "war effort"
- Angry at the Empire, suspicious of strangers initially
- Drives the second cart

**Kaela Vorn** (human, 32, Dmitri's wife)

- Pragmatic, keeps Dmitri's anger in check
- Good with finances, managing their resources

**Twin children: Anya and Pavel Vorn** (human, 8)

- Curious about everything, especially the party's gear
- Provide some levity but also vulnerability to the group

**Oskar Brandt** (halfling, 28)

- Baker's apprentice, traveling with the Vorns
- Cheerful despite circumstances, tries to keep spirits up
- Has interesting gossip from Zadash (worked near the markets)

**Thea Brandt** (halfling, 26, Oskar's wife, pregnant)

- Why they're fleeing - doesn't want child born in war-time Empire
- Slows the group slightly, needs extra care

**Jorah Kest** (human, 19)

- Stable hand, helping with the horses
- Young, inexperienced, but eager to prove himself
- Might want to hear adventuring stories from the party

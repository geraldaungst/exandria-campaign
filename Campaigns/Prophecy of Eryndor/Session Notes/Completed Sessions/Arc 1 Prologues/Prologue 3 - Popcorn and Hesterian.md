## The Culprit

**Saboteur & Motive:** [[Garnet Thorne]], the competitive local brewer, has been nursing a grudge against [[Tamsin Fieldgold]], the beekeeper, over a land dispute. With the Festival of Renewal approaching, [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] is infuriated to learn that her ales and [[Tamsin Fieldgold|Tamsin]]'s honey-infused meads will be featured side by side. Desperate to prove the superiority of her brews and overshadow [[Tamsin Fieldgold|Tamsin]]'s rising popularity, [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] concocts a plan to sabotage [[Tamsin Fieldgold|Tamsin]]'s meads during the festival.

[[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] secretly obtains a bittering agent known for its potency and plans to discreetly add it to [[Tamsin Fieldgold|Tamsin]]'s meads before the tasting event. Unknown to [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]], the bittering agent is also highly flammable. When she goes to add it to the hot spiced mead, some of it spills onto the fire beneath. The bittering agent ignites, sparking a chain reaction as several of the nearby lanterns catch fire and spread quickly through the crowd.

## The Aftermath

At some point, the rival will beg for leniency and offer the PCs a valuable map. She believes it to be a map to a treasure, but it will turn out to be a [[Map to the Blooming Grove]].

## Festival of Renewal

## Arrive at Plot

### Social

The Festival of Renewal in Whitestone is a beloved event marking the end of winter and the promise of spring. It's a time for the community to come together, celebrate life, and let go of past sorrows. A highlight of the festival is the Lantern Release, where people write their hopes and regrets on paper lanterns and release them into the night sky. This year, the festival has attracted visitors from far and wide, including [[Hesterian Shyr (Dot)|Hesterian]] and [[Popcorn Zikelfikel (Viv)|Popcorn]], who find themselves in Whitestone, each for their own reasons.

As the festival begins, the town of Whitestone is alive with music, laughter, and the aroma of festive foods. Colorful lanterns and banners line the streets, and performers entertain the crowds. A light snowfall dusts the ground as winter clings to the city, not quite ready to leave.

### Combat

The Lantern Release is about to start. The townsfolk gather in the main square, holding their lanterns. [[Hesterian Shyr (Dot)|Hesterian]] and [[Popcorn Zikelfikel (Viv)|Popcorn]], each holding their lanterns, find themselves standing near each other. As the mayor gives a [[Lady Vex'ahlia's Speech|speech about renewal and hope]], a series of small, mysterious explosions suddenly disrupt the ceremony. Lanterns catch fire prematurely, causing panic and confusion. A scream pierces the night as someone is injured in the chaos.

## Journey Into Plot

### Combat

[[Hesterian Shyr (Dot)|Hesterian]] and [[Popcorn Zikelfikel (Viv)|Popcorn]] must investigate the explosions and find clues. They can use skill checks to investigate the pattern of flames. Here are clues they discover:

- [x] The lantern fires were sparked in multiple locations, but seem to center on the food court.
- [ ] The burn pattern at the "Bee's Bounty" stall seems to indicate an accelerant was used.
- [x] A curled up and burned piece of paper near the Bee's Bounty seems to have an image of a flower.

In addition, a group of ruffians seems intent on taking advantage of the chaos to loot some of the vendor stalls. They are trying to blend into the crowd, but if cornered they will fight at first until they can escape. If escape seems unlikely they will surrender rather than die. Any ruffians who are interrogated will know some of the clues in the next section.

### Social

Part of the investigation will involve talking to townsfolk in the market.

Clues they can find out by talking to vendors and passersby (in this order):

- [x] Most of the injuries were minor, but someone got burned badly and was taken to see [[Steward Korvin Grrgg]] for healing.
- [x] Rumors are going around that unlike previous years, the festival award for "Best Brew" is actually competitive this year.
- [x] Three brews are in the running: Spring's Whisper Blonde Ale, Spice 'n Easy Mead, and Lionett Reserve Burgundy (a new entrant from distant Wildemount)
- [x] The usual favorite, [[Garnet Thorne]], seems off her game, though her festival brew is one of the best she has ever produced.
- [x] Tasting the brew (or talking to someone who has tried it) will reveal that the characteristic flavor in it comes from the rare Darkember berries, which grow in certain parts of the Parchwood in late fall. When the dried berries are properly prepared, the oil they produce adds a pleasantly smooth and mildly spicy flavor to the brew.
- [x] Several customers were overheard returning mead to Bee's Bounty claiming it was spoiled.
- [x] A young man was seen nearby the Bee's Bounty yesterday and again today.
- [x] The young man was also seen stacking kegs at Rose and Thorne's stall.
- [x] One vendor overheard heated arguments between [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] and local officials about zoning permits related to land use near her brewery.
- [x] In questioning, some vendors reference past petty crimes or business practices just bordering legality tied to [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]]'s establishment.
- [x] One vendor mentions complaints submitted about [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]]'s attempts to undercut competitors by spreading rumors to sully their reputations.
- [x] Long-time residents talk about how [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] took over the struggling family brewery after her parents died at a young age, sacrificing greatly keep it afloat.
- [x] [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]]'s mentor who originally taught her brewing was Old Man Godfrid - a beloved figure who also took [[Tamsin Fieldgold|Tamsin]] under his wing back in the day.
- [x] Old Man Godfrid died about two years ago. His death hit both of them hard. This was right around the time the land dispute began, if memory serves.
- [x] The land dispute stems from confusion over a hand-drawn inheritance map from Old Man Godfrid granting usage rights to certain acreage. Both interpreted it differently, fueling this conflict.

If the party goes to see [[Steward Korvin Grrgg|Korvin]], they will learn:

- [x] the burned person is [[Harren Burtlet]].
- [x] Burtlet has an empty vial on him with traces of the bittering agent.
- [x] A Nature check (DC 12) will reveal that one of the ingredients in the vial is Darkember berries. When the dried berries and seeds are ground into a powder and dissolved in alcohol they produce a very bitter and mildly toxic substance. This is commonly used to train house pets to stay away from specific locations or objects, but put into food would make it taste terrible.

## Kill Plot and Win

### Social

Players will eventually come across [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] one way or another. [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] may reveal some or all of this information, depending on how the encounter plays out:

- [x] [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] Lost Her Family Young: Both [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]]'s mother and father tragically died from an infectious outbreak when she was just 13.
- [x] Forced to Mature Beyond Her Years: With no other family left, [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] inherited the family's struggling small brewery business as just a teenager.
- [x] Predatory Partner Exploited Her: Naive about business, she obtained financing help from a cunning investor named Kalzar Morsov who seized more and more control until [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] was a silent partner in her own brewery.
- [x] Kalzar was Manipulative and Abusive: Kalzar would verbally insult and intimidate teenage [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]]. But outwardly he was charming to the village, keeping his abuse hidden.
- [x] She Escaped when Old Enough: When she turned 17, [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] found the courage to stand up to Kalzar. She regained control but the brewery was in dire financial straits.
- [x] Built Business with Mentor's Guidance: Old Man Godfrid, a longtime family friend, helped revive the brewery's reputation. But [[Garnet Thorne|Garnet]] still struggles with this traumatic past and pressure to succeed.

[[Qilynn Duskwhisper (Nicole)|Qilynn]] and the artificer first crossed paths during a chaotic night in [[Port Damali]] when a street brawl erupted. [[Qilynn Duskwhisper (Nicole)|Qilynn]], skilled in stealth and quick reflexes, helped [[Laucian Greycastle (Brent)|Laucian]] escape a tricky situation. Grateful for the assistance, [[Laucian Greycastle (Brent)|Laucian]] proposed a partnership, realizing their combined abilities could be advantageous in navigating the city's underworld. Since then, their bond has grown through shared adventures and a mutual understanding of each other's strengths.

The street brawl was ignited when a notorious gang attempted to extort the artificer who had inadvertently acquired a rare and sought-after [[Campaigns/Prophecy of Eryndor/Session Notes/Completed Sessions/Arc 1 Prologues/Laucian's Artifact|artifact]]. Unwilling to comply, he resisted, leading to a confrontation. [[Qilynn Duskwhisper (Nicole)|Qilynn]], passing by, noticed the artificer in trouble and intervened not initiating the conflict but joining to help a fellow resident of [[Port Damali]]. This shared encounter became the foundation for their alliance and subsequent adventures.

# Arrive at Plot
The PCs sneak into unused storage successfully at first. They hear odd animal sounds coming from a nearby crate. One way or another, the animal escapes (either due to the players investigating and releasing it, or the animal breaking free.)

# Journey Into Plot
Pursuing the creature may lead them into other parts of the ship where they have to either fake their way past crew or passengers, or stay hidden. The animal will get into trouble; one or more of the following:
- [ ] The [monkey](obsidian://open?vault=D%26D%20Compendium&file=bestiary%2Ftwo-headed-etcheda-monkey) attempts to drag off a crew member up into the rigging. It will maul the helpless sailor if not scared off or defeated quickly. Need to rescue them!
- [ ] It sneaks into the first-class passenger area, flashing its crystals and overturning furnishings, breaking glass. Well-dressed nobles flee in a hilarious panic from the bizarre creature.
- [ ] The [[Guzezzik|ship's cook]] screams from the galley about a devilish beast trying to steal meat cleavers and hot pans to attack with. The monkey shows unexpected tool use intelligence.
- [ ] It swipes a passenger's expensive hat, returning to perch on top of a swaying mast while playfully tossing the coveted headpiece back and forth between its heads. How to get it back?

If players do not immediately pursue the beast, one of the following may also occur:
- [ ] The crew needs all hands on deck to keep the ship stable amid brewing storm conditions. With chaotic winds and light rain, they can't spare members to hunt the loose creature.
- [ ] The captain orders crew to safeguard valuable noble passengers in secured quarters. But with skeleton staffing and duties, the less familiar PCs can better sneak after the monkey undiscovered.
- [ ] The captain refuses to harm the creature since a noble passenger claims ownership, but can't spare his essential crew from running the ship. So he secretly hires incognito "raiders" (the PCs) with no direct affiliation as his most viable solution.

# Discover Not Plot
They discover that the animal has a collar and is therefore a pet, not a wild creature. The collar is quite expensive, pointing to one of the wealthier passengers. They can also discover this if they examine the crate from which it broke out (which has the destination paperwork inside stating who it is from and where it is going).

# Journey to New Plot
 They need to decide how to handle this new information about the creature--do they kill it, risking the wrath and retribution of the owner, do they capture it and return it, do they turn the person in for illegal smuggling?

# Kill Plot and Win
This could resolve in multiple ways:
- [ ] The [[Lady Triotta Thessaly|noble passenger]] who owned the creature gives the PCs a written letter of marque to show any authorities, pardoning their stowaway status in gratitude, but it requires them to come back to [[Port Damali]] immediately to deliver a [[Letter to Celdric Ambril|message]] to the vendor from whom the noble received the animal (who will be [[Celdric Ambril]]).
- [ ] The [[Captain Thyakas|captain]] is thankful for their assistance and is willing to overlook their crime of stowing away if they will serve a "penance" as crew on the return journey since many of the current crew will be disembarking and she needs replacements.
- [ ] A passenger the PCs assisted offers patronage and urges them to come back aboard as their bodyguards. In return the passenger will cover the fee for their passage in both directions, thereby negating the charge of stowing away.

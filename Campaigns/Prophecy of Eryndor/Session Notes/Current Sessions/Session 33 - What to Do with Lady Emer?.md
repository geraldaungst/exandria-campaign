# Session Prep (1-2 hours)

## Strong Start Options

**Resolve Insight Check First** (RECOMMENDED) Open with: "Drawg, you wanted to Insight check Lady Emer's statement 'I'm just a researcher.' Go ahead and roll."

Then after result, turn to room: "Lady Emer lies beneath the immovable shield, hogtied and blindfolded, waiting for your next question. What do you do?"

**Insight Check DCs**: See [[Lady Emer Conversation Progression#Insight Check "I'm just a researcher"]]

- DC 10-12: She seems sincere
- DC 13-16: She's straightforward, genuinely views herself this way
- DC 17-19: Professional pride evident, thinks highly of her expertise
- DC 20+: Superiority complex - she believes she's more important than her employer

### Wyvern Taming (Drawg's Side Quest)

**Simple Skill Challenge:**

- Goal: 4 successes before 3 failures
- Starting DC: 20 (already accounts for Drawg's dragon nature)
- Each success after the first lowers DC by 2 for next attempt
- Two consecutive failures increases the DC by 2
- Timeframe: Hours, not days

**Tracking:**

- Successes: ☐☐☐☐
- Failures: ☐☐☐

**Sample Actions & Checks:**

- Speak with Animals + offering food: Animal Handling DC [current]
- Demonstrating strength: Athletics contested or Intimidation DC [current]
- Treating wounds/grooming: Medicine or Animal Handling DC [current]
- Protecting from danger: Automatic success
- Sharing kill from hunt: Survival + Animal Handling DC [current]

**Advantage Only If:**

- Exceptionally creative approach
- Another party member meaningfully assists

**Status Updates:**

- After success: "The wyvern's hostility lessens. It's starting to trust you."
- After failure: "The wyvern pulls back, unconvinced. You haven't lost ground, but this didn't help."
- At 2 failures: "The wyvern is growing wary. One more misstep might end this."

**Current Wyvern State:**

- Well cared for, not desperate (just ready for next meal)
- Attitude: Hostile (protective of Emer, uncertain about these strangers)
- If Emer alive: May be easier to sway with her help
- If Emer dead: More resistant initially (+2 to first 3 DCs) but ultimately more available

**Wyvern dialogue snippets (Int 5, simple thoughts):**

- "Soft things run. Fun chase."
- "Snakeface brings meat. Good meat."
- "Snakeface kind to me. Give me play things."
- "Mine. My sky. My rocks."
- "Hurt tail-biters before. They stay away now." [referring to basilisks learning to give her space]
- "Warm here. Safe here. Many meals."
- "You smell wrong. Not-food?" [confusion at being talked to]
- "Boring. Boring. Boring... FOOD!" [her typical day]
- "Sting makes them slow. Easy."

- Lady Emer CANNOT escape in current state (blind, manacled, immobilized)

# The Lorestone of Eryndor

## Current Shard Locations
- **Shards 1-4:** Lyren Willowwhisper (Obsidian Echoforge)
- **Shard 5:** Archivist Ovedo at Rexxentrum Archive (Cobalt Soul)
- **Shard 6:** Currently held by [[Dreyara Drimvar]] or one of her minions
- **Shard 7**: In the hoard of [[Shoagragoth, The Acid Crown]] (not yet known by the party). This is the shard that [[Keldar Stonefoot]] will point the party towards if they get that far.
- **Shard 8**: Unknown

## Assembling Process
1. **Cleansing:** Requires elemental plane components to remove chaos "noise"
2. **Harmonizing:** Synchronize shards to resonate together
3. **Weaving:** Tie shards with threads of fate
4. **Assembly:** Channel planar rift energy to bind parts together

## Competing Factions
- **Obsidian Echoforge:** Wants to assemble stone to close rifts
- **Malachite Cord:** Believes assembling the stone would be catastrophic
- **Emissaries of the Sunfall:** Formerly enemies of OEF, now working with them
- **Vaud Qalix:** Wants stone to control rifts, restore Draconia, gain power
- **Cerberus Assembly:** Connection to Hestarian's missing sibling, may have interest in the Lorestone

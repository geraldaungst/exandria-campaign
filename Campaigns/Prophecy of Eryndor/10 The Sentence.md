- [[Ceratos|Ceratos]] wants a permanent passage from the Far Realm into Exandria, and is having difficulty because it cannot touch the rifts itself, its warlock thralls don't yet understand them well enough to connect them, and the PCs keep closing the rifts they find.
	- It doesn't care whether its thralls stabilize a rift or tear it wider, only that the result lets it through.

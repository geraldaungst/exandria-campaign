# Adaptable Modules for Your Wildemount Campaign

_Side Quests and Plot Components for Levels 9-20_

## Overview

This guide focuses on **third-party content** that can be adapted to fit your campaign's themes: dimensional rifts, faction conflict, artifact recovery, moral complexity, and the Far Realm threat. Content is organized around your shard recovery arc and adjusted for your endgame timeline (Qalix confrontation at level 18, Ceratos at 19-20).

**Note on WotC Content:** Most official WotC modules have been removed since your player is likely to recognize them. The few that remain are included only where they're uniquely suited and heavily disguisable.

## Shard Recovery Setpieces

These modules align with your established Lorestone shard locations and the setpiece adventures they'll require.

### Shard 5 — Rexxentrum (Cobalt Soul Archive)

**Challenge Level:** 10-12  
**Current Holder:** Archivist Ovedo  
**Status:** Secret possession, actively researching

Depending on how players approach this, you may need content for investigation, negotiation, or complications:

#### The Candlekeep Murders (DMs Guild, Level 10)

When the Keeper of Tomes is murdered on Deadwinter's Eve, all of the Great Readers become suspects. Dark forces work to uncover the library's Vault of Secrets.

**Adaptation:** Reskin Candlekeep as the Rexxentrum Archive. Ovedo is the murder victim (or the prime suspect), and the "Vault of Secrets" contains the shard. The party must navigate Cobalt Soul politics while uncovering the real killer—possibly an Emissary of the Sunfall or Malachite Cord operative trying to prevent or steal the shard.

**Why it works:** Library setting is a direct match, investigation format preserves mystery, and the political intrigue mirrors Cobalt Soul's scholarly factions. The "murder mystery in an archive" format is generic enough that even a player familiar with Candlekeep Mysteries wouldn't immediately recognize it.

#### 12 Peculiar Towers (Kobold Press, Levels 1-13)

Collection of tower-based adventures with various themes.

**Adaptation:** Select a tower adventure appropriate for level 10-12 and reskin as a Cerberus Assembly mage's tower. Ovedo has hidden the shard there for "safekeeping," but complications arise—the mage has their own agenda, or the tower's defenses have gone haywire.

**Why it works:** Provides strong dungeon structure while allowing the Cobalt Soul/Assembly tension to drive narrative. Multiple towers in the collection give you options based on what flavor you want.

### Shard 7 — Shoagragoth's Lair (Blightshore)

**Challenge Level:** 12-14  
**Current Holder:** Shoagragoth, The Acid Crown  
**Location:** Xagonstar (buried dwarven stronghold), Ebonglass Massif

This is your major dragon setpiece. You need content for both the **journey to Blightshore** (including Rotthold investigation) and the **lair itself**.

#### The Journey: Rotthold and Blightshore Approach

##### Born in Blood (Arcane Library, Level 9)

When characters arrive at the fishing town of Estinn, they find it totally abandoned. A Lovecraftian horror adventure with an underwater dungeon and a newly-born godling.

**Adaptation:** Scale to level 11-12 and reskin as Rotthold. The town isn't abandoned but deeply wrong—the corruption from Shoagragoth's lair and the nearby Abyssal rift has seeped into the population. The "godling" could be an advanced Rendspawn or a creature Shoagragoth is trying to birth. The underwater dungeon becomes tunnels leading toward the Ebonglass Massif.

**Why it works:**

- Provides investigation structure for the Rotthold approach you've already outlined (Harbormaster's logs, Thesra Moonfall, Scavenger's Guild records)
- Horror tone matches Blightshore's corrupted landscape
- The "abandoned coastal town with dark secret" is common enough to not be recognizable
- Gives players a taste of Far Realm corruption before confronting the dragon

##### The Curse of Wardenwood (Arcane Library, Level 7, scale to 11-12)

A rescue mission in a cursed deep forest with a bait-and-switch that forces horrible choices.

**Adaptation:** Reskin as the Ebonglass Massif approach—the volcanic glass formations and corrupted landscape rather than forest. The "curse" is the proximity to Shoagragoth's territory and the Abyssal rift. NPCs trapped in the area could be previous expeditions seeking the shard (connecting to the Consecution's Hope backstory), and rescuing them provides intelligence about the dragon's lair and habits.

**Why it works:** The "horrible choices" structure fits your moral complexity design. Could reveal that someone the party wants to save has already been corrupted by Shoagragoth and made a thrall.

#### The Lair: Xagonstar

##### The Black Dragon Lair (DMDave/Roll20, Levels 4/9/16)

A modular black dragon lair encounter with multi-wave battle options, scalable to different levels.

**Adaptation:** Use the level 16 version scaled to 13-14. The lair structure (acid pools, hidden tunnels, thick mud) maps well to a corrupted dwarven stronghold. Add Shoagragoth's specific personality elements: his "court" of corrupted thralls, the Lorestone shard affecting his mind, and the unstable Abyssal rift he claims to control.

**Why it works:**

- Provides concrete lair mechanics (terrain, hazards, tactical options)
- Multi-wave battle format supports your "thralls first, dragon later" structure
- Generic enough that the actual lair can be whatever you need
- Good VTT maps included

##### Empire of the Ghouls components (Kobold Press, Levels 1-13)

While this is a full campaign, specific lair designs from the Underworld sections could inform Xagonstar's dwarven architecture and the corrupted denizens within.

**Adaptation:** Borrow dungeon design elements for the dwarven stronghold sections—the way Kobold Press handles fallen dwarven civilizations with new monstrous inhabitants. Use their approach to "corrupted grandeur" for Xagonstar's acid-damaged but still impressive halls.

### Shard 8 — Unknown Location

**Challenge Level:** 15-17  
**Current Status:** Location TBD based on campaign development

This is your highest-stakes shard recovery before the endgame. Consider what flavor you want: ancient ruin, powerful NPC guardian, hidden in plain sight, or something else.

#### Option A: Ancient Ruin / Calamity Site

##### Expedition to the Barrier Peaks components (WotC, Quests from the Infinite Staircase)

Alien technology dungeon with "things not of this world."

**Adaptation:** The one WotC module worth keeping for this slot. Reskin as ancient Aeor technology or a site where the boundary between planes is especially thin. The "alien" elements become Far Realm intrusion or pre-Calamity magical technology. The shard was hidden here specifically because the location's strangeness masks its resonance.

**Why it fits:** The aesthetic of "things that don't belong" directly supports your Far Realm themes, and the original module is obscure enough that even knowledgeable players rarely recognize it. The "expedition goes wrong" structure provides natural tension.

#### Option B: Powerful NPC Guardian

##### The Lich Queen's Begotten (M.T. Black, Levels 11-16)

Confront the Githyanki Lich-Queen in the Astral Plane. Features a moral dilemma about an innocent destined for evil.

**Adaptation:** The guardian doesn't have to be a lich-queen—the structure works for any powerful entity. Could be:

- An ancient metallic dragon who took the shard to prevent its misuse
- A Luxon-touched being who understands the dual-artifact requirement
- A trapped celestial or powerful undead from the Calamity era

The "innocent destined for evil" dilemma could parallel Vaud Qalix's situation, providing thematic foreshadowing before the level 18 confrontation.

**Why it works:** High-level planar adventure structure, moral complexity baked in, and the Astral Plane elements could tie to your Luxon Beacon subplot.

#### Option C: Hidden in Plain Sight

Consider whether the final shard might be somewhere the party has already been or with someone they already know. This requires no module but maximum campaign integration.

## Heist & Infiltration Content

**Perfect for:** The Gentleman's Gambit (Hesterian/Zadash), faction espionage, Lorestone shard theft

### Here's To Crime: A Guide to Capers and Heists (DMs Guild)

Not an adventure but a **system**—uses "Blades in the Dark" flashback mechanics for heists. Three-phase structure: Planning, Caper, Score.

**Application:** This could revolutionize how you run The Gentleman's Gambit. The flashback mechanic allows cinematic "it was all part of the plan" moments where Hesterian (or the party) reveals they anticipated complications.

**Specific benefits:**

- Keeps heist sessions from devolving into endless planning
- The "complication" system creates natural tension
- Allows Hesterian's player to feel competent without requiring perfect foresight
- Works for both the initial Myriad job and any later confrontation with Korfel

**Cost:** $1.99 on DMs Guild. Genuinely worth it for the Zadash content alone.

### A Handful of Heists (DMs Guild, Level 3, scale up)

Five heists designed for low level but easily scaled. "The Tower Heist" (stealing from Ahghairon's Tower) is the most adaptable.

**Adaptation:** Scale to level 10-11. Reskin as infiltrating an Obsidian Echoforge research facility to steal their Lorestone analysis, or a Cerberus Assembly vault containing records about the rifts.

**Why it works:** Provides concrete heist structure (entry points, patrols, vault mechanics) that you can reskin. The tower format works for both Echoforge and Assembly targets.

## Cosmic Horror & Far Realm Content

**Perfect for:** Foreshadowing Ceratos, Rendspawn encounters, corrupted locations

### The Horror Within (Arcane Library, Level 8, scale to 10)

A theater where everyone went insane after opening night. The "plague of madness" mechanic fits perfectly with Far Realm corruption.

**Adaptation:** Place in Zadash or Rexxentrum as a harbinger of Ceratos's growing influence. The theater was experimenting with dunamantic performance magic that accidentally opened a conduit to the Far Realm. Survivors could provide cryptic warnings about "the thing that watches through the rifts."

**Why it works:** Urban horror scenario that doesn't require wilderness travel, introduces Far Realm corruption in a contained environment, and can be run as a side quest while players are in a major city for other reasons.

### Watchers in the Dark (Arcane Library, Level 4, scale to 9-10)

A meteorite crash brings cosmic invaders.

**Adaptation:** Reskin as a minor rift opening that deposits Rendspawn in the countryside. Not connected to the main shard quest but reinforces the escalating threat. Could occur anywhere along the party's travel route.

**Why it works:** Quick encounter (one session) that demonstrates the rifts are getting worse without requiring major plot commitment.

## Political Intrigue & Investigation

**Perfect for:** Rexxentrum politics, Cerberus Assembly encounters, faction maneuvering

### Midgard Sagas: Murder on the Crossroads (Kobold Press, Level 3, scale to 10-11)

Trapped in a mansion during a blizzard with a murderer. Political intrigue and confined investigation.

**Adaptation:** Reskin as a Cerberus Assembly gathering or a meeting of faction representatives where someone is assassinated. Multiple factions present (Echoforge, Malachite Cord, Assembly) and someone is targeting those with Lorestone knowledge. The "trapped in a location" format forces the party to solve it before they can leave.

**Why it works:** Confined investigation creates natural tension, multiple suspects from different factions lets you showcase their philosophies, and the "who benefits from this death" question reveals faction motivations.

## Dragon & Draconian Content

**Perfect for:** Vaud Qalix's Draconia restoration plot, encounters related to Dreemoth Ravine

### Dragon-Themed Considerations

Your Shoagragoth encounter is covered above in the Shard 7 section. For other dragon content related to the Draconia plot:

**Fires of Iskh** (Arcane Library, Level 20) — While designed for level 20, the volcanic lair design could inform your Ceratos confrontation if you want an environmental component. The "ancient red dragon in a volcano" structure translates to "Far Realm entity in a planar breach."

For lower-level Draconia content, consider whether Qalix might send the party after dragon-related artifacts or locations that connect to Draconia's history. These would be custom adventures rather than adapted modules.

## Late Campaign & Endgame Content (Levels 15-20)

### Level 15-17: Final Shard Recovery

See Shard 8 options above.

### Level 18: Vaud Qalix Confrontation

The Qalix confrontation should be custom-built around your specific setup—his lair, his resources, Dreyara's role, and the revelation that he's not fully in control. However, structural elements from existing modules can help:

#### Call of the Netherdeep components (WotC)

**Useful elements:**

- **Alyxian's redemption arc:** A corrupted figure who can be redeemed mirrors your Qalix "not fully in control" concept. Study how the module handles the revelation that the villain is also a victim.
- **Rival NPC party system:** Comprehensive rules for a party that levels with the players. Could represent Qalix's elite agents who confront the party throughout the campaign.
- **Faction system:** Three factions competing for the same goal directly parallels your Lorestone conflict.

**Why it works:** You're not running this module, but its structures solve problems you have. The redemption arc is especially valuable for maintaining moral complexity in your Qalix confrontation.

### Levels 19-20: Ceratos Confrontation

This is your custom endgame—no module will fit exactly. However, useful structural references:

#### Netherdeep dungeon design (Call of the Netherdeep)

The Netherdeep itself is described as "a terrifying cross between the Far Realm and the deep ocean." This aesthetic directly applies to Ceratos's domain or a major rift that must be sealed.

**Useful elements:**

- Environmental hazards that aren't just damage but transformation
- The sense that reality doesn't work correctly here
- Creatures that were once something else

#### The Lich Queen's Begotten planar structure

If you haven't used this for Shard 8, its approach to high-level planar adventure—where the environment itself is hostile and the rules are different—informs your Ceratos realm design.

#### Fires of Iskh lair design

The "ancient creature in a transformed environment" structure translates well. The volcano becomes a Far Realm breach; the dragon becomes an entity that doesn't follow normal rules.

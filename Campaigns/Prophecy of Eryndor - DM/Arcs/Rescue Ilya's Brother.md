## Overview

[[Ilya the Sorceress - DM|Ilya]] asked the party to find her brother [[Varnes Dwell - DM|Varnes]]. The party learned he was held at [[Cloudfang Keep - DM]], in the high Cyrios Mountains, where [[Dreyara Drimvar - DM|Dreyara]] was also likely heading. Varnes was alive and well when freed.

## Timeline

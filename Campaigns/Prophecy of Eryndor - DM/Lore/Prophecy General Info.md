## Core Information

### Prophecy General Info

> 1. Rifts have begun to appear across Wildemount. Some people believe these rifts are symbolic and have a pattern. Where they open follows the ley lines from a single source. Otherwise they are chaotic and random, and efforts to find a pattern in their size, plane or behavior will prove fruitless.
> 2. Deep beneath the [[Ruins of Molaesmyr - DM]] is a Fey Crossing. About 520 PD, an elven sorcerer held it open permanently with a Lorestone shard, without a Luxon Beacon. In 585 PD the unstabilized crossing [[Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing|ruptured]], destroying the city and most of its residents at the time.
> 3. A [[The Prophecy|prophecy]] exists (see below) that describes what many have interpreted to be three groups or factions that would arise and struggle for control of the rifts.
> 4. The prophecy speaks of a "beacon" that will seal the rift. One group of scholars, the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]], believes that the [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM]] itself is the beacon, and they are seeking to assemble the shards. (This group is the "Tree" of the prophecy.) (One of the passengers from Emon was a [[Aethor Kalisk - DM|member of this group]] and had a lead on someone in [[Port Damali - DM]] who may have more information about the location of a missing shard.)
> 	- This group is partly correct--the Lorestone does need to be assembled, but it is not the beacon. It requires a [[Luxon Beacon Integration with Lorestone|Luxon Beacon]] to function properly to close rifts.
> 5. A second group ([[Malachite Cord - DM]]) believe the efforts to disperse the energy will actually enhance it, and that the presence of the "beacon" will instead *cause* the rift to open further. The Cord will be working to prevent the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] from succeeding in their quest and are instead researching divine magical means of closing the unstable rifts. (This group is the "Shield" of the prophecy.) (Another skyship passenger was a member of this group, spying on the first.)
> 	- This group is also partly correct. The Lorestone alone will in fact destabilize rifts and cause them to become more chaotic. The [[Rupture of the Molaesmyr Fey Crossing|cataclysm in Molaesmyr]] was exactly this: a Lorestone shard used without a Luxon Beacon.
> 6. A third group ([[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM]]) believes that the openings between planes are an opportunity and a sign that the gods are going to return to Exandria. They believe the rift can (and should) be controlled. They have reason to think a permanent doorway can be constructed via mechanical and arcane means, but the magic needed to make it work was lost after the Divergence. They are seeking pre-Calamity knowledge and artifacts and assembling a team of arcane and divine scholars hoping to solve the mystery before the event. (They are not yet aware of [[Aveqtaro Thaan]], but when they learn of her, they will conclude she is a key to their success.) They will also be working to stop both the other groups from succeeding. (This group is the "Crown" in the prophecy.)
> 	- This group is wrong--the rifts are chaotic and cannot be controlled.
> 7. It will eventually turn out that the hijackers were part of the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]], and the hijacking was intended to divert the first group.
> 8. The [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]] are not aware of the Cord yet, and the hijackers did not know about their spy on the skyship. Neither the [[Obsidian Echoforge - DM]] or the Cord are aware of the [[Emissaries of the Sunfall - DM|Emissaries]] splinter group.

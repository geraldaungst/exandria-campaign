## Core Information

### Hesterian and Korfel: Resolution Paths

Moved out of [[Hesterian Shyr's Infiltration]]. DM only.

#### The core question

When Hesterian meets her killer face-to-face in a position of power, how will she choose between immediate revenge and strategic patience?

#### What makes the meeting hard

- **No simple infiltration.** Spy work doesn't apply when the target knows exactly who you are and what you want.
- **Mutual leverage.** Each has devastating information about the other.
- **Witnesses.** Any confrontation happens in front of Korfel's people, who don't know his real identity.
- **Blood samples.** The [[Blood Sample Collection Protocol]] is a tactical flashpoint. He will want her blood, and has reason to want it for tracking.
- **Hesterian's side of the recognition:** shock ("You're supposed to be Korfel Withrethin, not some crime boss"), rage ("You killed me and built an empire while an innocent man rots in prison"), and a quick count of his guards. His side is in the "When Hesterian walks in" section of [[Korfel Withrethin]].

#### Moral questions

- **Justice or revenge:** does exposing [[Rylan Estevez Frame Job|Rylan's wrongful imprisonment]] matter more than her own satisfaction?
- **Greater good or personal vendetta:** taking down the Gentleman may destabilize Zadash's criminal balance.
- **Party loyalty or individual goals:** how far does she go before her quest endangers her companions?
- **Means and ends:** what crimes will she commit to get there?

#### Character growth

- From victim to agent: from reactive revenge to building justice.
- Understanding power: how institutional change differs from individual satisfaction.
- Family: learning [[The Capture of Melthes|Melthes's fate]] adds stakes beyond revenge.
- Identity: accepting her nature as a Hollow One while pursuing human goals.

#### Resolution paths

- **A. Justice through law.** Expose Korfel's crimes and identity to the authorities, free [[Rylan Estevez]], and accept the risk of destabilizing Zadash's criminal balance.
- **B. Criminal justice.** Use a Myriad position to take the organization apart from within; turn [[Cree Deeproots]] against Korfel through the Assembly connection; replace the empire with something more ethical.
- **C. Personal revenge.** Confront and kill him; accept collateral damage to the underworld; satisfaction over systemic change.
- **D. Uneasy alliance.** Use the mutual knowledge for ongoing cooperation and his resources for campaign goals; accept the moral compromise.

#### Where it meets other threads

- **Melthes:** if Hesterian mentions her brother, Cree may be recruited ([[Rescue Melthes]]).
- **The Assembly** is a common enemy of Hesterian and Cree, a possible basis for an alliance.
- **Zadash politics:** the outcome shifts the city's criminal and political balance.
- **Rylan's release** can become its own quest line.
- **Dechs's herb delivery** is a possible shared destination in Zadash.

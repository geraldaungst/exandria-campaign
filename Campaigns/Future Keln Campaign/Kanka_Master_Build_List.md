# A. NOTES (concepts)

*(Carried over from the previous notes doc, plus one addition — Titans.)*

## Deep
*Ready.* The Deeps are places where the world's magic — flowing up from the planet's core through underground veins — rises close to the surface. No visible surface markers; the ground looks ordinary. Only those with @The_Gift can sense one. Civilizations tend to grow up around Deeps, since magic runs stronger there.

## Elderright
*Ready.* The Elderright is the ceremonial title held by a former Right of Varrin after their own heir has had a child and taken the throne. No longer the ruling monarch, the Elderright serves as a counselor and mentor.

## Quickened
*Ready.* Common term for people who demonstrate exceptional abilities — adventurers, powerful warriors, spellcasters. The moment such abilities manifest is "the Quickening." There's no visible marker of being Quickened; the only real evidence is demonstrated ability. Society treats the Quickened inconsistently — some revere, some fear, many resent or envy.

## The Gift
*Ready.* A rare, inborn sensitivity that lets a person detect the presence and rough location of a @Deep. Unrelated to whether someone is @Quickened.

## The Veins
*Ready.* Underground channels through which the planet's magic flows outward from its core, feeding the Deeps. The full network is unmapped — a good subject for a future discovery.

## Magical Essence
*Ready — DM Notes only.* The animating life-force every living being carries in some degree. What separates the Quickened from "ordinary" folk isn't a hard line, it's a spectrum. Not common knowledge in-world.

## Collective Field
*Ready — DM Notes only.* When enough people gather, their combined essence passively stabilizes magic in settlements, without needing a Deep's amplification. Unconscious, not understood by those who benefit from it.

## Titans
*Ready, but deliberately incomplete.* Titans were once-living beings capable of channeling the planet's core magic directly. They are dead now, their petrified remains scattered in multiple places worldwide (Watcher's Knee among them) — a fact almost no one currently knows. Varrinthal's founding myth claims its first ruler, Varrin, was personally appointed by the Titans to claim the land, giving the monarchy's "divine right" an unintentional grain of truth. **Where Titans came from and why they died is an open question — leave unresolved.** This is consistent with your "history through contradiction" principle: different cultures may have different myths about them, and none needs to be canonically correct.

## Refined Essence / Titan Concentrate
*Shell only — mechanic not finalized.* Direction narrowed to an essence-bridge/spell-storage approach but not decided. Don't add content yet.

## Soul / The Divine
*Shell — intentionally unresolved, not a gap to fill.* If ever written, this note should describe the disagreement between faiths/cultures on the nature of the divine and the afterlife, not settle it.

# B. LOCATIONS

## Watcher's Rest
*Parent: Varrinthal (Kingdom).* Ready — extensive source material in your Settlement Guide.

**Public description (short, orienting):** A quarry town of roughly 1,000 people on the @Skarnveil, about 270 miles upriver from @Varrinthal_City. Built around two industries: quarrying the exceptional "kneestone" from nearby Watcher's Knee, and operating the only reliable ferry crossing for miles. Not built on a @Deep — magic here follows standard rules. Named for a local legend about a giant who sat down to wait and slowly turned to stone.

**Public Article — "The Legend of the Watcher":** the full local folktale, plus how different age groups/types of townsfolk regard it (children believe it, adults treat it as charming heritage, a superstitious minority still leaves offerings "just in case"). Full text is in the Settlement Guide, ready to adapt.

**DM Notes:** Watcher's Knee is the literal petrified knee of an ancient Titan; the rest of the body extends underground through the region. Kneestone's unusual properties (warmth, chatoyancy, enchantment retention) are because it's organic remains, not mineral. Discovery is meant to be optional/flexible — background flavor unless players pursue it.

**Also documented and ready to build as sub-Locations under Watcher's Rest:** Watcher's Knee (quarry), River's Bend Inn, Market Square (with the Watcher's Shrine), Town Hall, The Dusty Mallet (tavern), Karven's Goods (general store), Stone Yards, Main Docks, The Stone & Current (tavern), the small multi-faith Temple, The Crossing House (South Bank inn/tavern), Ferry Dock (South Bank).

## Skarnveil
*Ready.* The river that Watcher's Rest sits on. Flows generally north to south, originating unexplored in the @Kelvantor_Mountains, bending sharply at Watcher's Knee (creating the town's natural harbor), and emptying into Nareth's Bay at Varrinthal.

## Tur-Marethis
*Ready, brief.* Capital of @The_Ringhold, built high in the @Kelvantor_Mountains on one of its Deeps. Founded by the five survivors of @Ruins_of_Marethis.

## Ruins of Marethis
*Ready, brief.* The coastal city destroyed by a great tidal wave; five survivors fled into the mountains and founded Tur-Marethis. Ringhold legend holds that the Kelvantor range itself is the wave, turned to stone.

## Varrinthal (City)
*Shell — not yet detailed beyond being the kingdom's capital.* Presumably built on a Deep (referenced as "Varrinthal: Deep city" in the Settlement Guide), 270 miles downriver from Watcher's Rest. No further description decided.

# D. ORGANISATIONS

## The Ring (Ring of Five)
*Existing shell — content ready.* Governing council of @The_Ringhold. Five seats, called Voices, filled by nomination and election among @Quickened candidates. Named after the five founders of Tur-Marethis: Yselda Torb, Bess Marawen, Hob Halloway, Corwin Somes, and Poru Adderick.

## Tidemark Syndicate
*Ready.* Organized crime operating out of Varrinthal. Ordered the kneestone theft in the River Bottom Cache as a "test job," with more planned if it succeeds. Vindictive toward anyone who crosses them.

## Watcher's Rest Town Council
*Ready.* Type: Council. Parent: none required, but link to Watcher's Rest via a Relation once both exist.

**Public description:**
The Watcher's Rest Town Council advises the magistrate on local matters and represents the community's major interests. Five members meet weekly in Town Hall. Seats are held by whoever leads the town's five key constituencies: the quarry owners, the innkeepers and merchants, the mason's guild, the surrounding farms, and the river trade and docks. The council has real local legitimacy and can stall or support the magistrate's decisions, though final legal authority rests with the Crown-appointed magistrate.

**DM Notes:**
Current holders of the five seats (plain text for now — convert to @mentions once you build these as Characters):
- Quarry owners: Thrain Stonebreaker
- Merchant/innkeeper interests: Marda Helven
- Mason's guild / quality standards: Master Mason Elira Stonemark
- Agricultural interests: Farmer Hendrick Vale
- River trade and docks: Harbormaster Daven Crell

The council is a genuine power bloc, not just ceremonial — see the "Current Tensions" material in the Settlement Guide (labor dispute, ferry toll debate, quarry expansion pressure) for how council members are already pulling in different directions. Good material for early roleplay hooks once you're ready to build this out further.

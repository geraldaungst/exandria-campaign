The house rules we use at every table, in every world.

## Combat

- [[Critical Hits]]: critical hits always deal at least maximum regular damage
- [[Flanking]]: the standard flanking rules, with the DM as final arbiter
- [[Movement]]: how diagonals are counted and how to move through other creatures' spaces
- [[Strategic Initiative]]: choosing a later place in the initiative order

## Death and Recovery

- [[Enhanced Hit Dice]]: re-roll 1s on hit dice
- [[Fading Spirits]]: how characters are brought back from the dead
- [[Private Death Saves]]: death saves are rolled by the player and whispered to the DM
- [[Persistent Afflictions]]: some diseases, poisons, and curses need more than a spell

## Checks and Rolls

- [[Modifying Rolls]]: one die at a time from magical effects
- [[Skill Checks]]: how group attempts and retries work
- [[I Know Someone Who]]: a personal connection each player can call on once

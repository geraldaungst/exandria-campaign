Every PC has one personal connection in their back pocket. At any time and in any situation, a player with an inspiration die can declare, "I know someone who..." and describe an acquaintance who can help with the situation at hand. The player can give as much or as little detail about that person as they like.

The DM decides which ability fits the situation and sets a DC based on how available or willing the person might be, how to contact them, and so on. The base DC is normally 15. The player rolls the d20 ability check and adds the inspiration die (1d6). On a success, the person can be contacted and will help. On a failure, they may be unreachable, unwilling, or unavailable for some other reason.

This connection is in addition to any similar features from a character's background, class, species, feats, and so on.

If a player's use of this feature is particularly clever, the DM may award inspiration for it, which makes it effectively a free use.

Each player can use this once during the campaign. After every player has used it, the DM may allow everyone a second "someone who."

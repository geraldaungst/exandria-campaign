If any single member of the party could do a task, one or two party members can make the first attempt. The second person can make their own attempt, or use the Help action to give the first person advantage.

If the first attempt fails, other party members may try if time permits, but the DC increases significantly.

Sometimes the DM will call for a group check, when everyone would clearly make the attempt.

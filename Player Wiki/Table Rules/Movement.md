## On the Map

Maps use Pathfinder/3.5 measurement rules. Each diagonal move counts as 1.5 spaces (7.5 feet on most maps). In practice, the first diagonal move costs 5 feet and the next costs 10. For more complex moves, use the ruler tool.

## Moving Outside Combat

Before you move your token, describe what you intend to do. The DM will tell you how far you can go, alone or as a group, before you reach areas you can't currently see. There is some lag between when you move your token and when others see it, so this keeps everyone oriented.

## Moving Through Other Creatures

- You can move through the space of another character or a friendly creature.
- You can move through an enemy's space if the enemy is 2 sizes larger or smaller than you.
- Moving through another creature's space is always difficult terrain, and costs double movement.

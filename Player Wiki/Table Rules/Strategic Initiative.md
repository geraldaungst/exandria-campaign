After initiative is rolled, players have these options:

- A player who would like to go later in the order than their roll may choose a later position.
- If two or more players have the same initiative roll, those players may choose their order among themselves.

You can make either or both choices at any time before the first round of combat begins. Remaining ties, such as between players and opponents, are broken using Dexterity scores. The resulting order stays the same for the whole combat.

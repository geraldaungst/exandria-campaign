Lesser Restoration and Remove Curse may not work automatically. Depending on the disease, poison, or curse, these spells may need an ability check or additional components.

Each situation is different, and the character will generally know before casting what is needed. If an extra component is required, such as a rare herb to lift a curse, the character will know that something more is needed. They may have to do research or exploration to learn exactly what to get.

The DM uses this rule when the story or situation calls for it.

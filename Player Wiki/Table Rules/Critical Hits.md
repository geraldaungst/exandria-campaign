Critical hits always deal at least maximum regular damage. Roll the damage dice once, then add the maximum those dice can roll, plus any modifiers.

For example, an attack that normally deals 3d8 damage rolls 3d8 on a critical hit, adds 24 (the most 3d8 can roll), and then adds any damage modifiers.

This applies to every critical hit, for players and for NPCs, enemies, and monsters alike.

Reversing death should be difficult. Any magical attempt to bring someone back from the dead uses the Fading Spirits rules from Critical Role's *Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn*. This applies to every spell that can reverse death, including Revivify, Raise Dead, Resurrection, and Reincarnate.

## The Ritual

Every character present at the casting becomes part of a resurrection ritual, except when the spell is Revivify (see below). Up to three of them, usually the deceased character's companions, can each try to call the spirit back. A contribution can be anything the player or the DM finds moving: a speech, a song from a shared past, a display of swordplay meant to goad a rival, a confession of love, or something else entirely.

Each contributor makes an ability check. The player may suggest which ability fits, and the DM decides. The base DC is 15. The DM adjusts it for how sincere and fitting the contribution is, and may grant advantage to one that is roleplayed in a particularly touching way.

## The Resurrection Check

After all contributions, the DM makes one resurrection check: a d20 roll with no modifier. The base DC is 10, adjusted as follows:

- +1 for each time the character has already returned from the dead. Each return weakens the soul's hold on the world.
- -3 for each successful contribution.
- +1 for each failed contribution.

On a success, the soul returns to the body. On a failure, the soul does not return, and the character can never again be raised from the dead.

## True Miracles

A character returned by *true resurrection*, by *wish*, or by a god comes back at once, with no ritual. The DM may allow one casting of one of those spells to start a last-chance ritual for a character who has been permanently lost.

## Revivify

Only the caster takes part when Revivify is used. The caster makes a quick resurrection check: a d20 plus their spellcasting ability modifier against a DC of 10, +1 for each time the character has already returned from the dead. On a failure, the soul is not lost, but the spell fails, and every future resurrection check for that character has a DC 1 higher. Any further attempt must use a spell with a longer casting time.

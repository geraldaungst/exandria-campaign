What the people of Keln commonly know about their world.

## Places

- [[Callastri]]: the continent
- [[Varrinthal Kingdom]]: a hereditary kingdom in the southeast
- [[The Ringhold]]: a confederation in the north and west
- [[Kelvantor Mountains]]: the range that divides the two
- [[Watcher's Rest]]: a quarry and ferry town in Varrinthal

## Magic and People

- [[Deep]]: places where the world's magic rises close to the surface
- [[Veins]]: underground channels that carry magic outward from the planet's core
- [[The Gift]]: the inborn ability to sense a Deep
- [[Quickened]]: people with exceptional abilities
- [[Groundfolk]]: common people who are not Quickened

## Titles of Varrinthal

- [[Right of Varrin]]: Varrinthal's ruling monarch
- [[Elderright]]: a former Right of Varrin who has stepped back to counsel

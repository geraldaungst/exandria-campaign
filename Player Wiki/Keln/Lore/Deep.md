The Deeps are places where the world's magic—flowing up from the planet's core through underground veins—rises close to the surface. There are no visible surface markers; the ground looks ordinary. Only those with [[The Gift]] can sense one. Civilizations tend to grow up around Deeps, since magic runs stronger there.

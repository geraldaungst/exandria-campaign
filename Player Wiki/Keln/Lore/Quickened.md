Common term for people who demonstrate exceptional abilities: adventurers, powerful warriors, spellcasters. The moment such abilities manifest is "the Quickening." There's no visible marker of being Quickened; the only real evidence is demonstrated ability. Society treats the Quickened inconsistently. Some revere, some fear, many resent or envy them. See also [[Groundfolk]].

A rare, inborn sensitivity that lets a person detect the presence and rough location of a [[Deep]]. Having The Gift is unrelated to whether someone is [[Quickened]], though most with the Gift are also Quickened. It is rare but not unheard of for [[Groundfolk]] to have The Gift.

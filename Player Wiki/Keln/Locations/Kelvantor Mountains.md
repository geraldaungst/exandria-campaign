## Description

The **Kelvantor** range divides [[Callastri]], separating *[[Varrinthal City|Varrinthal]]* to the southeast from the *[[The Ringhold|Ringhold]]* to the north and west. The name — meaning roughly "petrified tide" in the tongue of the Ringhold's founders — reflects a Ringhold legend that the peaks are the very wave that destroyed *[[Ruins of Marethis]]*, turned to stone. Steep, sparsely settled, and difficult to cross, the range is home to *[[Tur-Marethis]]*, built high on one of its Deeps.

Kelvantor belongs to no single polity — it is wilderness, the natural buffer between Callastri's two great powers.

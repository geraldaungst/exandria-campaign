## Description

The Ringhold is a confederation of settlements spanning the north and west of [[Callastri]], governed from its capital, [[Tur-Marethis]], by a council known as [[The Ring]]. Membership is voluntary. Towns and cities across the continent may petition to join, gaining the Ringhold's protection and resources in exchange for accepting its governance. Older than most powers on Callastri, it traces its founding to the survivors of [[Ruins of Marethis]].

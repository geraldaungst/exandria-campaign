## Description

Cloudfang Keep is a crumbling stone castle in the high Cyrios Mountains. It stands on Frigid Summit, 23,421 feet above sea level, in extreme cold. The keep is the home and research facility of [[Lady Emer]], a medusa who studies planar rifts.

## Grounds and Guardians

- Stone double doors lead into the keep. Two owlbears disguised as statues stand on either side of them.
- More trained owlbears patrol the outer walls, and griffons patrol the sky above.
- The top of the keep's tower is ruined and open to the sky. Griffons nest there.

## Notable Areas

- **Stone garden:** A courtyard at the center of the keep. Flowers and herbs grow among statues of people Lady Emer has turned to stone. A marble pedestal wrapped in flowering vines rises 40 feet above the garden.
- **Library:** Shelves of books line the walls. Lady Emer's desk and her research on planar rifts are kept here.

## Getting There

- A mountain road leads to the keep's entrance.

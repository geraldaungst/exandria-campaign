> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Abandoned monastery
> **Part of:** [[Cyrios Mountains]]

## Description

The Monastery of Dimensional Harmony is an abandoned monastery high in the Cyrios Mountains. It is cut into the mountain's rock and extends into natural caverns that its builders enlarged. Firbolg monks built it and lived there. The steps and furniture are sized for Large creatures, and all writing in the monastery, from the guest registry to its religious texts, is in Giant script.

## Entrances

- **Main entrance:** A rectangular opening about 5 feet wide and 10 feet tall, cut into the mountain face near a mountain road. Rockfall and snow buried it for centuries until an avalanche cleared it. A passage leads from it down into the monastery.
- **Rear entrance:** A stair climbs from inside the monastery up through the mountain to a concealed opening higher on the snowy slope. Outside this entrance, [[Dreyara Drimvar]] confronted the adventurers known as The Third Act, who defeated her there.

## Halls and Caverns

- The upper halls are worked stone. They include a room for receiving guests, with a registry of visitors, as well as a dining area, a kitchen, and rooms holding records, ledgers, and correspondence.
- Beyond the halls, the monks widened natural caverns. One holds stone altars and seating carved for worship.
- A library cavern holds the monks' texts.
- An underground pond lies among the deeper caverns.
- In one cavern, worn stone platforms and wall alcoves held the monks' research records. The cavern is now thick with webbing.
- Phase spiders live throughout the caverns.

## The Memorial Chamber

A chamber of precise stonework honors the monastery's three founders. Each lies in a stone sarcophagus in an alcove, with the founder's name carved above in Giant and a weapon laid before the tomb:

- **Ondur** (west alcove): a great shield, for protection.
- **Brekkan** (north alcove): a longsword, for discipline.
- **Solvei** (east alcove): a longbow, for precision. This bow became [[Solvei's Vigilance]].

Four disks of soulwood, each about two feet across, are set flush with the floor in the chamber's corners. They can be rotated, and their alignment directs planar flow through the room.

## The Monks

- The monks joined religious practice with the study of planar magic. Their religious texts refer to phasing, and through years of meditation they learned to sense planar flow.
- They studied phase spiders, creatures that can cross from the Ethereal Plane. Their surviving records include data tables, anatomical sketches, and notes from repeated examinations.

## The Collapse

- A ritual in a chamber deep beneath the monastery failed. The floor of the chamber above it collapsed into it, leaving an open chasm, and the lower chamber was corrupted. The monastery was abandoned.
- One monk, [[Brother Kelmen]], remained in the monastery as a spirit until a ritual left unfinished at the time of the collapse was completed.

## The Lower Chamber

The lower chamber can be reached only by descending the chasm. Stone pillars form an octagon around a raised central platform, and the space is disorienting to stand in. A knot of darkness about the size of a fist floats at the center of the platform.

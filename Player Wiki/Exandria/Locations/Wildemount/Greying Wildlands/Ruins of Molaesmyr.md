## Description

Molaesmyr (pronounced mo-LAY-əs-meer) is a ruined elven city at the heart of the [[Savalirwood]]. It once stood in the lush Veluthil Forest. The cursed forest now surrounds the ruins and makes them nearly inaccessible.

## History

- Not long after the Calamity, Molaesmyr became the most prominent elven nation in Wildemount.
- In 585 PD, a cataclysm struck the city. Its cause is not understood.
- Within days the city was empty. Most of its people were killed, and the survivors fled.
- The survivors scattered in two directions. Some went north and joined the dwarven city of Uthodurn. Others went west and founded Bysaes Tyl.
- A curse spread outward from the ruins into the surrounding forest, which became the Savalirwood.
- Many have tried to learn what caused the disaster. None have learned much.

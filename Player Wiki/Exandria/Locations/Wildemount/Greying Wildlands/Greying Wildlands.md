## Description

The Greying Wildlands are the northernmost region of [[Wildemount]], a land of dense, desiccated forests, rolling mountains, and icy tundra. The harsh conditions keep the region sparsely populated.

## Geography

- The region lies north of the [[Dwendalian Empire]].
- Together with the islands of Eiselcross, the region is known as the Biting North.
- The [[Savalirwood]], a cursed forest, separates Uthodurn from the rest of Wildemount.

## Notable Sites

- [[Savalirwood]]: a forest corrupted by a curse, formerly the Veluthil Forest
- [[Ruins of Molaesmyr]]: the ruins of an elven city at the heart of the Savalirwood
- **Uthodurn:** a dwarven citadel governed as a diarchy, beyond the edge of the Empire's reach, and home to many elves who survived the destruction of Molaesmyr
- **Palebank Village:** an Uthodurn outpost on the cold shore of the Frigid Depths, and a waystation for expeditions to Eiselcross

## Climate

- Winters bring heavy snow, freezing fog, and icy winds.
- Spring thaws arrive with mist and light rain.
- Summers are mild, with occasional storms.
- Autumn brings foggy mornings and early frost.

## Description

The Savalirwood is a cursed forest in the [[Greying Wildlands]] of northern Wildemount. It was once the Veluthil Forest, a lush elven forest. After the elven city of Molaesmyr was destroyed in 585 PD, a curse spread outward from the city through the surrounding woods, which became known as the Savalirwood.

## The Corruption

- A dense, sickly purple fog fills the forest.
- Plant life grows twisted and aggressive.
- Native creatures are warped into more dangerous forms. Cursed fey roam the forest among them.
- The forest resists every attempt to settle or clear it.
- In about 250 years, no one has found a cure, despite efforts by the mages of Uthodurn and others.
- The [[Malachite Cord]] studies the corruption in search of a cure. Its members say the [[Planar Rifts|rifts]] may be connected to it.

## Geography

- The forest covers much of northern Wildemount.
- It surrounds the [[Ruins of Molaesmyr]] and makes them nearly inaccessible.
- It forms a natural barrier between the city of Uthodurn and the rest of Wildemount.

## Legend

The Savalirwood is the setting of the legend of the Aurora's Ascendant, whom the Malachite Cord reveres.

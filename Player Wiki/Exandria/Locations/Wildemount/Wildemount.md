## Description

Wildemount is a continent of Exandria. [[Tal'Dorei]] lies across the [[Lucidian Ocean]], and [[Marquet]] lies far to the southwest. Wildemount suffered the worst of the fighting in [[The Calamity]] and still bears the deepest scars of that war. [[Planar Rifts]] open across the continent.

## Geography

- The Shearing Channel separates Wildemount from Tal'Dorei. A land bridge joined the two continents until the Calamity destroyed it. The channel is a broken stretch of jagged rock, rapids, and strong winds that is nearly impossible to cross.
- **[[Menagerie Coast]]:** the southwestern shore along the Lucidian Ocean, governed by the Clovis Concord.
- **[[Cyrios Mountains]]:** the range between the Menagerie Coast and the Dwendalian Empire.
- **Wynandir:** the central landmass, divided between the [[Dwendalian Empire]] and [[Xhorhas]].
- **[[Greying Wildlands]]:** the northernmost region, a land of forest, mountain, and tundra.
- **Eiselcross:** islands in the far north. With the Greying Wildlands, they are known as the Biting North.
- **Blightshore:** a region east of Xhorhas.

## Wynandir

- Wynandir is the central landmass of Wildemount. It lies east and north of the Cyrios Mountains and the Menagerie Coast, south of the Greying Wildlands and Eiselcross, and west of the Blightshore.
- The Ashkeeper Peaks divide it. The Dwendalian Empire rules the western half. The eastern half is Xhorhas, and the [[Kryn Dynasty]] rules its north.
- The Empire and the Kryn Dynasty are at open war along the frontier.

## History

- Around 400 PD, the dragonborn nation of [[Draconia]] was founded above the Dreemoth Ravine.
- In 539 PD, Manfried Dwendal became the first Emperor of the Dwendalian Empire.
- In 585 PD, the elven nation of Molaesmyr, the most prominent in Wildemount after the Calamity, was destroyed. See [[Ruins of Molaesmyr]].
- In 811 PD, the Chroma Conclave destroyed Draconia.

## Peoples and Customs

- [[Dragonborn]] are common across Wildemount.
- The [[Ki'Nau]] are the indigenous people of the Menagerie Coast.
- Holidays observed in Wildemount are listed in the [[Calendar of Exandria]].

## Getting There

- **By air:** [[Port Damali]] has the only skyport in Wildemount, served by skyships from Tal'Dorei and Marquet.
- **By sea:** Ships from across the Lucidian Ocean call at the open ports of the Menagerie Coast.

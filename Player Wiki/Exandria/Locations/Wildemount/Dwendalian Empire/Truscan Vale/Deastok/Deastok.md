## Description

Deastok is an affluent city in the [[Truscan Vale]], at the southwestern edge of the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. It sits on a hill bordering the Cyrengreen Forest, surrounded by farmland divided by fences into tidy segments. The city is comfortable and well kept, and wealthy travelers come here for a countryside retreat. A timber industry also supports it. Its sister city, [[Kamordah]], lies to the north.

About 10,090 people live in Deastok: 60 percent human, 26 percent dwarf, 10 percent halfling, and 4 percent other peoples.

## Government

- Starosta Deter McGinneas is the Crown-appointed leader of the city.
- The real power in Deastok is the [[Truscan Family]], whose seat is here. The starosta and the city's other titled officials were appointed at the family's behest, and it is widely assumed that anyone in office is related to the Truscans or in their debt.

## Security

- Deastok has no city walls. Checkpoints stand on the main roads into town, where travelers show their papers.
- A lone stone watchtower stands near the checkpoint in the Jeweled Gates, with a brazier burning at its top. Soldiers keep watch from it, and travelers on the road see it well before the rest of the town comes into view.
- The Crownsguard keeps a barracks in the Jeweled Gates.

## Districts

- **Bursar Plaza:** The town's original core, historically a laborers' district, and now its commercial center. The plaza is enormous, with a great fountain at its center and three separate clusters of tents and stalls. Buskers work the crowds, and the district presents itself to visitors as a quaint countryside getaway. The [[Temple of Bahamut in Deastok|Temple of Bahamut]] stands to the south of the plaza, and the [[Shaded Bough]] is nearby.
- **Garden Grounds:** A residential district of parks and open space, home to Deastok's established wealthy families. The [[White Peak Inn]] is here.
- **Jeweled Gates:** The working and merchant district in the west of town, along with the checkpoint plaza.

Even the working districts are clean and well kept. Deastok has no run-down quarter.

## Notable Sites

### Checkpoint Plaza

The plaza inside the Jeweled Gates checkpoint holds a customs house, a carters' and caravan yard, a modest travelers' waystation, a public well, a short row of stalls catering to new arrivals, a notice board, and the Crownsguard barracks.

### The Statue of Bahamut and the Emperor

At the center of the checkpoint plaza stands a weathered stone statue, gilded rather than painted. [[Bahamut]], the Platinum Dragon, appears as an armored warrior with wings folded, twice the height of a crowned King Dwendal beside him, one gauntleted hand resting on the King's shoulder. Gold leaf picks out the King's crown and regalia, and platinum covers Bahamut's armor and draconic details. Small gilded birds circle the pair, and one rests on the dragon's pauldron.

### Truscan Estate

The seat of the [[Truscan Family]] is a large estate behind tall walls at the southwestern edge of town, near the forest.

## Religion

Deastok has a single official temple, the [[Temple of Bahamut in Deastok|Temple of Bahamut]].

## Getting There

- From the west, a road descends out of the [[Cyrios Mountains]] through the foothills to Deastok.
- The road continues east toward [[Zadash]].

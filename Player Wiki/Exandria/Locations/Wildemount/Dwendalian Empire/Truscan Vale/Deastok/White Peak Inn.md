## Description

The White Peak Inn is a small inn in the Garden Grounds district of [[Deastok]]. From the street it looks almost like a private house. A small sign on the door reads only WHITE PEAK INN, and the inn does no other advertising.

Inside, the room is warm and unhurried. Its patrons are mostly locals and regulars from the surrounding neighborhood.

## Proprietor

Torvald, a broad, weathered human in his fifties with a neatly kept grey beard, runs the inn and tends the bar.

## Description

The Shaded Bough is an inn near Bursar Plaza in [[Deastok]]. It is a large, gleaming white building of three stories, trimmed throughout with gold leaf. A carved wooden sign above the door shows an elaborate tree and reads THE SHADED BOUGH in gilt lettering, with DEASTOK'S FINEST beneath it between two stars. A doorman in a crisp green coat greets everyone who approaches as "my lord" or "my lady."

Inside, the common room has exposed beams and hanging lanterns, with music playing and most tables taken.

## Rooms and Services

- Suites and single rooms. A standard room costs 5 gp per night.
- Hot bath, drawn in a copper tub and brought to the room: 5 sp.
- Stabling: 5 sp per horse per night.
- "Deastok Country Breakfast": 3 sp.
- Private dining room: 2 gp, plus the cost of the meal.
- A lutenist plays at the tables, and a tip is expected.

## Reputation

Most of its guests are visitors to the city. Deastok's established families tend to avoid it.

## Description

The only official temple in [[Deastok]], dedicated to [[Bahamut]], the Platinum Dragon. It stands in the Bursar Plaza district, south of the plaza. Its pale platinum dome rises above the surrounding rooftops and can be seen from across the plaza.

## The Foyer

A carved relief in the foyer gives thanks for the Emperor's blessing over the city.

## The Sanctuary

Past an inner archway, light falls through high windows onto a wide, quiet sanctuary. Frescoes wrap the walls in an unbroken procession of dragons in flight, in battle, and in council, no two alike. Most of the dragons are gold, but not all:

- Near the eastern wall, a hammered bronze dragon grapples something clawed and shadowed.
- A silver dragon watches over a council of mortals from the shadow of a painted mountain.
- Brass wings spread wide above a battlefield strewn with banners.
- A copper dragon lies coiled around a sleeping village.

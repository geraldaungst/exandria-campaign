## Description

Kamordah is a city in the [[Truscan Vale]], north of its sister city, [[Deastok]]. It is built of pale stone quarried from nearby Mount Mentiri and of petrified wood from the surrounding forest, set on the many-colored mud of the Bromkiln Hills.

## Geography

- The Bromkiln Hills around the city are grey and rocky, with little vegetation, unlike the green fields and forests common elsewhere in the region.
- Patches of grey, bubbling mud form between the hills, and vents release sulfurous gas that gives the area a strong smell.
- The local groundwater is undrinkable. Residents rely on collected rainwater and fresh water brought in from outside.

## Known For

- The geothermal activity beneath the hills enriches the soil with minerals that support rare plants unable to survive elsewhere in Wildemount.
- The same soil supports valuable vineyards, and Kamordah is known for its wines.

## Government

Starosta Brennan Keef is the Crown-appointed governor of Kamordah. His close ties to the [[Truscan Family]] keep him in good standing with the Crown and with traders across the vale, though locals complain that he puts Truscan interests ahead of theirs.

## History

- Followers of Desirat the Twilight Phoenix founded the city.
- The Julous Dominion later drove them out, claimed the city, and tried to harness the area's geothermal activity.
- The city passed to the Dwendalian Empire when the Empire conquered the Dominion.

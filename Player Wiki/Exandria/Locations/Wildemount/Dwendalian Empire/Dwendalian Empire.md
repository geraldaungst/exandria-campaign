## Description

The Dwendalian Empire rules the western half of Wynandir, on the continent of Wildemount. Its capital is [[Rexxentrum]]. The Empire is landlocked and relies on trade with its neighbors, especially the Clovis Concord of the [[Menagerie Coast]].

## Geography

- Mountains surround the Empire: the Dunrock Mountains to the north, the Ashkeeper Peaks to the east along the border with [[Xhorhas]], and the [[Cyrios Mountains]] to the west.
- In the south, where the Cyrios Mountains and the Ashkeeper Peaks meet, the Wuyun Gates guard the pass to the Menagerie Coast.
- **Zemni Fields:** The northern lands where the Empire began, home to Rexxentrum.
- **Marrow Valley:** The heart of the Empire, once ruled by the Julous Dominion. [[Zadash]] is its largest city.
- **[[Truscan Vale]]:** The Empire's southwestern edge, overseen by the Truscan family and one of the richest parts of the Empire.

## Government

- [[Bertrand Dwendal|King Bertrand Dwendal]] rules from Rexxentrum. His law is absolute throughout the Empire.
- The [[Cerberus Assembly]] advises the Crown.
- Each town is led by a starosta appointed by the Crown. Lawmasters oversee local justice.
- Every six months, tithe collectors, commonly called Reapers, go from building to building, inspect ledgers, and assess what each citizen owes. They wear black cloaks trimmed in gold and travel with an escort of Crownsguard.

## Military and Law Enforcement

- **Crownsguard:** The Empire's law enforcement, posted in every major city, and its military reserve. They wear vermilion or maroon robes over bronze scale plates and carry crossbows, longswords, and shields.
- **Righteous Brand:** The Empire's army, in plate armor of silver and dark crimson.
- Both are trained at Bladegarden, a city with a large garrison.
- Garrisons in the mountains of the Marrow Valley guard the frontier against incursions from Xhorhas.
- Rumors tell of the Volstrucker, said to be the Empire's secret police, made up of spellcasters.

## Laws

- Necromancy is forbidden.
- Penalties are doubled for crimes against the Crownsguard, soldiers of the Righteous Brand, or nobles, and tripled for crimes against officials of the Cerberus Assembly.

## Religion

- Every temple in the Empire is owned and run by the government.
- Only approved deities may be worshipped: [[Bahamut]], the Platinum Dragon; Erathis, the Lawbearer; Ioun, the Knowing Mistress; [[Moradin]], the All-Hammer; Pelor, the Dawnfather; and the Raven Queen, the Matron of Ravens.
- Worship of any other deity is prosecuted as idol worship and punished with imprisonment.
- Open religious devotion is socially frowned upon, and divine magic is regarded with disdain.

## Travel

- Checkpoints stand on the main roads into Imperial towns, and travelers show their papers at each one.
- At every checkpoint, a clerk asks two questions: where the traveler is coming from (the last stop only) and what their business is.
- The answers are written into the traveler's papers with their name, race, and a brief description. The papers serve as a running travel log: each town's clerk adds an entry instead of issuing new papers.
- A traveler without papers is sent to a clerk, who draws up a set on the spot for a small fee.

## Relations

- **The Clovis Concord:** Trade with the Menagerie Coast is cordial and profitable. Most diplomacy between the two nations takes place in [[Nicodranas]].
- **Xhorhas and the Kryn Dynasty:** The Empire long left Xhorhas to itself, though unrest along the border never ceased. Hostility with the [[Kryn Dynasty]] has turned into open war, fought along the eastern frontier. The Crown has conscripted soldiers and requisitioned property for the war effort.

## History

- The Empire grew out of the Zemni Fields after the Calamity. Manfried Dwendal became its first Emperor in 539 PD.
- The Empire brought the Zemni Fields and the Marrow Valley under its rule, then conquered the Julous Dominion, whose capital was Zadash, in the Marrow War.
- After that war, Manfried took the title of King, which his successors have kept. The Empire is more than twelve generations old.

## Getting There

- **From the Menagerie Coast:** Through the Wuyun Gates, or over the Cyrios Mountains to [[Deastok]].
- **By road:** The Amber Road, the Empire's main north–south highway, runs from the Wuyun Gates through Zadash to Rexxentrum.

## Description

Rexxentrum is the capital of the [[Dwendalian Empire]], in the Zemni Fields. It is a massive city, more than twice the size of [[Emon]]. [[Bertrand Dwendal|King Dwendal]] rules from here, and most members of the [[Cerberus Assembly]] live here. Scholars and aspiring wizards come to the city to study, and it is the center of arcane learning in the Empire.

## Notable Sites

- **Soltryce Academy:** The Empire's foremost school of magic.
- **Cobalt Soul Archive:** The [[Cobalt Soul]] keeps an archive and library in the city.

## Getting There

- The Amber Road runs south from Rexxentrum through [[Zadash]] to the Wuyun Gates.

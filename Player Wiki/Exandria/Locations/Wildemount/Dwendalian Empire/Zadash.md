> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** City
> **Part of:** [[Dwendalian Empire]]

## Description

Zadash is the second-largest city in the [[Dwendalian Empire]] and lies at the geographic center of both the Marrow Valley and the Empire. It was the capital of the Julous Dominion and its last stronghold in the Marrow Valley before the Empire conquered it. Instead of erasing Dominion society, the Empire merged its own laws and customs with those of the city.

Zadash is comparable in size to [[Port Damali]], but where Port Damali spreads out, Zadash builds upward. Its buildings rise and lean toward the streets, and three great towers, the Tri-Spire, can be seen from far off. A storyteller once called it the crown jewel of the Empire's western expansion.

About 89,000 people live in Zadash: roughly 70 percent human, 11 percent halfling, 9 percent dwarf, and 10 percent other peoples.

## Layout

- Stone walls about 15 feet high enclose the city in a rough triangle. At the southern gate, a portcullis hangs from heavy chains, and Crownsguard stand watch on either side.
- Fields of crops lie outside the southern walls. Nearer the walls they give way to tent cities and hovels.
- Inside, interior walls divide the city. Their broad arches form wide tunnels with buildings set into their sides.
- A light rail of carts runs through tunnels in the city. The [[Cerberus Assembly]] manages it and decides who may ride, mostly the upper class.

## Districts

- **Tri-Spire:** The wealthiest district, on the north side, behind a decorative marble wall. Crownsguard guard its two gates, which are enchanted to dispel illusions. It takes its name from three towers: the Triumph Chime, the Zauberspire, and the Constellation Bridge. Within it lie the Silken Terrace, where the city's elite live and work and entry is restricted, and the Gilded Willows, a tree-shaded residential area.
- **Pentamarket:** The center of trade, in the west-central city, where intersecting streets form a pentagon. Carts, tents, and stalls fill an open bazaar every day, selling everyday goods.
- **Innerstead Sprawl:** A middle-class ring that connects all the other districts.
- **Signet Wall:** The district at the eastern end of the city. Marrow Keep, the main base of the Righteous Brand, is here, and recruits enlist there. The Shrine of the Dawn, a shrine to the Dawnfather, also stands here.

## Notable Sites

- **Valley Archive of the Cobalt Soul:** An archive and temple to the Knowing Mistress, maintained by the [[Cobalt Soul]], on the western edge of the Pentamarket. It holds the city's rarest books. Visitors sign in and are escorted by a monk, some areas require special approval, and nothing may be removed. Its tall tapering tower ends in a dome, flanked by three smaller spires.
- **Hall of Erudition:** An archive and school of magic. Its centerpiece is the Zauberspire, whose glass-walled top floor overlooks the city.
- **Triumph Chime:** A seven-story former temple and belfry, hollowed out and filled with gambling halls, bars, inn rooms, and a brothel. Each floor is built of a different colored stone, and silk banners hang from the outside.
- **Pillow Trove:** The largest inn in Zadash, expensive, and popular with diplomats and ambassadors.
- **Ossuary Gates:** A vast, multilayered catacomb graveyard used by the city's leading families. At its center stands the Raven's Den, a shrine to the Matron of Ravens, with a black obsidian statue whose face is a white porcelain mask.
- **Hearth of the All-Hammer:** A domed iron shrine to the [[Moradin|All-Hammer]] on the north side of the Pentamarket, with a great forge and anvil inside.
- **King's Hall:** The seat of the city's administration, where the lawmaster presides.
- **Umber Dungeon:** The city jail.

## Government

- Like other Imperial cities, Zadash is led by a Crown-appointed starosta, and its lawmaster sits in the King's Hall.
- Each shrine is run by a herald, an official of the government who gives sermons and counsel.

## Underworld

- The [[Myriad]] operates in Zadash. Its local boss is known as [[The Gentleman|the Gentleman]].
- Criminals use the city's sewers to move about unseen.
- Tales of the city's underworld describe a labyrinth beneath the streets: old sewers and tunnels, some said to date from the city's founding, and chambers missing from official maps. The Myriad is said to use them as its highways, with hidden entrances beneath taverns and noble homes.

## Getting There

- **North and south:** The Amber Road runs north to [[Rexxentrum]] and south to the Wuyun Gates and the Menagerie Coast.
- **West:** A road climbs through the foothills to [[Deastok]] and on into the [[Cyrios Mountains]].

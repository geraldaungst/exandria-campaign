> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Fallen nation
> **Part of:** [[Xhorhas]]

## Description

Draconia was a nation of [[Dragonborn]] whose cities floated above the Dreemoth Ravine, in what is now southern [[Xhorhas]]. It fell in 811 PD. Its ruins lie in and around the ravine.

## The Nation

- Draconia was founded around 400 PD.
- Draconblood dragonborn ruled it. Ravenite dragonborn, native to the ravine, labored in the lowlands beneath the floating cities and served the Draconbloods.
- The nation was renowned for its magic and its military strength.
- Draconia kept close diplomatic ties with the [[Dwendalian Empire]].

## The Fall

- In 811 PD the Chroma Conclave, an alliance of chromatic dragons, attacked across Exandria. While the rest of the Conclave struck [[Tal'Dorei]], the white dragon Vorugal, called the Frigid Doom, attacked Draconia.
- Draconia's defenses failed. Its floating cities fell into the ravine, and thousands died.
- The adventuring company Vox Machina later slew the dragons of the Conclave.

## Aftermath

- The surviving dragonborn scattered across Exandria.
- The Ravenites threw off Draconblood rule and drove the Draconbloods from the region.
- [[Xarzith Kitril]] was founded in southern Xhorhas as a refuge for the survivors.

## In Tales

The fall of Draconia is a popular subject for storytellers, especially in the taverns of the Menagerie Coast. Retellings embellish and garble the details. A common version claims that the ruling Draconbloods had wings and the Ravenites were born without them. This is false. See [[Dragonborn]].

## Description

Xarzith Kitril, meaning "Cold Jewel" in Draconic, is a [[Dragonborn]] city in southern Xhorhas. It was founded after [[Draconia]] fell to the Chroma Conclave in 811 PD, as a refuge for the dragonborn who survived the attack.

Its buildings combine draconic architecture with practical defenses and are made to withstand a harsh climate. Carvings of dragonborn history decorate them.

## Government

- The city is governed by a council, the Scars of Scale and Tooth, whose members are [[Dragonborn|Ravenite]] dragonborn.
- The Eye of the Scars, Protector of the Council, is responsible for the council's protection. [[Vaud Qalix]] holds the office and leads the Wardens of the Ravine.

## Known For

Its cooking uses black salt and Xhorhasian fire root, a spicy red-orange powder.

## Description

Xhorhas covers the eastern half of Wynandir, beyond the Ashkeeper Peaks from the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. Much of it is harsh, wild land often called the wastes. Drow, goblinoids, orcs, and many other peoples live there, some in independent tribes.

## Geography

- The Ashkeeper Peaks form the border with the Empire.
- The north is ruled by the [[Kryn Dynasty]] from its capital, [[Rosohna]].
- [[Xarzith Kitril]], a dragonborn city, lies in southern Xhorhas.
- The ruins of [[Draconia]], the fallen dragonborn nation, lie at the Dreemoth Ravine.

## Reputation in the Empire

- In the Empire, Xhorhas is widely regarded as a land of monsters. Creatures from Xhorhas are blamed for attacks in eastern Imperial towns.
- The Empire and the Kryn Dynasty are at war along the frontier.

## Known For

Southern Xhorhasian cooking uses black salt and Xhorhasian fire root, a spicy red-orange powder.

## Description

The Tyodan River runs through the [[Menagerie Coast]] and meets the [[Lucidian Ocean]] at [[Port Damali]].

## The Bridge at the Falls

- Northeast of Port Damali, the coastal trade road crosses the river on a single-span stone bridge wide enough for wagons. Its walking surface is worn but sound.
- The bridge spans the river at a waterfall. Travelers hear the falls before the bridge comes into view.
- Rocky outcroppings rise on both banks, and dense vegetation grows close along the approaches.
- A deep pool lies at the base of the falls.

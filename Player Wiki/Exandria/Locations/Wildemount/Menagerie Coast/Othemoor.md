## Description

The Othemoor is a marsh in the Menagerie Coast. Travelers approach it across open, rolling hills. Its edge rises ahead as a wall of dark, gnarled grey-green trees in a thin, hazy mist.

## Conditions

- The air is heavy and humid and smells of stagnant water. Warm rain falls often.
- The road ends at the marsh's edge. Beyond it the ground is mud and shifting, unstable footing, cut through with water-filled channels.
- Twisted willows grow among the marsh trees.

## Reputation

The marsh is known as perilous. Its creatures have ambushed travelers, and many expeditions into it have ended in failure or tragedy.

## History

Decades ago, creatures of the marsh ambushed an expedition led by Brom Stonefoot. Brom died in the Othemoor, and the [[Stonefoot Compass]], which he had made, was lost there. Brom was an ancestor of [[Keldar Stonefoot]], chief cartographer of Port Zoon.

## Description

The Shrine of Melora is an ancient shrine to Melora, the Wildmother, on a solitary hill east of [[Port Damali]]. Little of the original structure survives.

## The Site

- At the top of the hill, the trees open onto a bright clearing with fast-flowing water.
- Bridges span a fast-moving creek. The road leads across the first bridge to an island.
- Only the foundations of the original structure remain, including round stone walls that stand taller in some places than in others.
- Weathered stone pillars, carved with vines, animals, and flowing water, stand in a circle around a granite altar.
- Symbols of the Wildmother appear throughout the site.

## Rededication

- The [[Malachite Cord]] has undertaken to cleanse and repair the shrine and rededicate it to the Aurora's Ascendant, whom the Cord reveres as the Chosen of Melora.
- Newer celestial carvings, including stars, crescents, and a sunburst, now appear on some of the pillars beside the older carvings.

## Getting There

The hill lies east of Port Damali, reached from the Gilded Roadway. An old road paved with set stones climbs it through a dense stand of trees.

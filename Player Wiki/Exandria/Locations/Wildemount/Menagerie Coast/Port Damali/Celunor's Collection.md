## Description

Celunor's Collection is a shop near the Port Damali docks that sells rare books, scrolls, curiosities, and costly rare goods. It is styled as a sunken archive. Salvaged ship parts and maritime artifacts decorate the outside. Inside, water features, dim lighting, and shelves of bottled specimens sit among the books.

## Proprietor

- Celdric Ambril runs the shop and presents himself to customers as Celunor the Tidebound.
- He is a local celebrity and a storyteller who tells a tale about each item he sells.
- He takes commissions to find specific items and to research subjects for customers.

## The Drowned Library

The shop says its stock comes from the Drowned Library, a legendary repository of arcane knowledge that sank long ago. From time to time the shop announces new discoveries from the collection.

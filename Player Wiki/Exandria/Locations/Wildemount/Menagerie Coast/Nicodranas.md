## Description

Nicodranas is the easternmost city of the Menagerie Coast and its second largest, close to the Dwendalian Empire's southern border. Its name means "gathering of colors" in Draconic. The districts mix colorful Menagerie pavilions with gothic Dwendalian architecture. In character the city resembles its larger sister city, [[Port Damali]].

## Known For

- Nicodranas is nicknamed "the Escape." Travelers come for tropical leisure, and many come for its gambling houses.
- Many things that are unlawful in the Empire are regulated but legal here. The city has a reputation as the safest place in Wildemount to indulge in vice.

## Government and Law

- Nicodranas is one of the seven city-states of the Clovis Concord.
- It serves as an embassy city between the two powers. Most diplomacy between the Concord and the Dwendalian Empire takes place here.
- Both the imperial Crownsguard and the [[Zhelezo]] keep the peace, and both imperial and Concord law apply in the city.
- A sizable army is stationed in the city to discourage imperial ambitions.

## Trade

- The Empire is the city's main foreign trading partner.
- Some successful Dwendalian merchants have moved here with the Cerberus Assembly's approval.

## Notable Sites

- **Opal Archways:** A loop of eleven stone arches, placed seemingly at random. They were set up at the city's founding to mark its edges, before Nicodranas grew far beyond them. The surrounding district is full of dens of indulgence, all under Zhelezo supervision. Streets nearer the Archways are safer.
- **Lavish Chateau:** Home and stage of [[Marion Lavorre]], the Ruby of the Sea.

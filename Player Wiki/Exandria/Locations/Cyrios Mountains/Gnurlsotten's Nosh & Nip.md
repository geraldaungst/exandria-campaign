> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Roadside tavern and cheesery
> **Part of:** [[Cyrios Mountains]]

## Description

A tavern and cheesery at the edge of the [[Cyrios Mountains]], on the road toward [[Deastok]] and, beyond it, [[Zadash]]. It stands on its own rather than in any town. Regulars come from nearby settlements, and travelers on the mountain road stop here. The proprietor is [[Grent Gnurlsotten]].

## The Building

- A low building of mountain logs set against the hillside, with a goat pen alongside.
- The sign over the door reads **Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip**. A second board below it, in different lettering and added later, reads **"…and Cheesery."**
- Inside is a single low-ceilinged room with four round tables and a stone hearth at the far end. The back wall is bare rock where the building meets the mountain, and the room smells faintly mineral, like the inside of a cave.
- Cloth-wrapped wheels of pale cheese are stacked along one wall.
- The building encloses a natural cave in the rock, where the cheese is aged. Grent lets no one into it.

## Known For

- [[Cave-Aged Truscan]], a cheese made here and nowhere else.

## Customs

Newcomers are traditionally put through a series of challenges that ends in a nonlethal brawl with Grent. The regulars watch and bet on the outcome.

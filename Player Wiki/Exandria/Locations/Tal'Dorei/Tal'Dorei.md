## Description

Tal'Dorei is a continent across the Lucidian Ocean from Wildemount. Most of it belongs to the Republic of Tal'Dorei, governed from [[Emon]].

## Government

- The Tal'Dorei Council governs the Republic from Emon.
- Tal'Dorei was once a monarchy ruled by sovereigns. It became a republic after the Chroma Conclave's attack.
- Several cities govern themselves, including [[Whitestone]], the elven city of Syngorn, and the dwarven city of Kraghammer.

## Geography

- Regions include the Alabaster Sierras, the Bladeshimmer Shoreline, the Cliffkeep Mountains, the Dividing Plains, the Lucidian Coast, and the Rifenmist Peninsula.
- Westruun is a major city of the Republic.

## History

- In 811 PD the Chroma Conclave, an alliance of chromatic dragons led by Thordak the Cinder King, attacked Tal'Dorei and devastated Emon.
- The adventuring company Vox Machina slew the dragons of the Conclave.
- Emon was rebuilt after the attack.

## Getting There

Skyships connect Emon and Whitestone with other cities, including Port Damali in Wildemount.

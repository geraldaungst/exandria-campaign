> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** City-state
> **Part of:** [[Tal'Dorei]]

## Description

Whitestone is a city-state in a valley of the Alabaster Sierras, in northeastern Tal'Dorei. The Parchwood forest surrounds it. Winters are long and snowy, and snow can linger into spring.

## Government

The de Rolo family rules Whitestone through the Chamber of Whitestone. Lord [[Percival de Rolo]] and Lady [[Vex'ahlia de Rolo]] are familiar figures in the city, as is Lady Vex'ahlia's armored bear, Trinket.

## Notable Sites

- **The Sun Tree:** A glowing tree on a small hill in Dawnfather Square, visible from across the city. It is sacred to the Dawnfather.
- **Castle Whitestone:** The seat of the de Rolo family, on a hill above the city.
- **[[Shrine of Bahamut]]:** A small shrine of whitewashed stone.
- **[[Tipsy Quorum]]:** A tavern where members of Vox Machina are sometimes seen.

## Known For

- **Whitestone:** A mineral mined in the region and the source of residuum, a valued magical material. The city also exports granite and limestone.
- **The Festival of Renewal:** Held at the end of winter, with music, food, and performers, and visitors and merchants from far away. At the Lantern Release, people write their hopes and regrets on paper lanterns and release them into the night sky.

## History

- Settlers from Wildemount founded Whitestone generations ago around the Sun Tree, which they took as a blessing of the Dawnfather.
- The Briarwoods seized the city and killed most of the de Rolo family. Percival de Rolo and Vox Machina later retook it.
- During the Chroma Conclave's attack in 811 PD, Whitestone sheltered refugees from Emon.

## Getting There

Whitestone has a skyship port with routes to other cities, including Emon and [[Port Damali]].

## Description

Emon, the City of Fellowship, is the capital and largest city of the Republic of Tal'Dorei and the seat of the Tal'Dorei Council. High walls enclose the city, and its harbor opens onto a large inlet on the southwest shore.

## Districts

- **Cloudtop District:** Crowns the hill at the center of the city. Home to the government and nobility, and where the Tal'Dorei Council convenes.
- **Erudite Quarter:** The center of higher learning for all of Tal'Dorei.
- **Military District:** Barracks and training grounds for the Arms of Emon, the city guard, and for the Tal'Dorei military.
- **Cemetery District:** Dedicated to burial of the city's dead.
- **Port of Emon:** The harbor.

## History

- The Chroma Conclave devastated Emon in 811 PD.
- The city was rebuilt afterward, much of it with magic.

## Getting There

Skyships connect Emon with other cities, including Whitestone and Port Damali.

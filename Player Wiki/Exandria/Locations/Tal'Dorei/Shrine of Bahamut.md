> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Shrine
> **Part of:** [[Whitestone]]

## Description

A modest shrine to [[Bahamut]] built of whitewashed stone, with a slate roof and a single small spire. A dragon's head in profile, the symbol of Bahamut, is carved above a plain wooden door.

Inside, tapestries hang along the walls. At the far end, a small altar holds a silver icon of Bahamut in dragon form, with sapphire eyes. The shrine is used for quiet meditation and reflection rather than elaborate rites.

## The Tapestries

The tapestries show scenes from Bahamut's lore, including his battles against his sister Tiamat.

1. **Bahamut as a humble elder:** Bahamut walks the streets of Whitestone as an old man with gray hair and a neatly braided gray beard.
2. **The creation of the dragonborn:** Bahamut creates the first dragonborn.
3. **Guidance of metallic dragons and dragonborn:** Bahamut leads metallic dragons and dragonborn.
4. **Gifts of wisdom and wealth:** Bahamut bestows wisdom and wealth.
5. **Bahamut and the Celestial Court:** Bahamut in his celestial realm among the other deities.
6. **Bahamut's promise to Whitestone:** Bahamut, as an elderly man, stands under the Sun Tree in conversation with Lord [[Percival de Rolo]], who gestures toward the city. Lady [[Vex'ahlia de Rolo]] stands beside Percival with Trinket, her armored brown bear. The de Rolo children are gathered nearby.

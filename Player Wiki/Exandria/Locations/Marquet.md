## Description

Marquet is a continent far to the southwest of Wildemount, across the Lucidian Ocean. Much of it is desert and mountain, including the Aggrad Mountains. Its people speak Marquesian, and Marquesian goods and travelers are common in the ports of Wildemount.

## Ank'Harel

- Ank'Harel, in the heart of the desert, is Marquet's great center of culture, history, and power.
- Its streets are known for their safety, music, and culture, unlike the harsh desert around them.
- A city guard, the Hands of Ord, keeps the peace in the city and the nearby villages.
- Ank'Harel has a skyport.

## J'mon Sa Ord

- J'mon Sa Ord rules Ank'Harel and has done so since the city's founding, centuries ago.
- The ruler's long, unbroken reign has made J'mon Sa Ord a figure of legend.

## Ties to Wildemount

- [[Port Damali]] was founded as a colony of Marquet.
- Ships from Marquet call at Port Damali, and skyships also arrive from Marquet at its skyport.
- Marquesian spices are traded through Port Damali.
- The Marquet fragment of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]] was found in Marquet.

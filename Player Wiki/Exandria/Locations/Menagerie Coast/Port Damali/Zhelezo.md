The Zhelezo are the city guard and law enforcement of the Clovis Concord. They keep the peace in the Concord's cities along the Menagerie Coast.

## In Port Damali

- [[Port Damali]] has a large Zhelezo presence. They guard the city alongside its army.
- Zhelezo officers take charge of incidents in the harbor, securing the scene and posting guards.
- Zhelezo officers in the city can be found at Primework Station.
- Myriad operatives are common in the city's law enforcement, including the Zhelezo.

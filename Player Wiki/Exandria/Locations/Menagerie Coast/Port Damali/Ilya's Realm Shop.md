## Description

Ilya's Realm Shop is a high-end magic shop in Port Damali. Its owner, Ilya, is a retired adventurer who settled in the city after traveling Exandria.

## Stock and Services

- Magic items from Ilya's own adventuring days.
- A broad stock of common and uncommon magic items.
- Custom commissions, crafted to order.

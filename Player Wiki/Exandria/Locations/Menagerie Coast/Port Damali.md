## Description

Port Damali is the largest city on the western coast of Wildemount, a seaside metropolis where the [[Tyodan River]] meets the Lucidian Ocean. Called the jewel of the Menagerie Coast, it began as a colony of Marquet and is regarded as the unofficial capital of free trade outside the Dwendalian Empire. Music, color, and commerce fill its streets, and ships from across the world bring a mix of cultures and trades.

About 82,000 people live in the city. Roughly half are human, with large numbers of halflings and elves.

## Geography

- The city sits on a cluster of leveled hills. Changes in elevation mark the boundaries between wards.
- Architecture varies from ward to ward. The inner wards show their history or their wealth. The outer wards are poorer and crowded, brightened with cheap, colorful banners and street performers.
- Farmsteads, tents, and shacks mark the city's outer edge.
- The Prism Path, a network of main roads paved with smooth, multicolored stones gathered from the shore, runs through the inner wards.

## Districts

- **Tumbledowns:** Home to most of the city's people, in cramped houses of every style: clay domes, thatched huts, and wooden shacks along winding streets. Several small temples stand here.
- **Beaded Alley:** The center of general trade. Cords hung with beads and streamers stretch over each intersection, and performers work the crowds.
- **Crescents:** An affluent ward of rival guild families and well-connected nobles. It takes its name from seven large crescent-moon statues, a gift from the Alkamar family at the city's founding. The [[Pearl Shrine]], a temple to Avandra, stands here.
- **Gilded Esplanade:** The city's most extravagant ward. Its streets are lined with celebrated artists and performers and with markets of expensive goods and imports. It holds the Exalted Collection Auction House, venues for the city's festivals, and the skyport.
- **Larboard Light:** The great harbor along the city's southern edge, an upscale dockside ward where dozens of ships arrive each day. The office of the marquis stands at its northern end.

## Government

- Marquis [[Olesya Lapidus]] leads the city, governing with its guildmasters and nobles.
- Port Damali is one of the seven city-states of the Clovis Concord. Concord law is enforced more strictly in some cities than others. In Port Damali, the law is for sale like any other goods.
- A sizable army and many [[Zhelezo]] guard the city. Nobles and wealthy merchants keep their own bodyguards.
- The Concord maintains a navy of nearly four hundred warships. About a quarter of it is based in and around Port Damali.

## Crime

- Crime, murder, and corruption run deep in the city. Many residents call this an unfortunate but necessary part of how Port Damali works.
- The city is a long-standing center of activity for the [[Myriad]], a crime syndicate. Merchant guilds run most businesses, but the Myriad is the power behind the guilds, and nearly all major commerce has ties to it. Many powerful merchants and company owners are members or are indentured to it.
- Myriad operatives are common in the city's law enforcement. Most crime is covered up or ignored unless it interferes with Myriad interests.
- Port Damali is the center of Exandria's slave trade. Slavers pass through often, and those who fail the Myriad can be shipped away as cargo.

## Known For

- **Trade:** Nearly any goods or services can be found here, legal or otherwise. Inns, taverns, underground markets, and rare auctions are plentiful.
- **Shipping:** The city is the hub for most seafaring business across the Lucidian Ocean.
- **Temples:** Temples to the Prime Deities are common.

## History

- Port Damali was founded as a colony of Marquet.
- Five members of the Lapidus family, including the current marquis, have led the city.
- While the city's leadership was occupied with the rise of the Revelry, a pirate fleet, the long-dormant Myriad took control of many of the city's guilds.

## Getting There

- **By sea:** The harbor in the Larboard Light receives ships from across the Lucidian Ocean and beyond.
- **By air:** Port Damali has the only skyport in Wildemount, a spire in the Gilded Esplanade. Skyships connect it with [[Emon]] and [[Whitestone]], and ships also arrive from Marquet. Arrivals draw crowds.
- **By land:** Trade routes leave the city in every direction.

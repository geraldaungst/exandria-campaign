## Description

Port Zoon is one of the major cities of the Menagerie Coast and one of the seven city-states of the Clovis Concord. It is an industrial city. Its industrial districts are prominent, and machinery, smoke, and smog mark the city.

## Guilds

- Artisan and crafting guilds are numerous and powerful.
- The Port Zoon Cartographers Guild is led by its chief cartographer, [[Keldar Stonefoot]].

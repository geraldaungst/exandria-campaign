> **Type:** Hideout
> **Part of:** [[Port Damali]]

## Description

Aelorin's Headquarters is a hidden base of the [[Clasp]] beneath a tailor shop in Port Damali. It takes its name from Aelorin, a tall elf and a senior figure in the Clasp.

## The Shop

- Evaldo's Elegant Attire, a tailor shop, serves as the front. Its showroom displays finished garments, mannequins, and fabric swatches, with a workshop behind it.
- The building stands on a larger lot than most in its part of town. A path leads to the front door between two short walls, and clusters of cypress trees, uncommon inside the city, flank the entrance.
- A limestone bench sits among cypress trees beside the shop.

## The Hideout

- A stair leads down from the shop. A second, concealed exit rises to the bench beside the shop.
- Traps guard the hidden entrances and the main corridor.
- Rooms include a guard post, living quarters, a study and library, and private chambers. A brass plate with a needle-and-thread stitch pattern marks Aelorin's own room.

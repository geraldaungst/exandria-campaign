## Description

The Pudgy Pigeon is an inn and tavern in Port Damali, in a two-story stone-and-timber building with a weather-worn front. Its painted sign shows a plump gray pigeon perched on a tankard. A small covered porch with benches faces the street.

## Inside

- The ground-floor taproom has a bar along one wall, scattered wooden tables, and a small raised platform for occasional entertainment.
- An alcove near the entrance holds historical memorabilia.
- A side entrance leads to the rooms upstairs.

## Services

- A full food and drink menu.
- Three small rooms for rent on the upper floor, with a shared washroom.

## Reputation

The regulars are known for being unfriendly to newcomers.

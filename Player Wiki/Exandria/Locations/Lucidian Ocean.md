> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Ocean

## Description

The Lucidian Ocean lies south of [[Wildemount]] and southeast of [[Tal'Dorei]]. [[Marquet]] lies across it, far to the southwest of Wildemount.

## Coasts and Ports

- The [[Menagerie Coast]] runs along Wildemount's southwestern shore. Five of its cities have open ports, and ships from across the ocean call at them.
- [[Port Damali]], where the [[Tyodan River]] meets the ocean, handles most of the ocean's seafaring business. Ships from Marquet and beyond call at its harbor.

## Islands

- The Swavain Islands lie off the Menagerie Coast, with shipping posts and dangerous reefs. They are a homeland of the [[Ki'Nau]].
- The Shattered Teeth are an archipelago, often hidden by a persistent fog bank called the Fool's Curtain.

## Hazards

- **Diver's Grave:** a stretch of rough water with unusually frequent, fierce storms. Beneath it lies a deep seabed valley strewn with shipwrecks, where merrow live.
- **Piracy:** The pirates of the Revelry prey on the ocean's trade lanes. The Clovis Concord's navy numbers nearly four hundred warships.

## Peoples of the Sea

- Merfolk, sea elves, sahuagin, and merrow live in its waters.
- Some who live in and near the sea, merrow among them, worship the leviathan [[Uk'otoa]]. Legend holds that Uk'otoa lies sealed beneath the Lucidian Ocean.

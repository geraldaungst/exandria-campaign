Moradin, the All-Hammer, is one of the Prime Deities of Exandria (see [[Gods of Exandria]]) and the god of craft, creation, family, and legacy. He is the patron of craftspeople, smiths, artisans, and miners, and of home and family. Most dwarves worship him. He is said to have shaped the mountains when the world was young.

## Depictions

- His worshippers depict him as a faceless, stout, dwarflike figure of immense strength, hunched over and holding a flaming heart in his hands.
- A common symbol of his worship is a hammer whose ends are carved as dwarven heads. Anvils also bear his symbol.

## Teachings

The All-Hammer's commandments call on his followers to:

- remain stoic and tenacious in the face of catastrophe;
- uphold loyalty to family, to clan, and to their people;
- create things that last, since legacy is paramount.

## Worship

- Moradin is one of the deities approved for worship in the [[Dwendalian Empire]].
- Many of his temples stand at the heart of dwarven strongholds.
- His holy day is Deep Solace, on the eighteenth of Unndilar.

## Places of Worship

- [[New Temple of Moradin]]: a dwarven temple in the Crescents ward of [[Port Damali]]
- Hearth of the All-Hammer: a domed iron shrine with a great forge and anvil, on the north side of the Pentamarket in [[Zadash]]

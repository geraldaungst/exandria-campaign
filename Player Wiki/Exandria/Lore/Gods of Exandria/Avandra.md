Avandra, the Changebringer, is one of the Prime Deities of Exandria (see [[Gods of Exandria]]) and the goddess of change, freedom, trade, travel, and adventure. She encourages her followers to venture into the unknown.

## Teachings

The Changebringer's commandments hold that:

- luck favors the bold, and those who grasp their own fate have the Changebringer behind them;
- change is inevitable, and the righteous can make sure it is change for the better;
- tyranny must be resisted: fight for the freedom of yourself and others when you can, and inspire others to fight when you cannot.

## Worship

- Her worshippers include merchants, free spirits, and adventurers.
- Few of her temples stand in the centers of cities. Her shrines are more often found near well-traveled roads.
- Avandra is not among the deities approved for worship in the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. Some worship her there in secret.
- Her holy day is New Dawn, on the first of Horisal.

## Places of Worship

- [[Pearl Shrine]]: a temple to Avandra in the Crescents ward of [[Port Damali]]

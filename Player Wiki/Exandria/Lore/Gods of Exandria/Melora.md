Melora, the Wildmother, is one of the Prime Deities of Exandria (see [[Gods of Exandria]]) and the goddess of the wilderness and the sea. She watches over nature and good harvests, offers protection from storms, and guides the passage of ships.

## Teachings

The Wildmother's commandments call on her followers to:

- protect the untamed wilderness from exploitation and destruction;
- slay abominations and other mockeries of nature;
- embrace the savage nature of the world and live in harmony with it.

## Worship

- Worship of the Wildmother is banned in the [[Dwendalian Empire]].
- Her holy day is Wild's Grandeur.
- The [[Malachite Cord]] reveres the Aurora's Ascendant as the Chosen of Melora.

## Places of Worship

- [[Shrine of Melora]]: an ancient hilltop shrine east of [[Port Damali]]

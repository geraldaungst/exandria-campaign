Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon, is one of the Prime Deities of Exandria (see [[Gods of Exandria]]). He is the god of the metallic dragons and the creator of the [[dragonborn]]. He is the brother and sworn enemy of Tiamat, the Scaled Tyrant, queen of the chromatic dragons.

## Teachings

The Platinum Dragon's commandments call on his followers to:

- stand as examples of honor and justice;
- oppose evil wherever it is found, while showing compassion to those who have strayed;
- defend the weak, bring freedom to those who lack it, and protect just order.

## Depictions

- As a dragon. The silver icon in the [[Shrine of Bahamut]] shows him in dragon form.
- As an armored warrior with a dragon's face and folded wings, as in the statue at the checkpoint plaza in [[Deastok]].
- As an old man with gray hair and a neatly braided gray beard, walking among mortals.

His symbol is a dragon's head in profile.

## Worship

- Bahamut is one of the deities approved for worship in the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. Imperial art pairs him with the Crown: the statue in Deastok shows him with a gauntleted hand on the King's shoulder.
- His holy day is Embertide, on the fifth of Duscar.

## Places of Worship

- [[Shrine of Bahamut]]: a modest shrine in [[Whitestone]], hung with tapestries of his lore
- [[Temple of Bahamut in Deastok]]: Deastok's only official temple, under a platinum dome

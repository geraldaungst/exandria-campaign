Rifts are openings between Exandria and other planes of existence. They are appearing across Wildemount and, according to the [[Malachite Cord]], throughout Exandria. Their origin is unknown.

## Nature

- Each rift connects to a particular plane. Through a large enough rift, the landscape of the plane beyond is visible.
- Rifts vary in size and color.
- Creatures from other planes come through them.
- The Malachite Cord warns that many other planes are evil or chaotic, and that Exandria should not be permanently connected to them.

## Containment

Riftcages, devices made of soulwood, siphon energy from a rift and shrink it. They cannot close a rift entirely.

## Known Sites

- A rift at the [[Shrine of Melora]] opened onto the Elemental Plane of Fire.
- Rifts have also opened within [[Port Damali]].

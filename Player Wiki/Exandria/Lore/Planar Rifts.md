Rifts are unstable openings between Exandria and other planes of existence. They are appearing across Wildemount and, according to the [[Malachite Cord]], throughout Exandria. Their origin is unknown.

## Nature

- Each rift connects to a particular plane. Rifts vary in size and color.
- Some flicker like candle flames. Others stay open for generations. Some vanish.
- Uncontrolled rifts widen. Wider tears admit larger creatures, stronger magic, and even weather from the plane beyond.

## Effects on the Land

- Active rifts bleed raw, unshaped magic into the surrounding land. The magic carries the character of the plane beyond.
- Spells cast near a rift surge unpredictably.
- Plants twist or overgrow, and beasts turn aggressive or warped.
- Stray motes of elemental or necrotic energy drift through the air.
- The longer a rift stays open, the more the land around it suffers.

## Crossings

- Creatures cross through rifts. Some are pulled through by the rift itself, without meaning to cross.
- Species from unknown planes have been recorded near active rifts.
- Small pieces of foreign terrain, such as stone, vines, and pockets of air, appear around the tears.
- The Malachite Cord warns that many other planes are evil or chaotic.

## Signs of a Nearby Rift

- Air vibrating faintly
- Colors shimmering unnaturally
- Soulwood trees creaking without wind
- Wildlife falling silent

## Containment

Riftcages, devices made of soulwood, draw off a rift's leaking energy and shrink it. They cannot close a rift entirely.

## Known Sites

- Rift sites have been documented in the [[Savalirwood]], the Ashkeeper Peaks, the [[Cyrios Mountains]], and Ebonglass.
- A rift at the [[Shrine of Melora]] opened onto the Elemental Plane of Fire.
- Rifts have also opened within cities, including [[Port Damali]] and [[Port Zoon]].

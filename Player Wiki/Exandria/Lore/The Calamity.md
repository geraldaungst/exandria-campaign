The Calamity was the war between the Prime Deities and the Betrayer Gods, fought on Exandria with mortals on both sides. It devastated whole civilizations and reshaped the world. For the gods involved and the pantheons they belong to, see [[Gods of Exandria]].

## The Age of Arcanum

- The era before the Calamity is called the Age of Arcanum.
- Mortals had received the gift of arcane magic from the Prime Deities and used it to build powerful empires.
- Ruins and works from this age, often called pre-Calamity, still survive. The [[Lorestone of Eryndor]] is one such relic.

## The War

- The war began after the Betrayer Gods were freed from their planar prisons.
- Wildemount suffered the worst of the fighting. The final battles were fought in what is now [[Xhorhas]], where creatures that fought for the Betrayer Gods still roam the wastes.
- Vasselheim is said to be the only city in Exandria that survived the Calamity.

## The Divergence

- The war ended when the Betrayer Gods were banished again and the Prime Deities withdrew with them. The gods raised the Divine Gate, which keeps every god from physically entering the world. This withdrawal is known as the Divergence.
- Years are counted from the Divergence, written PD (Post-Divergence). See [[Calendar of Exandria]].

## Relics

Weapons and artifacts from the Calamity are still found across Exandria.

- **Vestiges of Divergence:** the weapons and tools of the gods' champions, and the greatest works of the artificers and mages of the Age of Arcanum.
- **Arms of the Betrayers:** weapons made for and wielded by the champions of the Betrayer Gods.

## Legacy

- Wildemount bears the deepest scars of the war, and its ancient battlegrounds are dangerous places.
- The [[Pallid Elves]] trace their origin to a catastrophe of the Calamity.

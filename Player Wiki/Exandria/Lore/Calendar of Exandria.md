The calendar used across Exandria was established by elves in an ancient age. Its names for the days and months are used by peoples throughout the world.

## Reckoning the Year

- Years are counted from the Divergence, the end of [[The Calamity]], and written PD (Post-Divergence).
- A year has 328 days in 11 months. A day has 24 hours.
- The week has seven days: Miresen, Grissen, Whelsen, Conthsen, Folsen, Yulisen, and Da'leysen. Many people treat Yulisen and Da'leysen as the weekend.

## Seasons

- **Spring** begins on the 13th of Dualahei.
- **Summer** begins at noon on the 26th of Unndilar. It is the shortest season.
- **Autumn** begins on the 3rd of Fessuran.
- **Winter** begins on the 2nd of Duscar. It is the longest season.

Dragonborn are a people created by [[Bahamut]], the Platinum Dragon. They are common in Wildemount, and most of Wildemount's dragonborn trace their roots to [[Draconia]]. They fall into two lineages, Draconblood and Ravenite.

## Draconbloods

- Draconbloods descend from dragonborn who came to the Dreemoth Ravine from elsewhere and built Draconia there.
- They have tails.
- They ruled Draconia until its fall in 811 PD. Afterward, the Ravenites drove them from the ravine.

## Ravenites

- Ravenites are native to the Dreemoth Ravine.
- They have no tails and are typically larger and hardier than Draconbloods.
- Under Draconia, they labored and served beneath the Draconblood rulers. After Draconia fell, they overthrew Draconblood rule.
- The council that governs [[Xarzith Kitril]] is Ravenite.

## Wings

Tales of Draconia's fall, often told on the Menagerie Coast, claim that the Draconbloods ruled because they had wings and that the Ravenites were born wingless. These tales are false. Tails, not wings, distinguish the two lineages.

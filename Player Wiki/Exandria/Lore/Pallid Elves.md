Pallid elves are elves with skin as pale as the moon and white hair. They are devoted to Sehanine, the Moonweaver.

## Origin

- Their ancestors were reclusive elves who worshipped the Moonweaver and lived in a woodland on a mountain plateau in the [[Cyrios Mountains]], hidden by the illusions of their moon priests.
- During [[The Calamity]], servants of Torog, the Crawling King, burrowed beneath the plateau. It sank, and the woodland became a valley of bleached, petrified trees known as the Pallid Grove.
- Survivors hid underground among the sunken roots for generations. Over that time they became the pallid elves.

## The Wider World

- Pallid elves emerged from the Pallid Grove only after long isolation, and many travel Exandria with open curiosity about a world that changed without them.
- They are known as insightful and perceptive.
- In the Clovis Concord they are generally met with friendly curiosity.
- Sehanine's worship is banned in the [[Dwendalian Empire]].

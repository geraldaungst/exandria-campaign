The Stonefoot Compass is a magic compass made by Brom Stonefoot, an ancestor of [[Keldar Stonefoot]], chief cartographer of [[Port Zoon]]. It is a Stonefoot family heirloom.

## Description

- The case is dark, weathered ironwood, carved with winding vines and ancient dwarven runes. Some of the runes still hold traces of gold leaf.
- A domed glass cover protects an ivory face marked with runes in place of letters. The needle is black steel shaped like a leaf, with a small moss agate at its center.
- A brass ring around the edge is notched for fine navigation, and a folding magnifying lens is hinged to one side.
- A tarnished brass plate on the back bears the maker's mark, a foot carved from stone, and the inscription "To find one's path is to know one's self."

## Properties

- Its needle always points true north. Weather, magnetism, magical distortion, and illusion do not turn it.
- Once a day, its holder can use it to find the way to a place, to locate an object, or to locate a kind of animal or plant. Guidance toward a place or an object lasts only while the holder keeps the compass in hand.

## History

Decades ago, Brom led an expedition into the [[Othemoor]], where creatures of the marsh ambushed it. Brom died there, and the compass was lost with him.

Soulwood is a rare wood regarded as both sacred and magical. It insulates against magic.

## Appearance

Soulwood is unusually dense and dark, with a faint iridescence.

## Legend

According to legend, long ago a group of druids, desperate to save the world, infused their souls into the wood. Their essence remains in it.

## Uses

- **Riftcages:** The [[Malachite Cord]] makes Riftcages from soulwood (see below) and seeks out any soulwood it can find.
- **The memorial chamber:** Four soulwood disks are set into the floor of the memorial chamber in the [[Monastery of Dimensional Harmony]]. Their alignment directs planar flow through the room.

## Riftcages

- A Riftcage is a palm-sized, egg-shaped device of dark soulwood with runes carved around it.
- It siphons energy from a [[Planar Rifts|rift]] and shrinks it. It must be held in place long enough to draw the energy away, and it cannot close a rift entirely.
- Few Riftcages exist, too few to contain the rifts spreading across Exandria.

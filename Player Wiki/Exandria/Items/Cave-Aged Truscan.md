A goat's-milk cheese made by [[Grent Gnurlsotten]] at [[Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip]] in the [[Cyrios Mountains]], and among the most sought-after cheeses in Wildemount. It is made nowhere else.

## Making

- Made from the milk of Grent's own goats.
- Aged in a cold natural cave enclosed by the tavern, which Grent lets no one enter.
- Sold as pale wheels wrapped in cloth.

## Price

- 80 gp a wheel at the tavern.
- Resold elsewhere for roughly double.

## Effect

For about an hour after eating a portion, a person can make their voice boom far louder than normal, clear enough to carry a great distance over wind, crowds, or storm. The cheese has no other effect.

## Rumors

Popular rumor credits the cheese's quality to a blood sacrifice, a lost dwarven recipe, or a pact with a cave spirit.

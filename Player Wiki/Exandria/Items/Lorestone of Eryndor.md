The Lorestone of Eryndor is an ancient obsidian disc, made before the Calamity, that bears the [[Prophecy of Eryndor]]. It was shattered, and its shards lie scattered across Exandria.

## Description

- The disc is obsidian, inscribed in lines of silvery mithral.
- The text is written in an ancient dialect of Elvish. Reading it requires fluency in Elvish and deep knowledge of history.
- Its shards are jagged and heavier than their size suggests.

## Shards

- Each shard carries part of the prophecy.
- Each shard is magical. It senses the direction of nearby planar rifts, the plane each one opens onto, and the rift's approximate size and stability.
- A shard grows warm near planar disturbances, more strongly the closer it comes.
- Close to a rift, a shard's bearer can perceive this plane and the plane beyond at the same time.

## Known Shards

- The [[Obsidian Echoforge]] holds four shards.
- The Marquet fragment was found in [[Marquet]]. Its lines are recorded under [[Prophecy of Eryndor#Lines on the Marquet Fragment|Prophecy of Eryndor]].

## Interest in the Lorestone

- The Obsidian Echoforge seeks to reassemble the Lorestone, believing it can close the [[Planar Rifts|rifts]].
- The [[Malachite Cord]] opposes reassembling it, fearing catastrophe.
- [[Vaud Qalix]] seeks its shards.

Solvei's Vigilance is a masterwork longbow that belonged to Solvei, one of the three founders of the [[Monastery of Dimensional Harmony]]. The spirit of [[Brother Kelmen]], a monk of the monastery, resides in it.

## History

- The bow lay on a stone slab before Solvei's tomb, in the east alcove of the monastery's memorial chamber. Each founder's weapon there stands for a virtue, and Solvei's bow stands for precision.
- When a ritual in the memorial chamber was completed, Brother Kelmen's spirit came to reside in the bow.

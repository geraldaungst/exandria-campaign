The Blade of Maroth Fenn is a kukri-style dagger. [[Dreyara Drimvar]] carried it as a personal gift from [[Vaud Qalix]], without knowing what it was.

## Description

- About fifteen inches from pommel to tip, with the cutting edge on the inner curve and jagged serrations along the spine.
- The blade is nearly black, with a silver spiral pattern through the steel.
- The grip is yellowed bone, smooth and well worn. The guard and pommel are silver and wood, once covered in delicate carvings that are now mostly worn away.

The Myriad is the largest criminal syndicate in Wildemount, with a presence in its major cities. Its members favor bribery, blackmail, and debts of favor over open violence.

## History

- The Myriad began as a shipping company in the Dwendalian city of Yrrosa that turned to smuggling.
- It grew into a well-hidden criminal network across Wildemount, then spread to the coastal cities of [[Tal'Dorei]], including Stilben and Kymal.

## In Port Damali

- [[Port Damali]] is one of the Myriad's long-standing strongholds. The syndicate is the power behind most of the city's merchant guilds.
- It took control of many of those guilds while the city's leaders were occupied with the rise of the Revelry, a pirate fleet.

## In Zadash

- A Myriad boss known as the Gentleman runs its operations in [[Zadash]].
- According to tales of the city's underworld, the Myriad moves through old sewers and tunnels beneath Zadash, and a Crownsguard crackdown captured many of its members after a plot against a Starosta.

## Rivals

- The Myriad's expansion into Tal'Dorei brought it into conflict with the [[Clasp]]. The two have fought sporadic gang wars in [[Emon]].

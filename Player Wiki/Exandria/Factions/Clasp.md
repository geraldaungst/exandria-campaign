The Clasp is a guild of thieves, assassins, black-market dealers, and contract brokers. It began in [[Tal'Dorei]] and keeps hideouts in cities across that continent.

## History

- The Clasp was built on the remains of the old Winksman Thieves Guild, founded during the rule of Drassig in Tal'Dorei.
- Clasp members saved many lives during the Chroma Conclave's attack on [[Emon]].

## Rivals

- The Clasp and the [[Myriad]] are rivals. The two have fought sporadic gang wars in Emon.

## In Port Damali

- The Clasp also operates in [[Port Damali]], where the Myriad is the dominant syndicate.
- Its hidden base in the city is [[Aelorin's Headquarters]], beneath a tailor shop. Aelorin, a tall elf, is a senior figure in the Clasp.

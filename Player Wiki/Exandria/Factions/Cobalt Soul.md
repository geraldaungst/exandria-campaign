The Cobalt Soul is an order devoted to Ioun, the Knowing Mistress, goddess of knowledge. Its members dedicate themselves to uncovering the truth and to acquiring, preserving, and sharing knowledge.

## Archives

- The order keeps archives in major cities. Each archive is both a library and a temple to Ioun.
- The order coordinates its archives from [[Rexxentrum]].
- The archives are open to those who seek knowledge. Some collections are restricted and require special approval. In some archives, visitors sign in and are escorted by a monk, and nothing may be removed.

### Known Archives

- **Rexxentrum:** The order's archive and library in the capital of the Empire.
- **Zadash:** The Valley Archive of the Cobalt Soul, on the western edge of the Pentamarket in [[Zadash]], holds the city's rarest books.
- **Port Damali:** The [[Cobalt Soul Archive in Port Damali]].

## Members

The order's members include monks and archivists, who maintain the archives and assist those who come to study.

## In the Dwendalian Empire

Ioun is one of the deities approved for worship in the [[Dwendalian Empire]], and the Cobalt Soul operates openly there.

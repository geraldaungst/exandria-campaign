The Emissaries of the Sunfall are an organization with a long history in [[Rexxentrum]]. They are known for seeking ways to harness power, both for their own benefit and, by their own account, for the benefit of Exandria.

## Leadership

The Emissaries are led by Valen Elderguard.

## Relations with the Obsidian Echoforge

- The Emissaries were once open enemies of the [[Obsidian Echoforge]], at times violently so.
- The two groups have since begun working together.

Vox Machina is a famous adventuring company of [[Tal'Dorei]].

## Members

- Grog Strongjaw, a goliath barbarian
- Keyleth, a half-elf druid of the Ashari
- [[Percival de Rolo]], Lord of Whitestone
- Pike Trickfoot, a gnome cleric of Sarenrae
- Scanlan Shorthalt, a gnome bard
- Vax'ildan, a half-elf rogue and Vex'ahlia's twin brother
- [[Vex'ahlia de Rolo]], Lady of Whitestone and a ranger

## Deeds

- Retook [[Whitestone]] from the Briarwoods, who had seized the city and killed most of the de Rolo family.
- Slew the dragons of the Chroma Conclave, led by Thordak the Cinder King, after the Conclave attacked Tal'Dorei and [[Draconia]] in 811 PD.
- Defeated Vecna, the Whispered One, after his ascension to godhood in 812 PD. See [[Gods of Exandria]].

## Whitestone

- Percival and Vex'ahlia rule Whitestone, and Vex'ahlia sits on the Tal'Dorei Council.
- Members of the company are sometimes seen at the [[Tipsy Quorum]], where Scanlan once sang.

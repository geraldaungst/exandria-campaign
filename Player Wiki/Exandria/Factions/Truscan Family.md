The Truscan family is a wealthy family that has made [[Deastok]] its home for generations. It is the real power in the city.

## Influence

- Deastok's Crown-appointed starosta and the city's other titled officials were appointed at the behest of Prime Arbiter Sydnock Truscan, who resides in [[Rexxentrum]].
- It is widely assumed that anyone holding office in Deastok is related to the Truscans by blood or marriage, or deeply in their debt.
- The family's influence reaches [[Kamordah - DM Notes]], whose starosta is closely tied to it.

## Seat and Lands

- The family's seat is the Truscan Estate, a large estate behind tall walls at the southwestern edge of Deastok, near the forest.
- The family owns much of the farmland around Deastok.
- All visitors to Truscan lands must first check in with Aldous Penwrye at the estate.

The Malachite Cord is a fellowship devoted to the Aurora's Ascendant, a figure of legend from the [[Savalirwood]]. Its members describe themselves as guardians of balance.

## Aims

- The Cord works against the corruption spreading through the Savalirwood, which it regards as a warning of greater disturbances across Exandria. Its members say the [[Planar Rifts|rifts]] may be connected to the corruption.
- It opposes reassembling the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]], fearing a catastrophe like the destruction of [[Ruins of Molaesmyr|Molaesmyr]].
- It has undertaken to rededicate the [[Shrine of Melora]] to the Aurora's Ascendant.

## Organization

- The Cord is led by [[Rinneth Starsong]].
- Its members work in separate groups, each pursuing its own mission.
- Members include druids and come from many peoples.
- Many members carry a wooden symbol on a green cord.
- The Cord's teachings are gathered in a book called the Verdant Codex.

## Work on the Rifts

- The Cord uses Riftcages, soulwood devices that draw energy from a rift and shrink it.
- Its members say there are too few Riftcages to contain the rifts spreading across Exandria.
- The Cord seeks soulwood, a rare wood.

## The Aurora's Ascendant

The Cord tells the following legend:

- The Ascendant was born in the Savalirwood on a night when the aurora filled the sky.
- She was raised by mortals and guarded by the fey.
- As corruption crept into the land, she stood against it, and the forest rallied to her.
- Finding the darkness deeper than the land itself, she rose into the heavens in search of the wisdom to heal it, and did not return.

The Cord reveres her as the Chosen of Melora and holds that her spirit remains present in the world.

## Relations with the Obsidian Echoforge

According to the Cord's leader, the Cord and the [[Obsidian Echoforge]] once stood on common ground. They have since divided: the Echoforge seeks mastery over the rifts, while the Cord seeks harmony with them. The two groups still confer when the rifts require it.

The Kryn Dynasty is a nation that rules the northern part of [[Xhorhas]]. Its founder and ruler is [[Leylas Kryn]], the Bright Queen, and its capital is [[Rosohna]].

## Overview

- The Dynasty is led by drow, who are its most numerous people. It has brought goblins, hobgoblins, bugbears, orcs, and others of Xhorhas within its borders.
- The Dynasty revealed itself to the wider world only within the last century.
- Its ruling class is organized into noble houses called Dens, seated in Rosohna under the Bright Queen.
- Its armed forces are called the Aurora Watch.

## Relations with the Empire

The [[Dwendalian Empire]] has long been uneasy about Dynasty forces gathering in Xhorhas. Hostility between the two nations has turned into open war, fought along the Empire's eastern frontier.

## History

Leylas Kryn led the uprising that founded the Dynasty. Its people named their new capital Rosohna, meaning "rebirth."

The Cerberus Assembly is a council of the most powerful mages in the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. It advises the Crown. Most of its members live in [[Rexxentrum]], and others are found throughout the Empire.

## Organization

Eight chief mages, the archmages, lead the Assembly.

## History

- The Assembly's name recalls a union of mages from the Age of Arcanum.
- In the Empire's past, a war between its mage houses nearly destroyed part of Rexxentrum. Under the ceasefire, the houses agreed to bind themselves to the Crown instead of facing imprisonment, and the Assembly was established as a power under the King's rule.
- The Assembly helped the Empire conquer the Julous Dominion.

## Influence

- The Assembly's influence reaches into nearly every part of Imperial life.
- Its supporters say it keeps the Crown in check and prevents abuses of power. Many outside the Assembly are wary of its growing power.
- Imperial law triples the penalty for crimes against officials of the Assembly.
- In [[Zadash]], the Assembly manages the city's light rail and decides who may ride it.

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Artificer and scholar of the Obsidian Echoforge
> **Found in:** [[Port Damali]]

## Description

Aethor Kalisk is an elderly gnome artificer. His face is soft and rounded, lined from long concentration, with bright blue eyes and deep laugh lines. He wears a finely woven, well-aged cloak over an intricately designed tunic, and a silver ring engraved with arcane symbols. He carries a satchel of scrolls and tomes.

## Role

- A member of the [[Obsidian Echoforge]] and one of its key researchers.
- He studies the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]] and the search for its scattered shards.

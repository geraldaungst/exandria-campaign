## Description

Bertrand Dwendal is the King of the [[Dwendalian Empire]]. He rules from the capital, Rexxentrum.

## Role

- His law is absolute throughout the Empire.
- The [[Cerberus Assembly]] advises him.
- The Crown appoints the starostas who lead the Empire's towns.
- He enforces the Empire's ban on necromancy.

## History

He belongs to the Dwendal line, which has ruled since Manfried Dwendal became the Empire's first Emperor in 539 PD. Manfried later took the title of King, and his successors have used it since.

## Description

A tall half-elf with long brown hair and hazel eyes, and an accomplished ranger. Her companion is Trinket, a large brown bear that wears armor.

## Role

- Lady of Whitestone, married to Lord [[Percival de Rolo]].
- Sits on the Tal'Dorei Council.
- A member of the adventuring company [[Vox Machina]].

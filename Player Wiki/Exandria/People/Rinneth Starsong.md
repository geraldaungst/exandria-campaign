> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Leader of the [[Malachite Cord]]

## Description

Rinneth Starsong is an older half-elf woman with a gentle manner. She has untamed auburn curls, a tanned and weathered face, hazel eyes of slightly different sizes, a crooked nose, and a crooked, warm smile. She dresses for travel in worn leather and olive-green canvas over plain linen.

## Role

- She leads the Malachite Cord, whose members work in separate groups, each pursuing its own mission.
- She oversees the Cord's work to cleanse the [[Shrine of Melora]] and rededicate it to the Aurora's Ascendant.

## Description

The Gentleman is the name by which the head of the [[Myriad]]'s operations in [[Zadash]] is known.

## Reputation

What is said about the Gentleman comes from rumor and storytellers' tales.

- Some tales describe him as a water genasi.
- His headquarters is said to lie beneath the Evening Nip, a tavern in Zadash.
- According to the stories, his agents infiltrated tax offices, the courts, and the lower ranks of the Righteous Brand. They bribed clerks, replaced official documents with forgeries, and poisoned officials who could not be bought, slowly enough that each death seemed natural.
- Some claim that for a time the Myriad held half of Zadash's real power.
- The same tales say a lieutenant's attempt to assassinate a starosta was discovered. The Crownsguard captured many Myriad members in the crackdown that followed, but the Gentleman escaped into the tunnels beneath the city.
- Some say his methods have grown more brutal since then. Others whisper that his eyes sometimes glow, or that he speaks to empty corners as if someone stands there.

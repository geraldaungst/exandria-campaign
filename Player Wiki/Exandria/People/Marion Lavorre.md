## Description

Marion Lavorre is a tiefling with ruby-red skin and dark red hair, which give her the title by which most people know her: the Ruby of the Sea. She is graceful and charming.

## Role

- One of the most celebrated courtesans in Nicodranas.
- She lives and performs at the Lavish Chateau in Nicodranas.
- Her clients have included powerful figures from Nicodranas and the Dwendalian Empire.

## Reputation

Those who know her credit her appeal as much to her wisdom, intelligence, and close attention to people as to her beauty.

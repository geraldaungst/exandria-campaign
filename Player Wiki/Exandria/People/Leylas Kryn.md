## Description

Leylas Kryn is a drow, the founder and ruler of the [[Kryn Dynasty]], who holds the title of Bright Queen. Little is known of her outside the Dynasty.

## Role

- She rules the Dynasty from Rosohna.
- The noble Dens of Rosohna answer to her.

## History

She led the uprising that founded the Kryn Dynasty and has ruled it for centuries.

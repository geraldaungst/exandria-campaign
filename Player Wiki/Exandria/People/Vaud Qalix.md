## Description

Vaud Qalix is a dragonborn official of [[Xarzith Kitril]].

## Role

- He holds the office of Eye of the Scars, Protector of the Council. He is responsible for protecting the Scars of Scale and Tooth, the council that governs Xarzith Kitril.
- He leads the Wardens of the Ravine.

## Rifts and the Lorestone

- He commissions research into planar rifts and the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]], and he is trying to acquire shards of that relic.
- He hired [[Lady Emer]] to run a secure, isolated research facility at [[Cloudfang Keep]] and funded her work. He gave her two assignments:
  - to monitor rift sites he chose;
  - and to study the Lorestone fragments.
- He works through intermediaries and through letters signed "V.Q." Those who work for him may never meet him. His agent [[Dreyara Drimvar]] carried specimens and reports between him and Lady Emer.
- He demands secrecy from those he employs.
- He had a man captured and held at Cloudfang Keep for interrogation about knowledge Qalix wanted.

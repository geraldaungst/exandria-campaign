> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Marquis of Port Damali
> **Found in:** [[Port Damali]]

## Role

- Leader of Port Damali, and the fifth of the Lapidus family to hold the office. She governs with the city's guildmasters and nobles.
- Works with the other leaders of the Menagerie Coast to keep the Clovis Concord an independent nation, free of control by the Dwendalian Empire.
- Her office is at the northern end of the Larboard Light.

## Reputation

A celebrated figure in Port Damali. It is said that money can buy almost anything in the city, including the marquis's attention.

## History

- The Lapidus family rose to prominence more than a century ago, when it turned from employing merchants to fielding mercenaries. Over time, those who wanted safety at sea came to hire Lapidus privateers.
- While she was occupied with the rise of the Revelry, a pirate fleet, the [[Myriad]] took control of many of the guilds she was meant to oversee. She has struggled to remove its influence from the city ever since.

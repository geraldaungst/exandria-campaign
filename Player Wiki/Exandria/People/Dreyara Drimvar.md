> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Agent of [[Vaud Qalix]]

## Description

Dreyara Drimvar was a woman in her early thirties, with fair skin, dark brown hair worn in a practical braid, and a faint scar under one eye. She dressed in a high-collared dark green tunic, leather trousers, and knee-high boots, and wore a dagger strapped openly to her arm.

## Role

- She was the primary operative and enforcer for Vaud Qalix.
- She was Qalix's go-between with [[Lady Emer]]. She delivered specimens and prisoners to Lady Emer at [[Cloudfang Keep]] and collected her reports. Lady Emer feared her.

## Reputation

It is said that she once served the Volstrucker, who are rumored to be the Dwendalian Empire's secret police.

## The Dagger

She carried a dagger that Qalix had given her as a personal gift. She did not know it was the [[Blade of Maroth Fenn]].

## Death

Dreyara confronted the adventurers known as The Third Act outside the rear entrance of the [[Monastery of Dimensional Harmony]]. They defeated her there, and she died.

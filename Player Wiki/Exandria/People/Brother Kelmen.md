## Description

Brother Kelmen was a firbolg monk of the Monastery of Dimensional Harmony. After the monastery's collapse, his spirit remained in its memorial chamber. His spirit now resides in [[Solvei's Vigilance]], the longbow that lay before the tomb of the founder Solvei.

## History

- When the floor above the monastery's lower chamber collapsed, a ritual Kelmen was part of was left unfinished. The lower chamber was corrupted, and his spirit remained in the memorial chamber, unable to complete the ritual.
- The ritual required living witnesses and a correctly calibrated memorial chamber. Calibration meant aligning the four soulwood disks in the chamber's floor. The monks could sense planar flow, but not measure it precisely. They needed a fixed point, something that held still within the planar current and would resonate when the alignment was correct, and they never found one.
- The ritual was eventually completed. Kelmen's spirit descended into the lower chamber and passed into the knot of darkness at its center. His spirit then came to reside in Solvei's longbow.

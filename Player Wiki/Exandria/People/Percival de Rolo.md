> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Lord of Whitestone

## Description

A tall human with a shock of pure white hair and wire-rimmed glasses.

## Role

- Lord of Whitestone, of the de Rolo family that rules the city.
- Married to Lady [[Vex'ahlia de Rolo]].
- A member of the adventuring company [[Vox Machina]].

## Reputation

Known as a maker of firearms.

## History

The Briarwoods seized Whitestone and killed most of the de Rolo family. Percival survived, and with Vox Machina he retook the city.

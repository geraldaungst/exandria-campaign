> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Proprietor and cheesemaker
> **Found in:** [[Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip]]

## Description

A goliath, short for his kind, who runs [[Gnurlsotten's Nosh & Nip]] in the [[Cyrios Mountains]] and makes [[Cave-Aged Truscan]].

- Stout, broad-shouldered, and thick-armed. Standing, he is barely taller than a stout human.
- Entirely hairless, with no beard or eyebrows. His skin is pale grey-blue, mottled with darker patches like weathered stone.
- Travelers often take him for a dwarf at first glance.
- Wears worn, practical clothes and a cheesemaker's apron.

## Reputation

- Blunt and gruff in manner.
- Keeps the goat herd that supplies the cheesery, and is protective of the animals.

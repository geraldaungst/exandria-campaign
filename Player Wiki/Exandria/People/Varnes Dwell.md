> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Security consultant
> **Found in:** [[Port Damali]]

## Description

Varnes Dwell is a human man in his early forties, about six feet tall, with a weathered, well-kept look and an upright, disciplined bearing. His dark brown hair is cut short and shows gray at the temples. His eyes are sharp and gray. He wears practical, well-made travel clothing and carries a leather portfolio of security diagrams, architectural sketches, and coded notes.

## Role

- He works as a security consultant based in [[Port Damali]].

## History

- Agents working for [[Dreyara Drimvar]] captured Varnes and delivered him to [[Cloudfang Keep]].
- [[Lady Emer]] petrified him there.
- The Third Act adventuring party freed him.
- After his release he returned to Port Damali.

## Family

- His sister is [[Ilya the Sorceress]], who owns [[Ilya's Realm Shop]].

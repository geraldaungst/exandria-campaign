> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Role:** Scholar of planar rifts
> **Found in:** [[Cloudfang Keep]]

## Description

Lady Emer is a medusa. Living snakes grow from her head in place of hair, and her gaze can turn creatures to stone. Her skin is gray-tinged, with scale patterns along her neck and shoulders, and her eyes are amber. She dresses in dark scholar's robes.

## Role

- She studies planar rifts. She has traveled to rift sites across Wildemount, and she keeps a library of her research at [[Cloudfang Keep]].
- She lives at the keep and guards it with trained owlbears and griffons.

## Reputation

- She turns thieves, trespassers, and assistants who fail her to stone. She keeps them as statues in the keep's stone garden.
- She can reverse her own petrification.

## Work for Vaud Qalix

- [[Vaud Qalix]] hired her to run a secure research facility. He funded her studies of the rifts and of the [[Lorestone of Eryndor]].
- She has never met Qalix in person. Their contact came through his letters and through his agent [[Dreyara Drimvar]], who delivered specimens to her and collected her reports.

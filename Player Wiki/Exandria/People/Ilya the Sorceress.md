## Overview

Ilya is a retired adventurer who traveled Exandria before settling in [[Port Damali]]. She owns [[Ilya's Realm Shop]], a high-end magic shop.

## The Shop

- Stocks magic items from her own adventuring days, along with a broad range of common and uncommon magic items.
- Her stock and prices suit wealthy buyers more than everyday customers.
- Takes custom commissions, made to order.

## Family

- Her brother is [[Varnes Dwell]]. He was once held at [[Cloudfang Keep]].

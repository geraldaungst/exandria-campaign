## Influence action

A player urges a monster (any NPC) to do something. Decide which of three it is before anyone rolls.

| Monster is | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Willing** | Does it. No check. |
| **Unwilling** | Won't, whatever the roll. No check. |
| **Hesitant** | Ability check. |

- **Check:** Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion) or Wisdom (Animal Handling).
- **DC:** 15, or the monster's Intelligence score if that is higher.
- **On a failure:** the same urging can't be tried again for 24 hours, or for a duration you set.
- **Body language:** the Search action (Wisdom: Insight, Medicine, Perception, or Survival) reads the creature. It can't be combined with Influence in the same action.

## Attitude

- **Friendly:** predisposed to help. Advantage on the check.
- **Indifferent:** the default. No desire to help or hinder.
- **Hostile:** inclined to hinder. Disadvantage on the check.

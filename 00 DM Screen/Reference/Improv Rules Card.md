| Size | Fragile HP | Resilient HP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny (bottle, lock) | 2 (1d4) | 5 (2d4) |
| Small (chest, lute) | 3 (1d6) | 10 (3d6) |
| Medium (barrel, chandelier) | 4 (1d8) | 18 (4d8) |
| Large (cart, dining table) | 5 (1d10) | 27 (5d10) |

- Huge or Gargantuan objects: split into Large or smaller sections and track each section's HP.
- Objects are immune to Poison and Psychic damage. Fragile objects can be broken automatically with Attack or Utilize.
- **Damage threshold** (big objects such as walls): the object takes no damage from a single hit below the threshold, and takes all of it at or above.
- Objects have no ability scores: they fail every saving throw and can't make ability checks.

## Improvised weapons

- 1d4 damage, type chosen by you. No Proficiency Bonus on the attack.
- Thrown: range 20/60 ft.
- If it resembles a Simple or Martial weapon, use that weapon (a table leg is a Club).

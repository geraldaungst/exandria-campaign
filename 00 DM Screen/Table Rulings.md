## Table Rulings

Standing adjudications for this campaign. Once a ruling is here, it applies consistently—don't relitigate at the table.

### Extradimensional Spaces

**Principle:** An object inside an extradimensional space (bag of holding, portable hole, Heward's handy haversack) is not present on the Material Plane. It cannot be perceived from outside, cannot be interacted with, and neither affects nor is affected by anything in the surrounding environment. The space's opening is a window, not a threshold. Opening the bag does not bring the contents into this plane. Only physically removing an object—by reaching in and taking it, or by inverting the container and dumping everything out—makes it present.

**Follows from this:**
- A magic item's ongoing or passive effects do not function while stowed.
- Nothing inside can be detected, scried, or targeted from outside.
- Stowed objects are unaffected by area effects, environmental conditions, or
  planar phenomena occurring around the bearer.
- A creature inside perceives nothing of the outside.

**Why this matters:** Without a fixed rule here, stowed magic items become a free detector array or a way to shelter objects from consequences. Neither is intended.

### Planar Detection Range

Applies to [[Lorestone of Eryndor - DM|Lorestone]] shards and any comparable detection property.

**Principle:** Detection radius scales with the strength of the planar disturbance. A listed radius is the *maximum*, for a fully open, active rift. Weaker disturbances register proportionally closer, and intensity increases with proximity within that range.

| Disturbance                         | Range                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Active, open rift                   | Full listed radius            |
| Recently closed or collapsed rift   | A few hundred feet            |
| Dormant thinning (e.g. [[Deastok]]) | Not detected; it isn't a rift |
| Fainter than that                   | No detection                  |

Within range, detection conveys what the shard's item block describes: direction, which plane the rift connects to (if any), and its approximate size and stability. Intensity rises as the bearer closes in.

**Why this matters:** Keeps shards from functioning as universal planar detectors. Most planar oddity in the world sits below the floor.

### Spells on Rifts

**Principle:** *Dispel Magic* can temporarily stabilize a small rift, and *Banishment* aimed at rift energy backfires, as [[Lady Emer's Research Notes|Lady Emer]] observed. Rifts are chaotic, so the size of the effect varies from rift to rift: how long a dispel holds, how much larger the rift reopens, how violent the backlash.

**Why this matters:** The players have Emer's notes and may try this. Her observations hold; they just aren't a reliable formula.

### Handling the Luxon Beacon

**Principle:** Extended study or handling of the [[luxon-beacon-egw|Luxon Beacon]] produces the effects Lady Emer recorded: whispers, a sense of slipping in time, and dreams of other lives. The effects build with extended contact and linger for hours. Brief handling does nothing, and while stowed in an extradimensional space it has no effect (see Extradimensional Spaces).

**Why this matters:** The players have read her account and will expect it.

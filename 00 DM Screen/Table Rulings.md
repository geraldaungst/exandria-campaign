## Table Rulings

Standing adjudications for this campaign. Once a ruling is here, it applies

consistently—don't relitigate at the table.

### Extradimensional Spaces

**Principle:** An object inside an extradimensional space (bag of holding,

portable hole, Heward's handy haversack) is not present on the Material Plane.

It cannot be perceived from outside, cannot be interacted with, and neither

affects nor is affected by anything in the surrounding environment.

The space's opening is a window, not a threshold. Opening the bag does not

bring the contents into this plane. Only physically removing an object—by

reaching in and taking it, or by inverting the container and dumping everything

out—makes it present.

**Follows from this:**

- A magic item's ongoing or passive effects do not function while stowed.
- Nothing inside can be detected, scried, or targeted from outside.
- Stowed objects are unaffected by area effects, environmental conditions, or
  planar phenomena occurring around the bearer.
- A creature inside perceives nothing of the outside.

**Why this matters:** Without a fixed rule here, stowed magic items become a

free detector array or a way to shelter objects from consequences. Neither is

intended.

### Planar Detection Range

Applies to [[Lorestone of Eryndor|Lorestone]] shards and any comparable

detection property.

**Principle:** Detection radius scales with the strength of the planar

disturbance. A listed radius is the *maximum*, for a fully open, active rift.

Weaker disturbances register proportionally closer, and intensity increases

with proximity within that range.

| Disturbance | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Active, open rift | Full listed radius |
| Recently closed or collapsed rift | A few hundred feet |
| Dormant thinning (e.g. [[Deastok]]) | Tens of feet |
| Fainter than that | No detection |

Detection conveys **presence and intensity, not direction.** The bearer knows

something is here and whether it is stronger or weaker than a moment ago—

locating a source requires moving around and comparing.

**Why this matters:** Keeps shards from functioning as universal planar

detectors. Most planar oddity in the world sits below the floor.

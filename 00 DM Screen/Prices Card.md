Source: 2024 Basic Rules, [Magic Items](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items) (Magic Item Rarities and Values). Checked 2026-10-20.

## Magic item value by rarity

| Rarity | Value | Crafting cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 100 GP | 50 GP |
| Uncommon | 400 GP | 200 GP |
| Rare | 4,000 GP | 2,000 GP |
| Very rare | 40,000 GP | 20,000 GP |
| Legendary | 200,000 GP | 100,000 GP |
| Artifact | priceless | not craftable |

- **Consumables** (other than a Spell Scroll): half the value and half the crafting cost.
- **Spell Scroll:** worth double what it costs to scribe.
- **Item built from a purchasable item** (a magic sword, plate armor): add the base item's price from Equipment.
- Buying and selling magic items, treasure, and trade goods: see the links below.

## Mundane prices

- Gear and weapons: [2024 Basic Rules, Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment)
- [Lifestyle Expenses](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#LifestyleExpenses) and [Food, Drink, and Lodging](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#FoodDrinkandLodging) (2024 PHB, needs your D&D Beyond login)
- No book has a services price table. Set the price from the nearest comparable.

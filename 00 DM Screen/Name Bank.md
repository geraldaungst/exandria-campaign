**Purpose:** Spare names for improvised NPCs, grouped by where the NPC is from. Draft list: the cultural inspirations are my choice, not Exandrian canon, so change, add, or delete freely. Cross a name out (`~~Name~~`) once it is used so it doesn't turn up twice.

Pick by pointing at the list without looking, or roll a die and count. Mix a given name from one row with a family name from another to avoid obvious repeats.

## Dwendalian Empire (Truscan Vale, Zadash, Rexxentrum)

Low Countries and Rhineland flavor. Family names are mostly trades and landmarks.

- **Given:** Ansgar, Mechthild, Joost, Griet, Rutger, Femke, Lenaerd, Odilia, Sander, Tilda, Corneel, Wilhelmina, Hendrik, Aleid, Roelof, Gerlinde
- **Family:** Kesselman (kettle-maker), Brouwer (brewer), Holtkamp (wood field), Meerbusch (lake bush), Waldvogel (forest bird), Tuchscherer (cloth-cutter), Vandersloot, Dijkstra

## Xhorhas

Caucasus flavor: Georgian, Armenian, and Chechen names, chosen to avoid the usual steppe-raider shorthand. Families go by a place or an old deed, not a surname.

- **Given:** Nino, Tamaz, Mzia, Levan, Tsira, Zurab, Ketevan, Nodar, Maia, Vakhtang, Anahit, Arsen, Lusine, Vazgen, Khedi, Ruslan
- **By name of:** the Ash Road, the Split Tower, the Red Well, the Ninth Fire, the Quiet Pass, the Salt Kettle

## Menagerie Coast (Port Damali, Port Zoon)

Harbor towns: Swahili coast, Portuguese, Greek, and Tamil names side by side.

- **Given:** Zuberi, Neema, Halima, Idris, Tiago, Inês, Dimitris, Eleni, Anbu, Kavya, Makena, Rui, Aliki, Selvam, Amani, Joaquim
- **Family:** Karanja, Vasilakis, Oliveira, Pereira, Ndiaye, Arumugam, da Maré (of the tide), Mwakilema

## Tal'Dorei (Emon, Whitestone)

Occitan, Catalan, and Polish names. Some families carry a town name, some a craft.

- **Given:** Jordi, Montserrat, Bartek, Zofia, Aurèle, Ysabeau, Casimir, Wiktoria, Gaspard, Núria, Tadeusz, Elionor, Lluc, Halina, Mathieu, Berenguer
- **Family:** Ferrer (smith), Puig (hill), Delmas, Kowalczyk, Rouquier, Sadowski, Vidal, Czerwiński
